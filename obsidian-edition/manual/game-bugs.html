The game's bugs, fixed

Part of Obsidian Edition's documentation, from Features and rules. Spoilers: some of these name places and people from later in the game, most of them under People and quests.

The patched game mends these whatever the Options tab says. The rules the game got wrong, each with a switch, are further down. The script bugs are mended in the Loader's copy of the game's scripts; many were found by yuan-ti and the OpenDS project (credits). How the fixes work: DEVELOPMENT-CONTENT.md (the script bugs, the spells, worn items), the memory fixes in DEVELOPMENT.md, and the rows' own links.

Wrong numbers

The gameWith the Loader
Cause Serious and Critical Wounds2d9 and 3d9 (the Cure spells heal AD&D's 2d8+1 and 3d8+3)2d8+1 and 3d8+3
Slay Living's descriptionSaid a creature that saves takes 3 to 17Says 2 to 16 (half of 4d8), what the game does

The gameWith the Loader

Making and keeping characters

The gameWith the Loader
DELETE in the rosterDeleted the character in the row clicked counting from the top of the list, not from the top of what was shown, so with the list scrolled down another character was deletedDeletes the one chosen (how)
The creation screen's least scoresThe class's prime requisite raised to 17 and every other score to 9 to 14, against the manual: a fighter's STR never below 17, a thief's CHA never below 9, while a druid's CHA, a ranger's STR and DEX and a psionicist's CON had no minimum of their ownThe manual's: cleric WIS 9; druid WIS 12, CHA 15; fighter STR 9; gladiator STR 13, DEX 12, CON 15; preserver INT 9; psionicist CON 11, INT 12, WIS 15; ranger STR 13, DEX 13, CON 14, WIS 14; thief DEX 9; a multiclass character the highest of their classes' (character creation)
XP for high prime requisitesNone, though the manual promises 10% more XP to a class whose prime requisites are all 16 or more10% more of every award for each such class, divided by a multiclass character's classes (one XP for them all); a human's class followed now. The Loader adds it as each award comes, so it needs the Loader following the game (Experience and levels)

Rules it got wrong, with a switch

Most of the rule changes put right a rule the game gets wrong: it departs from its own manual, from itself, or from the Player's Handbook it follows. Each has a switch on the Options tab, on by default, so the game's own rule is still there to go back to. (The rule changes that are the mod's own choices, not corrections, are left out: helms' AC, resting, the XP level cap, hit dice, creation scores, half-giants' weapons, creature size, weapon traits (cahulaks, stinkpots, polearm reach) and ages.)

Two more, with a switch

Two of the game's bugs are mended by a switch of their own: Cilla's figure (the game gives the elf the half-elf woman's; see Elves and half-elves), and a click on a spell's icon on the Effects screen ending it (Spells on the Effects screen).

Saved games

There's no need to start a new game for these: they're in the patched game, not in a save, so they work from the moment a game is loaded. What a bug already did stays in the save: your saved games says what.
