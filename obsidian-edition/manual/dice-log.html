The dice log, line by line

Part of Obsidian Edition's documentation, from the README: every kind of line the Loader's dice log writes, and what it means. How to read and search the log is in the README.

The lines below are the log as it is written: what Save... writes, and what the window shows with Readable layout unticked. With it ticked (the default) the window lays the same lines out outcome first, the roll and HP in columns, the working dimmed under each (the readable layout).

Attack rolls, in more detail. hits on anything but a 1 or only a 20 hits replace needs 12+ when the roll is out of the ordinary range. X attacks Y from behind ... and X attacks Y BACKSTAB ... mark attacks from behind and backstabs, and X attacks Y unseen ... a hidden archer's shot (see Attacks from behind and backstabs). A thri-kreen of the party with nothing in hand attacks with a claw (1d4), four times a round, then with its bite (1d4+1), whose damage line shows its +1 bite; with weapons in hand, it attacks with them and then still bites. The claw's dice are the ones it has now (Erdlu Claw's 1d6+1, Animal Affinity's 1d10, Body Weaponry's 2d4). Anyone else unarmed, and a monster's own attacks, have no with. The target's AC includes armour, DEX and spells. A natural 20 always hits and a natural 1 always misses, but a 20 does no extra damage: the game has no critical hits (see No critical hits).

Kits in the lines. A kit shows where it changes a number, by name: an attack's THAC0 line has from behind (Rescuer) for the Rescuer's +1, the initiative's parts Wayfinder's Path-finder (or Wayfinder down), a healing spell's +1 Healer or a Lifebinder's + 1d8 Lifebinder (the helper's roll), and a Wilderness Avenger's +1 Wilderness Avenger on a Cause spell's damage (Kits).

Saving throws, in more detail. The modifiers are those of the game's saving throws. With the rule changes off, the game uses petrification/polymorph for almost every spell and doubles the d20 against fire, cold and electricity spells, as in the example above, so Fireball's victims usually save; with them on (the default), the line names the spell save and DEX's adjustment instead (+5 DEX 21 dodging). A natural 1 always fails and a natural 20 saves, unless no save is allowed: none allowed (cannot save) -> failed (cannot save, even on a natural 20 when the d20 was a 20) is a save the game gives −100 (Chaos for all but warriors, Cloudkill, Scare below 6th level, Color Spray with the manual's spells): it fails (the game's own compare read the total as a big number and the creature saved; the patched game's compare is signed, a bug fixed whatever the Options). With Spells and psionics as the manual describes them the line also counts +2 Fire Shield against fire and cold and +1 Blur. For a damaging spell the result says what the save left: saved: half damage, 19 of 38, failed: full damage, 38, or saved: no damage for spells such as Chill Touch (saved: no damage (not 14) with the damage rolled); a creature that saves without a roll has not needed (saves automatically) -> saved; the HP line after it shows what the creature really lost, once resistances and protections have had their say. A failed save also lets the spell's effect take hold. Spells left on the ground (Grease, clouds) make creatures save again as they stay in them; those lines have no "from".
