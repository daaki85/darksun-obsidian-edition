Dark Sun: Shattered Lands · Obsidian Edition

Changes from the Original Game

A Dark Sun: Shattered Lands mod · Current version 2.16.1 · What the mod adds and changes, and its main bug fixes

These notes follow the repository, so they can include changes made since the current version that are not yet in a release; the changelog says which pull request brought each one.

Obsidian Edition is a free mod for the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands. It shows the rolls the game keeps hidden, brings its rules closer to AD&D 2nd Edition, adds kits, weapons, magic items and people to Athas, dresses the party in what they wear, and fixes bugs left over since 1993. You start the game with the Loader, which loads the mod with your options and logs every roll as it happens. Nearly everything is switchable, the game's own files are never touched, and your saves stay where they are.

At a Glance

  • Every hidden roll shown. The Loader's dice log writes each attack, saving throw, check and creation roll as it happens, with the number it needed and where each bonus came from. A combat log in the game (L) shows the rolls on the map.
  • Rules closer to AD&D 2nd Edition. Saving throws, weapon specialization, two weapons, thief skills, class restrictions and the class tables as the books have them; spells, psionics and monsters as the manual describes them.
  • More to choose and find. Three kits for each class (every kit), twenty-two new magic weapons and more new magic and plain items (every item, and where it is), and new people and an arena bout in the game's first areas.
  • The party as it is dressed. The figures show the gear they wear, magic gear glows on them, and elves have pointed ears.
  • Quicker play. Keys to choose a target, loot a body and sell plain gear, 40 save slots, a faster default speed, and no copy protection.
  • Bugs fixed. The main fixes are under Bug Fixes; every one, with what the game did, is in the game's bugs, fixed.
  • Your choice. Nearly every change has a switch in the Loader's Options. The game's own files are never touched, and saves made with the original game load (see Your Saved Games).

The full catalogue follows, by part of the game.

In the Game's Screens

  • NewA combat log in the game: L on the map shows every roll, in small lettering, 8 pixels a line (7 lines at a time) and the Loader's colours, at one of three levels of detail, and L again hides it. It works in a fight or after one (a thief's roll, a new level's hit points). It is a panel over the map's lower right that scrolls back (its scrollbar, or the mouse wheel on Windows), shrinks to one line or opens up with filters (All, Party, Damage, Saves).
  • NewNames over figures: a spell's name floats over its caster as it is cast, "Miss" over anyone an attack misses, and "Armor: 7 left" when a spell that lasts a number of uses (Armor, Mirror Image, Reflected Image...) loses one, in the game's lettering (an Options switch, on).
  • NewOut of a fight, the combat log panel's top line shows how long the game has run, Day 47, 14:20; in the arena, once the game has shown the count, how many arena fights the party has had instead (the game's own count, one a trip into the arena).
  • NewThe inventory screen shows THAC0, all five saving throws, the THAC0 with each weapon, DEX's reaction and defence adjustments, a thief's skills and, with ages on, the character's age, in the game's own lettering.
  • NewThe creation screen shows the THAC0, right of the hit points: THAC0: M18 R19, melee (with STR's to-hit) and ranged (with DEX's missile adjustment), the kit counted, changing as you click STR and DEX or choose a kit.
  • NewWith ages on, the creation screen shows the age a new character will start at, above RECALL: Table 7's dice, rolled again with each roll of the die (STORE and RECALL keep it with the roll).
  • NewView Character shows THAC0 and saves, and for a character of several classes, which class levels up next.
  • NewThe USE screen shows the spell slots left at the spell level and list on show (wizard slots on the MAGE list, priest slots on the CLERIC list), and the most you get after resting.
  • NewIn a fight, the Look box shows a monster's hit points, AC, THAC0, alignment, magic resistance and chief defence (NEEDS +1 WEAPON, IMM FIRE COLD...). Close it to read the rest.
  • NewItem boxes show a wand's charges left, the bonus a cloak, boots or belt gives to thief skills, and a plain wooden, bone, stone or obsidian weapon's to-hit penalty (Hit -1 for bone, Hit -2 for obsidian).
  • NewThe Effects screen lists your kits and weapon specializations, and a Shinobi's chance to read languages; with the pointer on the icon of a spell that lasts a number of uses (Armor, Mirror Image, Reflected Image...), it says how many charges are left, for Enlarge how much more it gives (ENLARGED (+90% DICE), or DAMAGE with its rule off), and for Barkskin, Flesh Armor and Magical Vestments the armour AC they give (BARKSKIN (AC 3)).
  • NewReading a scroll on the inventory screen tells you what happened (YOU LEARN THE SPELL, YOU CAN NOT READ THE SCROLL, THAT IS NOT A SHINOBI SPELL...) on the line under the paper doll. The game showed nothing there.

Combat

Weapon specialization

  • NewFighters specialize in one weapon spec, a kind of weapon such as the long sword (+1 to hit, +2 damage, 3/2 attacks a round), with mastery at 5th level (+3, +3) and grand mastery at 9th (the damage die one size up against every target, as in Player's Option: Combat & Tactics: a long sword 1d10, and 1d20 against a large foe; and one more attack).
  • NewGladiators specialize in two weapon specs, a third at 6th level and a fourth at 9th. Rangers gain expertise with the bow and one other weapon spec. The Battle Mage, Crusader and Mind Warrior kits gain expertise with one melee weapon spec of their choice (3/2 attacks a round, 2 from 7th level).
  • FixedWarriors attack at AD&D's plain rate with weapons outside their specializations (was 3/2 with any weapon). Specialists shoot missiles faster.
  • ChangedEvery thri-kreen, foes too, throws a chatkcha once more a round, AD&D's 2/1 for its own weapon (was at the weapon's rate, like anyone); a thri-kreen Mind Warrior may take the chatkcha as its weapon spec.
  • NewThe weapons spells make count for specialization: the Flame Blade as a long sword, Shillelagh as a club, Spiritual Hammer as a mace.

Saving throws

  • FixedSpells are saved against with the spell save (was petrification/polymorph for almost every spell).
  • FixedAgainst fire, cold and electricity, DEX helps you dodge, from −5 to +6 (was a doubled d20). Needing 14 at DEX 12, the chance to save against a Fireball is 35% (was 70%).
Developers' NotesThe doubled d20 isn't in the manual or in AD&D. It made most enemies shrug off a Fireball. Nimble targets still dodge; slow ones now burn.

Two weapons

  • FixedTwo weapons cost −2 to hit with the main hand and −4 with the off hand, less with high DEX; rangers, Twin-blades and Battle Dancers fight with two at no penalty in light armour, a Justifier in any, and a Ghetto Fighter at 0 and −2 (was a small bonus at DEX 5 or less, otherwise nothing).
  • FixedThe off hand attacks once a round, and extra attacks belong to the main hand (was the full rate in each hand).

Held targets

  • FixedA blow on a held (Paralyzed) or Out Cold creature hits automatically and does normal damage, as in AD&D (was a roll to hit as ever, and double damage). It cuts both ways: a held party member is hit every time too.

Armour and protection

  • ChangedHelms give AC 1 (was 0).
  • ChangedRings and cloaks of protection follow AD&D: two rings don't stack, a ring gives no AC with magical armour, and a cloak does nothing with magical or metal armour or a shield.
  • FixedAcid and corroding touches let each item save by its material, as in AD&D (was any non-magical armour destroyed outright).

Races

  • ChangedHalf-giants can hold a two-handed weapon in one hand.

Size and weapons

  • NewCreature size: a large target (a half-giant, a large monster, anyone Enlarged) takes AD&D's damage for large targets from a weapon (a long sword 1d12, a great axe 2d8, a gythka 1d10; a dagger 1d3, a chatkcha 1d4+2), and Hold Person and Charm Person don't work on it at all. A weapon's item box shows both damages (1D8-1/1D12-1), and Shillelagh's damage reads 1D6+1 (2D4 against a large foe). (the game keeps no sizes)
  • ChangedThe pick is AD&D's footman's pick, 1d6+1 (2d4 against a large foe), and the axe the battle axe, 1d10 against a large foe, as Player's Option: Skills & Powers has them (the pick was the horseman's 1d4+1 (1d4); the axe 1d8 against any size). Deepbiter and the Metal Pick hit as the footman's pick too.
  • NewCahulaks fight as Dark Sun's rules describe them: a bladed head in each hand, so one more attack a round (at −2 and −4, as with two weapons, DEX helping), and a hit can tangle a man-sized or smaller foe, which then loses its next turn unless it saves against petrification. Half-giants, large monsters, anyone Enlarged, the bodiless (elementals, slimes, swarms, shadows) and a few other beasts can't be tangled (the creatures' table (spoilers) marks each). Twin-blades and Battle Dancers can't use cahulaks.
  • NewStaff sling stinkpots (AD&D's Arms and Equipment Guide; with the cahulaks' traits, one switch: Weapon traits): the staff sling throws clay pots of burning sulphur, 1d3 damage against any size, and a target hit saves against poison or is nauseated for 1d3+1 rounds, −2 on its attack rolls (NAUSEATED on the Effects screen). Undead, and anything immune to poison, are unaffected. (was a plain 1d4 shot)
  • NewPolearm reach (with Weapon traits): a polearm held with no other weapon in the hands (a bow in the missile slot is fine, and so is a shield in a half-giant's other hand, the only one who can hold a polearm in one) strikes a foe two squares away as well as an adjacent one, for the party and its foes alike. Two squares diagonally don't count. (a polearm struck only an adjacent foe)

Classes and Kits

All classes

  • ChangedLevels stop at a cap of 749,999 XP instead of 9th level, shared out: one class goes as far as its next level needs no more (cleric 11th, druid 12th, fighter, gladiator and ranger 10th, preserver and psionicist 11th, thief 13th); two or three classes share it evenly (374,999 or 249,999 each); a human who changed class has what its old classes' levels didn't use (was 9th level for every class).
  • ChangedCharacter creation keeps what the die rolls: lower a score to raise another, but the six never add up to more than the die gave (shown under CHR as SUM:99/101), and the hit points stay as rolled, moving only with CON's bonus (was any score clicked up to its most, and the hit points set by a click).
  • NewSTORE and RECALL on the character creation screen, as in Baldur's Gate: keep a roll, try the die again, and bring the kept roll back.
  • FixedAn air cleric can use daggers, as its sphere allows (was none: no dagger in the game listed the air cleric).
  • ChangedAn air cleric may also use cahulaks (Dark Sun's cahulaks are thrown weapons, as AD&D's daggers are) (was not: the class restrictions took them away).
  • NewNew characters start with fuller gear: a weapon in hand they can use, a sling, leather armour where they may wear it, a Stalker, Assassin or Shinobi a cloak, and some kits weapons of their own (a Battle Dancer two long swords, a Poisoner two of its weapon spec, daggers or short swords). A multiclass preserver starts with no armour, so it can cast.
  • FixedClass restrictions hold: a character may use an item only if every one of its classes allows it, and a multiclass preserver can't cast in armour (was any one class allowing it). Thieves wear light armour only; a multiclass thief (or a human once a warrior or cleric) may wear heavier, losing its thief skills and backstab in it.
  • FixedMulticlass characters gain hit points as in AD&D: CON's bonus on every hit die (a warrior's if one class is a warrior), the total divided between the classes.
  • ChangedEvery character's hit dice are rolled twice and the better kept, at character creation and at every level (a rule change, on unless unticked).
  • FixedExperience, priests' THAC0 and spell slots follow AD&D's tables: a gladiator needs 2,250 XP for 2nd level (was 2,000), a preserver has 4 2 1 slots at 5th (was 3 2 1), a cleric 3 3 1 (was 3 2 1).
  • FixedA ranger's spells last and deal damage at its level minus 7, as AD&D has it (was its full level).
  • NewAges (a rule change, on unless unticked): each character has an age from the Dark Sun rules' tables, shown on the inventory screen and the Loader's Characters tab. Middle, old and venerable age change the scores, and a character dies at the most its race lives; everyone is a year older each 365 days of game time (the dice log says so), and magic ages them faster. Haste now asks each creature it speeds for a system shock roll by CON, as in AD&D: one that fails dies, and a party member who survives is a year older. Restore Youth (a 6th-level priest spell: Magic and Psionics) takes magical years back.
  • FixedCON 20 and up gives a hit point back at AD&D's rate, one an hour at CON 20 to one every 10 minutes at 25 (was one every 5 minutes at any of them); and a dwarf's or halfling's poison save gets only its racial bonus, as in AD&D (was CON's adjustment as well) (a rule change, CON as in AD&D, on unless unticked).
  • FixedDual-classing as the manual has it: from 3rd level, with every prime requisite of the old class at 15 or more and every one of the new class at 17 or more (a rule change, on by default) (was 2nd level, and one score a class checked: a ranger, druid or psionicist on WIS alone).
  • FixedPreservers' INT sets their chance to learn a spell from a scroll (35% at INT 9 to 100% at 24) and the most spells of each level they can know. New preservers choose their starting spells.

A few of them:

  • Arcanist: two preserver spells per turn in a fight, for a d3 hit die.
  • Scholar: one more spell slot at each spell level, and one more spell learnt at each level, for 1 worse to hit.
  • Healer: a water cleric only, given the sphere of the cosmos: the spells only druids had, Cure Serious and Critical Wounds, Raise Dead and Neutralize Poison among them, each cure healing 1 more a die.
  • Assassin: a backstab one step deadlier (×3 at 1st level up to ×6 from 13th), for −15 to picking pockets and locks.
  • Wilderness Avenger: +1 to hit and damage against anything, and Cause Wounds spells doing 1 more a die, for no healing spells at all.
  • Rescuer: cures heal 1 more a die and +2 movement in a fight, for no shield and only +1 from behind.
  • Poisoner: every melee hit poisons (a save or 10 damage), for a dagger or short sword spec only.
  • Battle Mage: a warrior's THAC0, leather armour it can cast in, expertise with one weapon, and spells that still go off after being hit.
  • Shinobi: a thief trained by the Veiled Alliance who casts preserver spells from 6th level (4th-level ones, Improved Invisibility, Solid Fog and Confusion, at 13th), and learns its own from a scroll only if it can read it (its read languages).
  • Ravager: fights without a shield or missile weapons, with a natural AC that hardens from 7 to 3 as it levels.
  • Battle Dancer: dances as each fight starts, a roll against DEX at −4: +1 to hit, damage and AC for a few rounds, or −1 if it stumbles. Long swords and bows only, and no shield.
  • Military Scout: better at stealth, traps and climbing under the open sky, with +2 initiative and +1 to hit with bows and slings there, and its hiding isn't halved by the sun; worse under a roof, and it never backstabs.
  • Spellarcher: shoots every arrow as a magic one (1 better, at least +1), and weaves the touch spells it knows into arrows: a button on the USE screen for each, a spell slot for 5 arrows a preserver level in its empty quiver (or added to the same spell's arrows there), each casting its spell on what it hits at the archer's preserver level. They stay in the quiver until shot or the next rest, when any left fade, so a slot spent just before resting is lost. Making them plays the spell's own cast sound.
  • Wild Fury: Wild Fighting as each fight starts, a roll against CON at −4: one more attack a round in melee and +3 damage, or −3 to hit and AC 3 worse if it fails.
  • NewThe game's own party has features of its own: Gerakis fights wild (as a Wild Fury does), K'ratchek casts and uses psionics as a Wasteland Seer does, Cilla hides as well in the sun as out of it (her feature, the Elven Shadow-walker), and Cermak is an Arcanist.
Developers' NotesEach kit is built into the game's own code, so it works everywhere the game checks: THAC0, spell slots, hit dice, PSP costs, attacks a round and what you may hold. The player's guide has all 37, with a short description of who takes each one on Athas.

Magic and Psionics

  • FixedSpells and psionics as the manual describes them (a rule change, on by default, from the next start): saves against Bestow Curse and Blood Flow; Flaming Sphere negated by a save; no save against Quicksand; Solid Fog (1d3 rounds + 1 a level), Cause Fear (1d4 rounds), Minor Spell Turning (3 rounds a level) and Stinking Cloud (1d4+1 rounds) for the manual's time; Turn Pebble to Boulder 11-26; Minute Meteors five globes of 1d4; Flame Arrow a 5d6 bolt for every 5 caster levels; Ironskin a blow stopped a caster level; Chaos saved against by creatures of very high or very low INT; Color Spray saved against only by creatures of more levels than the caster, and Hold Undead only on undead of the caster's level or lower; Free Action frees creatures held by Web, Solid Fog, Quicksand, Entangle or Grease; Fire Shield +2 and Blur +1 on saves; Chill Touch weakens for an hour and sends undead fleeing; Cloudkill kills weak creatures; Feeblemind stops psionics too, and Heal cures it; Lower Resistance takes 30% plus 1% a level; Strength and Cat's Grace roll by class; Rainbow Pattern holds a round a level and breaks when its victim is hurt; Cell Adjustment heals 1d4; Biofeedback always leaves a blow at least 1. Turn Undead follows AD&D's table: turned undead flee for 3d4 rounds, the strongest results destroy, and a priest who fails can't try the same undead again that fight (was destroyed or nothing, as often as the priest liked). Monsters' hit dice count as their level for saving throws (was level 0 for a monster with no class).
  • FixedMonsters as the manual describes them (with the same rule change): Shadows and Greater Shadows drain Strength and shrug off cold, the Babau and Blue Slaad carry deadly poison, the Styr breathes fire (and only +1 weapons hurt it), the Djinn turns invisible and makes mirror images, and the Tari's bite can carry disease (was cold damage and paralysis, a corroding touch, disease, and nothing of the rest).
  • FixedSpells roll to hit (a rule change, on by default, from the next start): the missile spells (Acid Arrow, Turn Pebble to Boulder, Magical Stone, Condense, and each of Minute Meteors' globes, at +2) and the Strine's spines roll against the target's AC, as do the touch spells cast at an enemy (Chill Touch, Shocking Grasp, Vampiric Touch, the Cause Wounds spells, Cause Disease, Poison); a miss does nothing (was every one landing).
  • ChangedResting heals by the priests' remaining spells (and psionicists' Cell Adjustment) and a night's natural healing (3 HP and CON's bonus, plus CON 20+ regeneration) (a rule change, on by default) (was every member healed to full).
  • FixedEnlarge makes only the weapon's dice bigger, 10% a caster level, rounded up, with STR and magic added after, as in AD&D (a rule change, on unless unticked) (was the whole melee damage made bigger, STR included).

Improved Thieving

  • NewA soft breath as a thief or ranger hides in a fight, and a purse's jingle for a picked pocket (lifting one of the three named weapons plays the game's quest sound).
  • FixedThief skills follow AD&D's table by level, with Dark Sun's race and DEX adjustments, and none goes above 95 (was a 3rd-level elf with move silently 66 and hide in shadows 61).
  • NewThieves hide in shadows and move silently in a fight to backstab, halved in daylight. Rangers do it to attack from behind, at full strength under the open sky (was a backstab only on a target turned to face someone else).
  • FixedA backstab multiplies only the weapon's dice; STR and magic bonuses are added once after, as in AD&D, and only the round's first attack is a backstab (was the whole damage multiplied, STR included, so a strong thief could hit for over 100, on the first two attacks of the round).
  • NewPicking pockets with a thief's Pick Pockets button on the USE screen (where a priest's Turn Undead is), then a click on anyone in sight: any thief can try, leader or not, and what's lifted goes in that thief's backpack. A cleric/thief has both buttons, the purse beside Turn Undead. Fail and you roll move silently to get away. A purse holds 2 to 5 ceramic pieces for each of the thief's levels, and a few named weapons can be lifted for 200 XP (once a game each).
  • ChangedWith Thieves as in AD&D, a thief who fails a lock, or is caught at a pocket, may try that one again only after gaining a level, as Player's Option: Skills & Powers has it; a thief who slips away unnoticed may try again at once, and another thief may try at once (was again and again; caught, never).
  • ChangedA thief beside any square of a big creature counts as beside an enemy, and can't hide there (was its one square only).
  • NewA worn cloak adds 10 to hiding, boots 10 to moving silently, and a belt 5 to picking pockets and opening locks, for thieves and rangers alike; the inventory screen's thief skills count them.

Developers' NotesShattered Lands rolls dice for everything and shows you almost none of it. Fireball victims kept saving and nobody knew why (the game doubles the d20 against fire). The Loader started as a way to see those numbers. Once we could see them, we started fixing them.

Bug Fixes

The main fixes are below; every bug of the original game the mod mends, with what the game did, is in the game's bugs, fixed.

  • FixedThe Quicksilver Glove fought as if it were wooden (−3 to hit, −2 damage): it had no material, which the game read as wood. It is leather now, as the Chameleon Gloves are.
  • FixedAlagorn told two items wrong: the Quicksilver Glove as "Quicksilver Gauntlets" that graft a weapon to the hand (it casts Detonate on every hit), and the Helm of Contemplation as a shield against psionic attacks (it is Gaze Reflection). His menus now name the glove as the game does, and his two stories say what the items do.
  • FixedAlagorn, asked about clothes when the party carried none, said "no magic clothes", then "Ah, magic clothes", and showed an empty menu. Now he says one thing and the menu matches.
  • FixedThe Effects screen showed "(15 CHARGES)" on a magic item's power (Shadowseeker's Detect Invisibility), from a stray byte. Charges now show only for a spell that lasts a number of uses or a timed effect.
  • FixedA party member cured of poison at a rest (a druid's free Neutralize Poison) still died of the poison: the rest checked for poison before its cures. The cures now come first.
  • FixedIn a busy area, things could stop being drawn (an item dropped, a body, a figure, a doorway), as SSI's own note for Wake of the Ravager describes: the game has 300 picture slots and gave none back until the next area. Once all are taken, a slot nothing uses any more is now reused, so new pictures always show.
  • FixedAfter many changes of gear in one area, the figures on the map could vanish (the party's and the monsters'), coming back for a moment when the inventory was opened: the Loader's dressing of the figures filled the game's store of pictures. The Loader now reuses the store's room for the new outfits, so they always show.
  • FixedThe game's people put on their weapons and armour only at their first action in a fight: until then they were AC 10, their armour unworn, so an opening blow, a backstab or a first-round hit met none, and their Look box showed no weapon. Now they wear and wield their gear from the moment they're on the map, as the game puts it on. They also wear the cloaks, belts, boots, amulets and up to two rings they carry, which the game never put on: each counts by the same rules as the party's.
  • FixedMirror Image stopped no weapon blow, and swallowed the caster's own spells (Armor cast on oneself was lost three times in four, an image with it). Now each hit, and each foe's spell aimed at one creature, has a 75% chance to strike an image instead (50% for Reflected Image); spells from your own side always get through.
  • FixedArmor used up two charges for each hit, and one for any spell cast on its wearer, even a friend's Bless. Now one charge for each damaging hit, a weapon's or a spell's.
  • FixedShare Strength left its strength on the one who got it after the power ended. Now it ends on them too.
  • FixedThe preserver's Dispel Magic also stopped everyone it caught from casting spells, your own party included. Now it only dispels.
  • FixedCause Serious and Critical Wounds did 2d9 and 3d9 damage. Now 2d8+1 and 3d8+3, as AD&D has them and as the Cure spells heal.
  • FixedAbsorb Disease gave the psionicist 1d6 STR as well (and the STR alone with no disease to take). Now it only takes the disease.
  • FixedBless and Aid left out the wrong allies: the check for an enemy beside each one measured from the wrong place.
  • FixedNegative Plane Protection never stopped anything: it saved as against Vampiric Touch, which allows no save. Now a save against death magic stops an undead's draining touch, and the undead takes 2d6.
  • FixedAcid Arrow's acid never burned after the hit. Now it does 2d4 at the start of each round it lasts (four rounds for an 11th-level caster).
  • FixedCloak of Bravery's +4 on saves against fear never applied. Now it does against Scare, Fear, Cause Fear and Insect Plague, and the new Spook.
  • FixedA click on a spell's icon on the Effects screen no longer ends the spell by mistake. A psionic power's icon still ends it, as that's how you stop a power.
  • FixedA berserk or confused party member could be left fighting for the monsters when it wore off. Now they're back on your side.
  • FixedA thri-kreen's claws and bite did crushing damage, as the game makes every unarmed attack, so creatures that crushing weapons can't hurt were safe from it. Now they cut.
  • FixedThe fire elementals' resistances were the wrong way round. Now the fire elemental needs +2 weapons and the lesser one +1, and neither is hurt by fire.
  • FixedMonsters' walls (Wall of Fire, Ice, Force and the rest) came out a single point. Now they run across the line of fire, four cells long.
  • FixedItems that raise or lower an ability score worked from the backpack as well as worn. Now only while worn. (Found by OpenDS and yuan-ti.)
  • FixedThe gladiator's better AC (1 for every 5 levels) came with no armour on. Now only in armour, as Dark Sun has it. (Found by OpenDS and yuan-ti.)
  • FixedPehtucl's ambush at the slave pens' fountain sent every Arena Guard and Guard in the pens after the party, from across the map, and a fight in the passage before the fountain could set off the alarm. Now only Pehtucl and the five guards he calls hunt the party, and the fountain masks a guard's yell in that passage too.
  • FixedAn arrow or sling stone counted as from behind (+2, the target's DEX and shield lost) by the target's facing, from any distance. Now only melee does; a missile is from behind only as a hidden archer's shot, shown as unseen.
  • FixedScare allowed no saving throw at all, so even a 7th-level fighter was Scared. Now 6th level and up always save, as the game's own code means; below 6th, creatures can't save, but an elf or half-elf priest saves as usual (its rule asked for both races at once: found by yuan-ti).

Your Saved Games

You don't need to start a new game for these fixes. They're in the patched game and the Loader, not in your saves, so they work from the moment you load. The fixes stop the old bugs from happening, but don't undo what they already did to a save.

A save isn't for an older version where a release changed what a save holds: 2.13.0, 2.13.2 and 2.14.0 did, so a save made with one of them or later doesn't load in the versions before it. Older saves load in newer versions as they are.

A new game is still the way to see all of the mod. Kits, STORE and RECALL and fuller starting gear come with characters made with the mod, and much of the new content (Kalzith, Semyon, the vulture, the new items in the slave pens) is in the game's first areas. The new and changed gear the game's people carry and its chests hold comes only in areas the save hasn't been to: where the party has been, they keep what they had. A save made with the original game loads and plays with every fix and rule change, but its characters have no kits, and what it has already been through stays as it was. What else an older save keeps is in the README's saved games.
