Changes from the Original Game

A Dark Sun: Shattered Lands mod · Current version 2.19.2 · What the mod adds and changes, and its main bug fixes

These notes describe version 2.19.2, and on GitHub any later change not yet in a release; the changelog says which pull request brought each one, and each version's release notes what it changed from the one before.

Obsidian Edition is a free mod for the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands. It shows the rolls the game keeps hidden, brings its rules closer to AD&D 2nd Edition, adds kits, weapons, magic items and people to Athas, dresses the party in what they wear, and fixes bugs left over since 1993. You start the game with the Loader, which loads the mod with your options and logs every roll as it happens. The new content and a few rules are switchable, the game's own files are never touched, and your saves stay where they are.

At a Glance

  • Every hidden roll shown. The Loader's dice log writes each attack, saving throw, check and creation roll as it happens, with the number it needed and where each bonus came from. A combat log in the game (L) shows the rolls on the map.
  • Rules closer to AD&D 2nd Edition. Saving throws, weapon specialization, two weapons, thief skills, class restrictions and the class tables as the books have them; spells, psionics and monsters as the manual describes them.
  • More to choose and find. Three kits for each class (every kit), twenty-two new magic weapons and more new magic and plain items (every item, and where it is), and new people and an arena bout in the game's first areas.
  • The party as it is dressed. The figures show the gear they wear, magic gear glows on them, and elves have pointed ears.
  • Quicker play. Keys to choose a target, loot a body and sell plain gear, quick saves and autosaves, 40 save slots, a faster default speed, and no copy protection.
  • Bugs fixed. The main fixes are under Bug Fixes; every one, with what the game did, is in the game's bugs, fixed.
  • Wake of the Ravager too. The sequel starts from the Loader with most of the mod, and your party can go on into it (Wake of the Ravager).
  • Your choice. The new content, and a few rules, have a switch in the Loader's Options; the rest is always on. The game's own files are never touched, and saves made with the original game load (see Your Saved Games).

Each change is tagged. [Fixed] is a bug of the game, or a rule it got wrong against its own manual or AD&D: these are always on, with no switch. [Changed] and [New] are the mod's own choices. Every change is told in full in Features and rules, which the links below go to.

In the Game's Screens

  • NewA combat log in the game: L on the map shows every roll in the Loader's colours, at one of three levels of detail, in a panel that scrolls back, shrinks to a line or filters (the combat log).
  • NewOut of a fight, the panel's top line shows the game's time, Day 47, 14:20, or in the arena how many arena fights the party has had.
  • NewNames over figures: a spell's name over its caster, "Miss" over anyone missed, "Armor: 7 left" as a spell's charge is used, "Arrows: 17 left" over an archer (names over figures).
  • NewThe chance to hit, before the swing: with an enemy chosen with Tab, the dice log and the combat log's header say what the d20 must show and the chance (needs 14+ (35%)); rest the pointer on an enemy and the number floats over it (the chance to hit).
  • NewSquares in reach: on a party member's turn a small cross marks every square one click can still walk to, counted by the game's own rules; R hides and shows them (squares in reach).
  • NewHold Alt on the map, out of a fight: a gold ring under every door, chest, body, item and person in view that a click does something with, and the names of those nearest the pointer (showing what can be used).
  • NewThe inventory screen and View Character show THAC0, all five saves, the THAC0 with each weapon, DEX's adjustments, a thief's skills and the age (THAC0, saves and thief skills).
  • NewThe creation screen shows the THAC0 (THAC0: M18 R19), changing with STR, DEX and the kit, and the starting age (the creation screen).
  • NewThe USE screen shows the spell slots left at the level on show, and the most you get after resting (spell slots).
  • NewIn a fight, the Look box shows a monster's hit points, AC, THAC0, alignment, magic resistance and chief defence (the Look box).
  • NewItem boxes show a wand's charges, a cloak's, boots' or belt's bonus to thief skills, and a plain weapon's material penalty (Hit -2 for obsidian) (charges in item boxes).
  • NewThe Effects screen lists kits and weapon specs, a spell's charges left, and what Enlarge, Barkskin and the like give (the Effects screen).
  • NewReading a scroll tells you what happened (YOU LEARN THE SPELL, YOU CAN NOT READ THE SCROLL...) where the game showed nothing (what a scroll says).

Combat

  • NewWeapon specialization: a fighter specializes in one weapon spec (+1 to hit, +2 damage, 3/2 attacks), with mastery at 5th level and grand mastery at 9th; gladiators take two, then more; rangers and three kits gain expertise (weapon specialization).
  • NewTwenty-two weapon specs: six more for the weapons taken from Wake of the Ravager (two-handed sword, lotulis, double spear, trikal, javelin, darts), on a weapon window of four rows; a specialist throws the javelin and darts faster (the twenty-two weapon specs).
  • FixedWarriors attack at AD&D's plain rate outside their specializations (was 3/2 with any weapon); specialists shoot missiles faster; the weapons spells make count as their weapon.
  • ChangedEvery thri-kreen throws a chatkcha once more a round, AD&D's 2/1 for its own weapon (was at the weapon's rate).
  • FixedSpells are saved against with the spell save (was petrification/polymorph for almost every spell) (spell saves).
  • FixedAgainst fire, cold and electricity, DEX helps you dodge, −5 to +6 (was a doubled d20): at DEX 12, a 35% chance to save against a Fireball (was 70%) (DEX, not a doubled d20).
Developers' NotesThe doubled d20 isn't in the manual or in AD&D. It made most enemies shrug off a Fireball. Nimble targets still dodge; slow ones now burn.
  • FixedTwo weapons cost −2 and −4 to hit, less with high DEX, and the off hand attacks once a round (was no penalty, and the full rate in each hand); rangers and some kits are exempt (two weapons).
  • FixedA blow on a held or Out Cold creature hits automatically, for normal damage (was a roll to hit, and double damage) (held targets).
  • ChangedHelms give AC 1 (was 0) (helms).
  • ChangedRings and cloaks of protection follow AD&D: two rings don't stack, and neither works with magical armour (a cloak nor with metal armour or a shield) (rings and cloaks).
  • FixedAcid lets each item save by its material (was any non-magical armour destroyed) (acid).
  • ChangedHalf-giants can hold a two-handed weapon in one hand (half-giants).
  • NewCreature size: a large target takes AD&D's damage for large targets (a long sword 1d12), and Hold and Charm Person don't work on it (the game keeps no sizes) (creature size).
  • ChangedThe pick is the footman's pick, 1d6+1 (2d4 large), and the axe the battle axe, 1d10 large (was the horseman's pick, and an axe 1d8 against any size).
  • NewWeapon traits: cahulaks strike once more a round and can tangle a foe (cahulaks); the staff sling throws stinkpots that nauseate (stinkpots) (was a plain 1d4 shot).
  • NewPolearm reach (with Weapon traits): a polearm held alone strikes a foe two squares away, for the party and its foes alike (polearm reach).
  • NewThe sequel's weapon traits (with Weapon traits): the lotulis' and double spear's last swing of a round strikes a second foe beside the wielder, and a trikal hit can trip a man-sized foe, which loses its next turn; the trikal reaches two squares as a polearm does (lotulis and double spear, trikal).

Classes and Kits

  • ChangedLevels stop at 749,999 XP, shared out between a character's classes, instead of 9th level, and XP stops there too (was 9th level for every class, and XP counting on to 2,000,000,000) (level cap).
  • ChangedCreation keeps what the die rolls: the six scores never add up to more than it gave, and hit points stay as rolled (was any score clicked up to its most) (as rolled).
  • NewSTORE and RECALL on the creation screen, as in Baldur's Gate (STORE and RECALL).
  • FixedClass restrictions hold: the game's own item lists still need only one of a character's classes, and AD&D's limits on armour, shields and weapons hold for every class, the strictest winning (was the game's lists alone); thieves wear light armour, a multiclass preserver can't cast in armour; an air cleric uses daggers and cahulaks (class restrictions).
  • NewNew characters start with fuller gear: a weapon they can use, a sling, leather armour where allowed, and their kit's own weapons (fuller starting gear).
  • FixedMulticlass characters gain hit points as in AD&D, CON's bonus on every hit die (multiclass hit points).
  • ChangedHit dice are rolled twice and the better kept, at creation and every level (hit dice).
  • FixedExperience, priests' THAC0, saving throws and spell slots follow AD&D's tables: a gladiator needs 2,250 XP for 2nd level (was 2,000), a 12th-level fighter saves at 7/9/8/8/10 (was 11/11/11/13/11 from 10th level on), a psionicist by Dark Sun's own table (was a thief's) (class tables).
  • FixedA ranger's spells last and hurt by its level minus 7 (was its full level) (rangers' casting level).
  • NewAges from the Dark Sun rules' tables: age changes the scores, a character can die of old age, and magic (Haste) ages them (ages).
  • FixedCON 20 and up regenerates at AD&D's rate (was every 5 minutes), and a dwarf's or halfling's poison save gets its racial bonus only (CON).
  • FixedDual-classing from 3rd level, with every prime requisite at 15 and 17 (was 2nd level and one score) (dual-classing).
  • FixedPreservers' INT sets their chance to learn a spell from a scroll and the most they know (preservers' INT); new preservers choose their first spells.
  • NewPsionic powers picked at creation on the game's own window (was the same powers for every character of a discipline) (psionic powers).

Kits

  • NewThree kits for each class and thirteen for two or three classes taken at once, 37 in all, each giving something and costing something; one kit a character (kits). The Kits page has each one's gains and costs:
  • Warriors: the fighter's Myrmidon, Sentinel and Ravager; the gladiator's Arena Champion, Twin-blade and Brute; the ranger's Stalker, Justifier and Seeker (fighter, gladiator, ranger).
  • Priests: the cleric's Elementalist, Healer and Crusader; the druid's Grove Warden, Lifebinder and Wanderer (cleric, druid).
  • Preservers: the Scholar, Battle Mage and Arcanist (preserver).
  • Psionicists: the Mind Bender, Mind Warrior and Kineticist (psionicist).
  • Thieves: the Swashbuckler, Assassin and Shinobi, a Veiled Alliance thief who casts preserver spells of its own (thief).
  • More than one class, each for one race: Battle Dancer, Shadowblade, Military Scout, Wilderness Avenger, Rescuer, Ghetto Fighter, Wayfinder, Spellarcher, Titan Mind, Poisoner, Spirit Caller, Wasteland Seer and Wild Fury (multiclass).
  • NewThe game's own party has features of its own: Gerakis fights wild, K'ratchek casts as a Wasteland Seer, Cilla hides as well in the sun, and Cermak is an Arcanist (the game's own party).
Developers' NotesEach kit is built into the game's own code, so it works everywhere the game checks: THAC0, spell slots, hit dice, PSP costs, attacks a round and what you may hold. The Kits page has all 37, with a short description of who takes each one on Athas.

Improved Thieving

  • FixedThief skills follow AD&D's table by level, with Dark Sun's race and DEX adjustments, none above 95 (was a 3rd-level elf with move silently 66) (thief skills).
  • NewThieves hide in shadows and move silently in a fight to backstab, halved in daylight; rangers to attack from behind (was a backstab only on a target facing away) (hiding).
  • FixedA backstab multiplies only the weapon's dice, on the round's first attack only (was the whole damage, STR included, on two attacks).
  • FixedA thief's later attacks in a round after a backstab from hiding count as from behind only when the thief is behind the target (was the first attack's from behind kept for the round: the game's attack routine never clears it).
  • NewPicking pockets with a button on the USE screen: any thief can try, and a purse holds ceramic pieces by the thief's level (picking pockets).
  • ChangedA thief who fails a lock, or is caught at a pocket, tries that one again only after a level (was again and again; caught, never).
  • ChangedA thief beside any square of a big creature can't hide there (was its one square only).
  • NewA worn cloak adds 10 to hiding, boots 10 to moving silently and a belt 5 to picking pockets and locks; a soft breath sounds as a thief hides, a purse's jingle at a picked pocket.

Your Saved Games

You don't need to start a new game for these fixes. They're in the patched game and the Loader, not in your saves, so they work from the moment you load. The fixes stop the old bugs from happening, but don't undo what they already did to a save.

A save isn't for an older version where a release changed what a save holds: 2.13.0, 2.13.2, 2.14.0, 2.15.0 and 2.18.0 did, so a save made with one of them or later doesn't load in the versions before it. Older saves load in newer versions as they are.

A new game is still the way to see all of the mod. Kits, STORE and RECALL and fuller starting gear come with characters made with the mod, and much of the new content (Kalzith, Semyon, the vulture, the new items in the slave pens) is in the game's first areas. The game's people and chests have the new gear only in areas the save hasn't been to. A save made with the original game loads and plays with every fix and rule change, but its characters have no kits. More in the README's saved games.
