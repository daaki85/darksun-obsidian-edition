# The dice log, line by line

*Part of Obsidian Edition's documentation, from the [README](README.md#the-dice-log): every kind
of line the Loader's dice log writes, and what it means. How to read and search the log is in the
README.*

The lines below are the log as it is written: what **Save...** writes, and what the window shows
with **Readable layout** unticked. With it ticked (the default) the window lays the same lines out
outcome first, the roll and HP in columns, the working dimmed under each ([the readable
layout](README.md#the-dice-log)).

## Fights

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Round 2: K'ratchek 32, Cermak 31, Cilla 30, Gerakis 26, Slig 26` | A new round of a fight, numbered from the fight's start, and the order everyone acts in (highest first). The order also stays in view above the log for the whole round, however far the log has scrolled: `Round 2. Now: Cilla (30). Still to act: Gerakis 26, Slig 26. Done: K'ratchek 32, Cermak 31. Down: ...`, and the [combat log in the game](FEATURES.md#the-combat-log-in-the-game) begins with who acts and who is next. The lines under it (shown with **Show details**) give each score's make-up: `    Gerakis 26 = 20 + 6 (0-9 roll), tie broken by 38 (0-199 roll)` (see [Initiative](HOW-THE-GAME-WORKS.md#initiative)). If the log was started in the middle of a round, the list has only the rolls it saw. |
| `Initiative: K'ratchek 32, Cermak 31, ...` | The same order when the log doesn't know the round's number. |
| `Gerakis's turn` | Whose turn it is now, each time the turn passes in a fight. |
| `X attacks Y with Long Sword +1 (1d8+1): d20 = 14, needs 12+ (45%), hits AC 1, target AC 3 -> HIT` | An attack roll, the weapon and its damage dice: the d20, the roll this attacker needed against this target (THAC0 − target AC) and the chance of it, the lowest AC the roll hits, and the target's AC as the game used it. More below the table. |
| `    THAC0 16, +1 Blessed, +6 STR, +1 weapon = 8` | Where the attacker's THAC0 for this attack comes from: STR (melee) or DEX (missiles), spells (Bless, Prayer, Slow, Graft Weapon, the target's Blur), attacking from behind, the weapon's plus, the penalty for plain non-metal weapons (wooden −3, bone −1, stone and obsidian −2), the two-weapon adjustment (see [Two weapons](FEATURES.md#two-weapons)) or a cahulaks head's (`-4 cahulaks, off head at DEX 10`, the main head's −2: [Cahulaks](FEATURES.md#cahulaks)), a weapon spec's (`+1 specialized`, `+3 grand mastery`: [Weapon specialization](KITS.md#weapon-specialization)), a kit's by name, the new spells' (`+1 Erdlu Claw`, `-3 Fogbolt`, `+2 target's Fogbolt`: [New wizard spells](FEATURES.md#new-wizard-spells)), a stinkpot's `-2 Nauseated` ([Staff sling stinkpots](FEATURES.md#staff-sling-stinkpots)), and the difficulty setting for monsters. |
| `  X hits Y for 14: 1d8 = [6] +8 STR 20` | The damage of that hit: the dice, the weapon's bonus, a weapon spec's (`+2 specialized`; a grand master's bigger die, `(d10 for d8: grand mastery)`, against a large target `(1d20 for 1d8: a large target, grand mastery)`), a kit's or Wild Fighting's (`+3 Wild Fighting`), and the STR bonus the game adds for melee. Damage is at least 1. On a creature with Biofeedback, `-2 Biofeedback`: the game takes 2 off (to 0 of a blow of 2 or less; with the manual's spells at least 1 is left). |
| `  X hits Y for 27: 1d8 = [5] +12 STR 24 +10 backstab (the dice 5 x3 = 15)` / `  X hits Y for 51: (1d8 = [5] +12 STR 24) x3 backstab` | A backstab (see [backstabs](HOW-THE-GAME-WORKS.md#attacks-from-behind-and-backstabs)) multiplies the weapon's dice, the rest added once; with that option off, the whole damage, STR bonus included. |
| `  X hits Bulette for 10: 1d12 = [11] -1 bone (1d12 for 1d8: a large target)` / `(... for 2d4: a target smaller than large)` | With [Creature size as in AD&D](FEATURES.md#creature-size) ticked (the default), a weapon rolling other dice for the target's size: AD&D's against a large target (a half-giant, a large monster, anyone Enlarged), or a Shillelagh's 1d6+1 against a smaller one. |
| `  X hits Y for 17: 1d8 = [4] +12 STR 24 +1 Enlarge (the dice 4 x110% = 5, level 1)` / `  X hits Y for 22: (1d8 = [4] +4 weapon +12 STR 24) x110% Enlarge (level 1)` | A blow by an Enlarged attacker: with [Enlarge multiplies the weapon's dice only](FEATURES.md#enlarge-multiplies-the-weapons-dice-only) ticked (the default), the dice grown by 10% a caster level, rounded up, the rest added after; unticked, the game's own, the whole damage multiplied, rounded down. |
| `    X's cahulaks tangle Y? Save against petrification: d20 = 2, needs 19+ -> tangled: loses its next turn` | Under a cahulaks hit that did damage, with [Cahulaks as Dark Sun's rules have them](FEATURES.md#cahulaks) ticked (the default): the target's save against being tangled, its own number against petrification (a 20 always saves); `-> saved`, or tangled, losing its next turn. A target too big for it, or with nothing to tangle, has no line ([the creatures' table](CREATURES.md)). |
| `Y's saving throw vs a stinkpot from X (paralysis/poison/death): d20 = 7, needs 14 (40% to save) -> failed: nauseated` / `    X's stinkpot: Y is nauseated, -2 to hit for 1d3+1 = 2 + 1 = 3 rounds` | After a staff sling's hit that did damage, with [Staff sling stinkpots](FEATURES.md#staff-sling-stinkpots) ticked (the default): the target's save against poison (the game's own, as against a monster's poisoned hit, with its bonuses), then the rounds it is nauseated (−2 to hit) on a failure; `-> saved` and nothing more on a success. A held or sleeping target fails with no roll (`nauseated, no saving throw (helpless)`). An undead or a creature immune to poison: `    X's stinkpot bursts on Y: undead, unaffected` (or `immune to poison`). |
| `    Paralyzed: hit automatically, for normal damage (AD&D's held target)` | Under an attack on a held (Paralyzed) or Out Cold target, with [Held targets as in AD&D](FEATURES.md#held-targets) ticked (the default): the attack hits whatever the d20 (the line above it still shows the roll and what it needed), and the damage line after it is the damage as rolled. |
| `  X hits Y for 36: (1d8 = [5] +1 weapon +12 STR 24) x2 Paralyzed: a helpless target takes double` / `  Y is poisoned by the hit: Poison (10 damage), no saving throw (Paralyzed)` | With [Held targets as in AD&D](FEATURES.md#held-targets) unticked: a blow on a held (Paralyzed) or Out Cold target does double damage, the game's own rule (see [helpless targets](HOW-THE-GAME-WORKS.md#helpless-targets-take-double)): after a backstab's multiple, before Biofeedback's 2. A helpless target makes no saving throw, so a Poisoner's poison on it has no save line: the log names it with the hit, and its 10 (`  Y takes 10 from Poison (10 damage)`) when it lands, which can be after the round's next swing. |
| `Mistra dances: d20 = 3, needs 18 or less (DEX 22 -4) -> +1 to hit, damage and AC for 15 rounds` / `Halton fights wild: d20 = 9, needs 11 or less (CON 15 -4) -> 1 more attack a round and +3 damage for the fight` | A Battle Dancer's dance or a Wild Fury's Wild Fighting as a fight starts (see [Multiclass kits](KITS.md#multiclass-kits)). |
| `Mistra dances: d20 = 20, needs 18 or less (DEX 22 -4) -> stumbles: -1 to hit, damage and AC for 3 rounds (d8 = 3)` / `Mistra's dance is over` / `Halton fights wild: d20 = 14, needs 11 or less (CON 15 -4) -> fails: -3 to hit and AC 3 worse for the fight` | A dance that failed, and its rounds (a d8); a dance's end, good or bad, when its rounds run out; and Wild Fighting that failed. |
| `Chosen with Tab: Guard (50 HP) - Enter attacks it` | An enemy chosen with Tab in a fight (see [Choosing an enemy](FEATURES.md#choosing-an-enemy-tab-enter-and-the-rings)). |
| `Look: Mountain Stalker: HP 38/60, AC 4, THAC0 11, chaotic evil.` | A monster looked at in a fight: what the [Look box](FEATURES.md#what-hurts-a-monster-the-look-box) shows of it, and on the lines below its defences and what its hits do, as the game's window shows them on closing the box. |

**Attack rolls, in more detail.** `hits on anything but a 1` or `only a 20 hits`
replace `needs 12+` when the roll is out of the ordinary range. `X attacks Y
from behind ...` and `X attacks Y BACKSTAB ...` mark attacks from behind and
backstabs, and `X attacks Y unseen ...` a hidden archer's shot (see [Attacks from behind and
backstabs](HOW-THE-GAME-WORKS.md#attacks-from-behind-and-backstabs)). A
thri-kreen of the party with nothing in hand attacks `with a claw (1d4)`, four
times a round, then `with its bite (1d4+1)`, whose damage line shows its
`+1 bite`; with weapons in hand, it attacks with them and then still bites.
The claw's dice are the ones it has now (Erdlu Claw's `1d6+1`, Animal
Affinity's `1d10`, Body Weaponry's `2d4`). Anyone else unarmed, and a
monster's own attacks, have no `with`. The
target's AC includes armour, DEX and spells. A natural 20 always hits and a
natural 1 always misses, but a 20 does no extra damage: the game has no critical
hits (see [No critical hits](HOW-THE-GAME-WORKS.md#no-critical-hits)).

**Kits in the lines.** A kit shows where it changes a number, by name: an attack's THAC0 line has
`from behind (Rescuer)` for the Rescuer's +1, the initiative's parts `Wayfinder's Path-finder`
(or `Wayfinder down`), a healing spell's `+1 Healer` or a Lifebinder's `+ 1d8 Lifebinder (the
helper's roll)`, and a Wilderness Avenger's `+1 Wilderness Avenger` on a Cause spell's damage
([Kits](KITS.md)).

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Shocking Grasp damage: 1d8 = [5] +10 = 15 (1d8 + 1 for each caster level: 10 at caster level 20, which counts as 10)` | A spell's damage roll, rolled for each target before its saving throw, with the spell's formula from the game's data. Damage stops growing at caster level 10 (Fireball does at most 10d6). Magic Missile, Flame Arrow and Minute Meteors are rolled elsewhere in the game, without the caster's level, so their line says how many steps the dice stand for. |
| `Lex's Magical Stone at Zombie: attack d20 = 9, needs 12+ (45%) (THAC0 16, +1 DEX; target AC 3) -> MISS: the spell does nothing, the 7 damage rolled lost` / `Ester's Cause Light Wounds at Slig: touch attack d20 = 15, needs 10+ (55%) (THAC0 18, +1 STR, +1 spells; target AC 6) -> HIT` | With [Spells roll to hit](FEATURES.md#spells-roll-to-hit): a missile spell's or a touch spell's attack roll, after its damage roll (the game rolls the dice first). THAC0 (Acid Arrow's a fighter's of the caster's level), the DEX (missile) or STR (touch) adjustment, the rest of the game's to-hit adjustments (`spells`: Bless, Prayer, the target's Blur...) and a monster's `difficulty`, against the target's AC. A miss does nothing: the damage line before it is lost, and "Miss" floats over the target. |
| `  Slig takes 15 from Shocking Grasp, now 3/18 HP` / `  Cilla takes 9 from Fireball (halved: Prot Fire)` | What the spell really did to each creature, after its save, resistances and protections (or the healing it gave). A protection that halved it is named: Resist Fire or Cold, Protection from Fire or Lightning, Fire Shield, a Wanderer's own. A creature that is Out Cold gets no save and takes the most the dice can do (the game's damage code does that), marked `(Out Cold: the most the dice can do)`. |

| `    Acid Arrow burns 3 rounds more, 2d4 at the start of each (...)` | How many more rounds Acid Arrow's acid burns, worked out from the caster level as a duration is. |

| `Harm: all the target's hit points but 1d4 (...)` / `  Wall of Thorns' wall instead: 8 + AC 3 = 11` / `    Slig is swept off its feet: loses its next turn` | The [6th-level priest spells](FEATURES.md#6th-level-priest-spells)' own lines: Harm leaving 1d4 hit points (the dice the game rolls go unused), Wall of Thorns' damage by the creature's AC in place of the dice rolled, and a creature that fails its save against Whirlwind losing its next turn. |
| `Strength: 1d6 = 5 -> Cilla's STR +5 while it lasts (at most 24)` / `Cat's Grace: 1d6 = 4 -> Gerakis's DEX +4 while it lasts (at most 24)` / `Friends: 2d4 = [4 + 3] = 7 -> Xan's CHA +7 while it lasts (at most 25)` | The amount Strength (or Adrenalin Control) adds to STR, and the [new spells](FEATURES.md#new-wizard-spells) Cat's Grace to DEX and Friends to CHA. With [Spells and psionics as the manual describes them](FEATURES.md#spells-and-psionics-as-the-manual-describes-them) Strength's and Cat's Grace's die is by class, the class named: `Strength: 1d8 = 6 (fighter) -> Ester's STR +6`, `Cat's Grace: 1d8 = 3 (thief) -> Seneca's DEX +3`. |
| `Y magic resistance 30% vs Fireball: d100 = 71 -> not resisted` / `Guard magic resistance 20% (-40 Lower Resistance on it, level 10) vs Scare: ...` / `Templar magic resistance 20% (-40 this Lower Resistance, level 10) vs Lower Resistance to Magic: ...` | The magic resistance roll (only shown for targets that have some), counting Mind Bar, Lower Resistance and the Amulet of Magic Resistance (`+15 Amulet of Magic Resistance`): halved by one on the creature, or, with the manual's spells, 30% + 1% its caster's level off. A Lower Resistance being cast takes its own 30 + level off its own roll (the game's rule; with the manual's spells, the bigger of the two). |
| `Chill Touch on Zombie, undead: no damage, no STR lost; Afraid 1d4 = 3 rounds instead` / `Cloudkill: Guard dies (under 4 HD: no save; its level for saves 3)` / `    Jenna takes 14 damage: Rainbow Pattern's hold on it ends` / `Cell Adjustment: 1d4 = 3 -> Seneca healed 3 HP, and Seneca is cured of disease` / `Heal cures Seneca's Feeblemind: INT and WIS as they were` / `Seneca is feebleminded: Biofeedback can't be used (no psionics)` | What [Spells and psionics as the manual describes them](HOW-THE-GAME-WORKS.md#each-spell-in-more-detail) does beyond the game: Chill Touch on an undead, Cloudkill's kill by hit dice (`4 to 5 HD: the save failed` for the middle band; the death then said as `  Guard is slain by Cloudkill`), damage freeing a creature Rainbow Pattern holds, Cell Adjustment's 1d4 and its cure, Heal curing Feeblemind, and a feebleminded creature's psionic power failing its check. |
| `Shadow drains 1 STR from Gerakis: Weakened for 2d4 = 5 turns (no save)` / `Tari's bite on Seneca: 1 in 10, and a d2 for the manual's 1 in 20: 2 -> no disease` / `Djinn turns invisible (14 rounds) and makes 6 mirror images of itself` | The monsters as the manual describes them (the same box): a shadow's or greater shadow's hit draining STR in place of its cold or paralysis (the STR change follows as the game works it out), a Tari's bite that rolled the game's 1 in 10 for disease and then the d2 that makes it the manual's 1 in 20 (on a 1 the disease's own save comes next), a djinn's first turn of a fight. |
| `At rest: Seneca casts Cure Serious Wounds on Gerakis (4th-level slots: 1 left)` / `At rest: K'ratchek casts Cell Adjustment on Cilla (17 PSP left)` / `Gerakis rests 8 hours: +3, +2 (CON 16) = +5, now 35/40 HP` | [Resting as in AD&D](FEATURES.md#resting-as-in-add): a priest's or psionicist's cast before the rest, with the slots of that level (or PSP) it has left after it (the healing then follows as the game's own lines), and each member's natural healing: 3, CON's part, the total, and the hit points after (`(+2: no more than the most)` when the most stopped it). |

**Saving throws, in more detail.** The modifiers are those of [the game's saving
throws](HOW-THE-GAME-WORKS.md#the-games-saving-throws). With the [rule
changes](FEATURES.md#rule-changes) off, the game uses petrification/polymorph
for almost every spell and doubles the d20 against fire, cold and electricity
spells, as in the example above, so Fireball's victims usually save; with them
on (the default), the line names the spell save and DEX's adjustment instead
(`+5 DEX 21 dodging`). A natural 1 always fails and a natural 20 saves, unless no save is allowed:
`none allowed (cannot save) -> failed` (`cannot save, even on a natural 20` when the d20 was a 20) is a save the game gives −100 (Chaos for
all but warriors, Cloudkill, Scare below 6th level, Color Spray with the manual's
spells): it fails (the game's own compare read the total as a big number and
the creature saved; the patched game's compare is signed, [a bug
fixed](GAME-BUGS.md#spells-and-psionics) whatever the Options). With [Spells and
psionics as the manual describes
them](FEATURES.md#spells-and-psionics-as-the-manual-describes-them) the line also
counts `+2 Fire Shield` against fire and cold and `+1 Blur`.
For a damaging spell the result says what the save left: `saved: half damage, 19
of 38`, `failed: full damage, 38`, or `saved: no damage` for spells such as
Chill Touch (`saved: no damage (not 14)` with the damage rolled); a creature that
saves without a roll has `not needed (saves automatically) -> saved`; the HP line after it shows what the creature really lost, once
resistances and protections have had their say. A failed save also lets the
spell's effect take hold. Spells left on the ground (Grease, clouds) make
creatures save again as they stay in them; those lines have no "from".

## Checks, thief skills and searches

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `X DEX check: d20 = 9, needs 16 or less (DEX 16) -> success` | An ability check. A natural 20 always fails. |
| `Daaki CHA check: 0-19 = 15, needs 16 or less (CHA 20 - 4) -> success` / `Cilla INT check: d20 = 3, needs 4 or less (INT 14 - 10) -> success` | An ability check a script makes with a roll of its own rather than the game's check, against the character acting (the leader outside a fight): talking Chahl round, breaking chains, spotting a secret door. The roll is shown as the script makes it (0 to 19, say), with what it needed. |
| `Daaki shoots at the archery target (15 squares from the target, 8 from the Bowyer): 0-20 = 2, needs under 14 (DEX 21 - 15 / 2), the centre 4 or less -> the centre: the Bowyer's reward, 6 Arrows +2` | A shot at the Bowyer's archery target in Teaquetzl: the roll, what it needed, how far the archer is from the target and from the Bowyer, and why a centre shot did or didn't earn his arrows (more than 13 squares from the target, the Bowyer within 25, once a game). |

| `Fight over: 3 rounds, 4 kills; the party dealt 61 damage and took 19` / `    Seneca: 2 of 2 attacks hit, 30 dealt, 1 kill` / `    Jenna: 3 of 3 attacks hit, 22 dealt, took 6` | A fight's summary as it ends (the game's fight flag down for 2 seconds; each arena wave its own): its rounds, the party's kills, the damage the party dealt (spells too) and took, then a line for each member who attacked, dealt or took damage or killed: weapon attacks that hit out of those made, damage dealt, kills, damage taken. | `Cilla's 3rd Ranger level: hit points d10 = 2, raised to 3 for CON 21` | The hit point roll for a new level: the class's die (d8 clerics and druids, d10 fighters, gladiators and rangers, d4 preservers, d6 psionicists and thieves; the Battle Mage and Arcanist [kits](FEATURES.md#kits) have their own), never less than 2, 3 or 4 with CON 20, 21-22 or 23+, and doubled for half-giants. With [the better of two](FEATURES.md#hit-dice-the-better-of-two) both rolls show (`d10 = 2 and 7, the better 7`), and with [multiclass hit points](FEATURES.md#multiclass-hit-points) CON's bonus on the die (`, +4 CON 18, into the total shared by 3 classes`). |
