Dark Sun: Shattered Lands · Mod

Obsidian Edition
Patch Notes

Current version 2.4.0 · October 16, 2026 · Every change from the original 1993 game

Obsidian Edition is a free mod for the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands. It shows the rolls the game keeps hidden, brings its rules closer to AD&D 2nd Edition, adds kits, weapons, magic items and people to Athas, dresses the party in what they wear, and fixes bugs left over since 1993. You start the game with the Loader, which loads the mod with your options and logs every roll as it happens. Everything is switchable, the game's own files are never touched, and your saves stay where they are.

In the Game's Screens

  • NewA combat log in the game: L on the map shows every roll in the game's own lettering and the Loader's colours, at the least, in short or in detail, and L again hides it, in a fight or after one (a thief's roll, a new level's hit points). A panel over the map's lower right that scrolls back (its scrollbar, or the mouse wheel on Windows), collapses to one line or opens up with filters (All, Party, Damage, Saves); or, as an option, a strip always shown below the map (the game's screen 22 rows taller).
  • NewThe inventory screen shows THAC0, all five saving throws, the THAC0 with each weapon, DEX's reaction and defence adjustments, and a thief's skills, in the game's own lettering.
  • NewView Character shows THAC0 and saves, and for a character of several classes, which class levels up next.
  • NewThe USE screen shows the spell slots left at each spell level, and the most you get after resting.
  • NewIn a fight, the Look box shows a monster's hit points, AC, THAC0, alignment, magic resistance and chief defence (NEEDS +1 WEAPON, IMM FIRE COLD...). Close it to read the rest.
  • NewItem boxes show a wand's charges left, and the bonus a cloak, boots or belt gives to thief skills.
  • NewThe Effects screen lists your kits and weapon specializations.

Combat

Weapon specialization

  • NewFighters specialize in one weapon type (+1 to hit, +2 damage, 3/2 attacks a round), with mastery at 5th level (+3, +3) and grand mastery at 9th (a larger damage die and one more attack).
  • NewGladiators specialize in two weapon types, a third at 6th level and a fourth at 9th. Rangers gain expertise with the bow and one other weapon type.
  • ChangedWarriors attack at AD&D's plain rate with weapons outside their specializations (was 3/2 with any weapon). Specialists shoot missiles faster.

Saving throws

  • ChangedSpells are saved against with the spell save (was petrification/polymorph for almost every spell).
  • ChangedAgainst fire, cold and electricity, DEX helps you dodge, from −5 to +6 (was a doubled d20). Needing 14 at DEX 12, the chance to save against a Fireball is 35% (was 70%).
Developers' NotesThe doubled d20 isn't in the manual or in AD&D. It made most enemies shrug off a Fireball. Nimble targets still dodge; slow ones now burn.

Two weapons

  • ChangedTwo weapons cost −2 to hit with the main hand and −4 with the off hand, less with high DEX; rangers and Twin-blades fight with two at no penalty in light armour (was a small bonus at DEX 5 or less, otherwise nothing).
  • ChangedThe off hand attacks once a round, and extra attacks belong to the main hand (was the full rate in each hand).

Armour and protection

  • ChangedHelms give AC 1 (was 0).
  • ChangedRings and cloaks of protection follow AD&D: two rings don't stack, a ring gives no AC with magical armour, and a cloak does nothing with magical or metal armour or a shield.
  • ChangedAcid and corroding touches let each item save by its material, as in AD&D (was any non-magical armour destroyed outright).
  • ChangedHalf-giants can hold a two-handed weapon in one hand.

Classes and Kits

All classes

  • ChangedEvery class can reach 10th level (was 9th).
  • ChangedCharacter creation keeps what the die rolls: lower a score to raise another, but the six never add up to more than the die gave (shown under CHR as SUM:99/101), and the hit points stay as rolled, moving only with CON's bonus (was any score clicked up to its most, and the hit points set by a click).
  • NewSTORE and RECALL on the character creation screen, as in Baldur's Gate: keep a roll, try the die again, and bring the kept roll back.
  • ChangedAn air cleric can use daggers, as its sphere allows (was only Galefang: none of the game's daggers listed the air cleric).
  • NewNew characters start with fuller gear: a weapon in hand they can use, a sling, leather armour where they may wear it, a Stalker, Assassin or Shinobi a cloak, and some kits weapons of their own (a Battle Dancer two long swords, a Poisoner two daggers). A multiclass preserver starts with no armour, so it can cast.
  • ChangedClass restrictions hold: a character may use an item only if every one of its classes allows it, and a multiclass preserver can't cast in armour (was any one class allowing it). Thieves wear light armour only; a multiclass thief (or a human once a warrior or cleric) may wear heavier, losing its thief skills and backstab in it.
  • ChangedMulticlass characters gain hit points as in AD&D: CON's bonus on every hit die (a warrior's if one class is a warrior), the total divided between the classes. Every hit die is rolled twice and the better kept.
  • ChangedExperience, priests' THAC0 and spell slots follow AD&D's tables: a gladiator needs 2,250 XP for 2nd level (was 2,000), a preserver has 4 2 1 slots at 5th (was 3 2 1), a cleric 3 3 1 (was 3 2 1).
  • ChangedA ranger's spells last and deal damage at its level minus 7, as AD&D has it (was its full level).
  • ChangedPreservers' INT sets their chance to learn a spell from a scroll (35% at INT 9 to 100% at 24) and the most spells of each level they can know. New preservers choose their starting spells.

  • Arcanist: one more spell slot at each level, and two preserver spells per turn in a fight, for a d3 hit die.
  • Battle Mage: a warrior's THAC0, leather armour it can cast in, expertise with one weapon, and spells that still go off after being hit.
  • Shinobi: a thief trained by the Veiled Alliance who casts preserver spells from 6th level, and learns its own from a scroll only if it can read it (its read languages).

Developers' NotesEach kit is built into the game's own code, so it works everywhere the game checks: THAC0, spell slots, hit dice, PSP costs, attacks a round and what you may hold. The player's guide has all 37, with a short description of who takes each one on Athas.

Magic and Psionics

  • NewSix new wizard spells, each with its own icon and description: Cat's Grace (2nd level: DEX +1d6 for an hour of game time a caster level), Erdlu Claw (1st level: unarmed attacks become talons that hit at +1 for 1d6+1, as a +1 magical weapon; Body Weaponry's and Animal Affinity's dice gain +1) and Fogbolt (1st level: a dark grey bolt that gives a creature −3 to its attacks and +2 to attacks on it, for 2d4 rounds), and at 1st level too Reflected Image (one image of the caster, struck instead of it half the time), Spook (a creature flees for 3 rounds unless it saves) and Friends (CHA +2d4 for a short while, for better prices).
  • ChangedA click on a spell's icon on the Effects screen no longer ends the spell by mistake. A psionic power's icon still ends it, as that's how you stop a power.

Improved Thieving

  • ChangedThief skills follow AD&D's table by level, with Dark Sun's race and DEX adjustments (was a 3rd-level elf with move silently 66 and hide in shadows 61).
  • NewThieves hide in shadows and move silently in a fight to backstab, halved in daylight. Rangers do it to attack from behind, at full strength under the open sky (was a backstab only on a target turned to face someone else).
  • NewPicking pockets with Thieves' Tools (Mirlon in the slave pens carries a set, and gives it to a thief leading the party who agrees to fetch his gem; Kel sells two); use them on anyone in sight. Fail and you roll move silently to get away. A few named weapons can be lifted for 200 XP.
  • NewA worn cloak adds 10 to hiding, boots 10 to moving silently, and a belt 5 to picking pockets and opening locks, for thieves and rangers alike; the inventory screen's thief skills count them.
  • FixedThief skills no longer drop for holding a weapon. The game's armour penalty looked at the hands, quiver and legs, and never at the armour worn.

World

  • NewKalzith, a defiler slave who scribes spell scrolls by night (six spells the game has no scroll of) and sells them to anyone who treats him with respect.
  • NewMore of Semyon's story, for players who help him in the arena.
  • NewA use for the cooked vulture: share it with the pens' cook for a full rest and 100 XP each.
  • NewMerchants give your party leader a discount for high Charisma, from 5% at CHA 18 to 40% at 25, and pay 60% of an item's price (was all of it).
  • NewMerchants keep the magical items you sell them, to buy back (was destroyed when sold).

Graphics

  • NewThe party's figures show the weapons, shields, armour, helms and cloaks they wear, in each material's colours, walking and fighting.
  • NewElves have pointed ears.
  • NewHalf-elves no longer look like humans: a half-elf man is built like the elf, a little broader, with short fair hair, a leather jerkin, green trousers and pointed ears; a half-elf woman is a little slimmer than the human woman, with golden hair in a bun, pointed ears, a purple cloak and red boots.

Quality of Life

  • NewTab chooses the nearest enemy and Enter attacks it, with no aiming of the mouse.
  • NewPress the mouse wheel and drag to scroll the map.
  • ChangedIn a fight the view stays put while a figure walks, moving only to bring back one that walks off the screen (was: it followed each one near the edge).
  • Changed40 save slots on four pages (was 10), each also kept under its name in a folder of the Loader's, and 29 stored characters (was 19).
  • NewA game speed setting, so the whole party walks as smoothly as one.
  • NewCrash reports: if the game stops with an error, the message stays on screen and a report is written, with the game's own error message in it; if it freezes, a freeze report saves what it was doing; if DOSBox closes while the game still runs, a report says so.
  • NewThe game's window at double, triple or quadruple size, or full screen, chosen in the Loader.
  • ChangedNo more copy protection: the dragon asking for a word from the manual never comes.

The Loader

  • NewThe Loader: it starts the game with the mod and your options, and stays open while you play, laid out in the game's own colours.
  • NewThe dice log: every attack, damage roll, saving throw, thief skill, initiative score and character creation roll as it happens, with the number it needed, the chance of success, and where every bonus came from.
  • NewCharacters: each party member's sheet as View Character lays it out, plus THAC0 with each weapon, saves as the d20 needed right now, attacks a round, current AC and what it's made of, spell slots and thief skills.
  • NewSpells: every wizard spell, priest spell and psionic power's damage dice, saving throw, effect and duration, read from the running game.
  • NewDialogue: everything said, with the speaker's portrait and name, and the reply you chose.
  • NewOptions: every rule change and addition in these notes has its own switch, nearly all on by default, and Restore defaults sets them all back.
Developers' NotesShattered Lands rolls dice for everything and shows you almost none of it. Fireball victims kept saving and nobody knew why (the game doubles the d20 against fire). The Loader started as a way to see those numbers. Once we could see them, we started fixing them.

Bug Fixes

  • FixedDELETE in the character roster deleted the wrong character when the list was scrolled.
  • FixedA character not yet played showed 0 in every thief skill.
  • FixedThe game's Sling +1 had no icon.
  • FixedMirror Image stopped no weapon blow, and swallowed the caster's own spells (Armor cast on oneself was lost three times in four, an image with it). Now each hit, and each foe's spell aimed at one creature, has a 75% chance to strike an image instead (50% for Reflected Image); spells from your own side always get through.
  • FixedArmor used up two charges for each hit, and one for any spell cast on its wearer, even a friend's Bless. Now one charge for each damaging hit.
  • FixedShare Strength left its strength on the one who got it after the power ended. Now it ends on them too.
  • FixedThe preserver's Dispel Magic also stopped everyone it caught from casting spells, your own party included. Now it only dispels.
  • FixedHold Undead held living creatures too. Now only the undead.
  • FixedCilla, an elf in the ready-made party, was drawn with the half-elf woman's figure; she looks like an elf now (a switch keeps the game's figure).

Rules the game got wrong are put right too, each under its own heading above with a switch, on by default: the saving throws against spells and the doubled d20 against fire, cold and electricity, two weapons and warriors' attacks (Combat); class restrictions, multiclass hit points, the class tables and rangers' spells (Classes and Kits); thief skills (Improved Thieving); and acid on armour (Combat).

Your Saved Games

You don't need to start a new game for these fixes. They're in the patched game and the Loader, not in your saves, so they work from the moment you load: images, Armor's charges, Share Strength, Dispel Magic and Hold Undead follow the new rules from the next spell cast, even on effects already running. The fixes stop the old bugs from happening, but don't undo what they already did to a save:

  • A character who kept the strength from Share Strength after the power ended still has it (it shows on their Effects screen). Load a save from before the power was used to be rid of it.
  • A character silenced by the preserver's Dispel Magic ("No spell use" on their Effects screen) casts again once it wears off.
  • A new game whose arena fight never began (no announcer, and walking said NO PATH FROM HERE: a 2.4.0 bug) can't be mended: start a new one.

A new game is still the way to see all of the mod. Kits, STORE and RECALL and fuller starting gear come with characters made with the mod, and much of the new content (Kalzith, Semyon, the vulture, Mirlon's tools, the new items in the slave pens) is in the game's first areas. A save made with the original game loads and plays with every fix and rule change, but its characters have no kits, and what it has already been through stays as it was.
