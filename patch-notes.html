Dark Sun: Shattered Lands · Mod

Obsidian Edition
Patch Notes

A Dark Sun: Shattered Lands mod · Current version 2.13.2 · Every change from the original game

Obsidian Edition is a free mod for the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands. It shows the rolls the game keeps hidden, brings its rules closer to AD&D 2nd Edition, adds kits, weapons, magic items and people to Athas, dresses the party in what they wear, and fixes bugs left over since 1993. You start the game with the Loader, which loads the mod with your options and logs every roll as it happens. Everything is switchable, the game's own files are never touched, and your saves stay where they are.

In the Game's Screens

  • NewA combat log in the game: L on the map shows every roll, in the game's own lettering and the Loader's colours, at one of three levels of detail, and L again hides it. It works in a fight or after one (a thief's roll, a new level's hit points).
  • NewThe combat log is a panel over the map's lower right that scrolls back (its scrollbar, or the mouse wheel on Windows), shrinks to one line or opens up with filters (All, Party, Damage, Saves).
  • NewNames over figures: a spell's name floats over its caster as it is cast, "Miss" over anyone an attack misses, and "Armor: 7 left" when a spell that lasts a number of uses (Armor, Mirror Image, Reflected Image...) loses one, in the game's lettering (an Options switch, on).
  • NewOut of a fight, the combat log panel's top line shows how long the game has run, Day 47, 14:20; in the arena, once the game has shown the count, how many arena fights the party has had instead (the game's own count, one a trip into the arena).
  • NewThe inventory screen shows THAC0, all five saving throws, the THAC0 with each weapon, DEX's reaction and defence adjustments, a thief's skills and, with ages on, the character's age, in the game's own lettering.
  • NewThe creation screen shows the THAC0, right of the hit points: THAC0: M18 R19, melee (with STR's to-hit) and ranged (with DEX's missile adjustment), changing as you click STR and DEX.
  • NewWith ages on, the creation screen shows the age a new character will start at, above RECALL: Table 7's dice, rolled again with each roll of the die (STORE and RECALL keep it with the roll).
  • NewView Character shows THAC0 and saves, and for a character of several classes, which class levels up next.
  • NewThe USE screen shows the spell slots left at the spell level and list on show (wizard slots on the MAGE list, priest slots on the CLERIC list), and the most you get after resting.
  • NewIn a fight, the Look box shows a monster's hit points, AC, THAC0, alignment, magic resistance and chief defence (NEEDS +1 WEAPON, IMM FIRE COLD...). Close it to read the rest.
  • NewItem boxes show a wand's charges left, and the bonus a cloak, boots or belt gives to thief skills.
  • NewThe Effects screen lists your kits and weapon specializations, and a Shinobi's chance to read languages; with the pointer on the icon of a spell that lasts a number of uses (Armor, Mirror Image, Reflected Image...), it says how many charges are left, for Enlarge how much more it gives (ENLARGED (+90% DICE), or DAMAGE with its rule off), and for Barkskin, Flesh Armor and Magical Vestments the armour AC they give (BARKSKIN (AC 3)).
  • NewReading a scroll on the inventory screen tells you what happened (YOU LEARN THE SPELL, YOU CAN NOT READ THE SCROLL, THAT IS NOT A SHINOBI SPELL...) on the line under the paper doll. The game showed nothing there.

Combat

Weapon specialization

  • NewFighters specialize in one weapon spec, a kind of weapon such as the long sword (+1 to hit, +2 damage, 3/2 attacks a round), with mastery at 5th level (+3, +3) and grand mastery at 9th (a larger damage die and one more attack).
  • NewGladiators specialize in two weapon specs, a third at 6th level and a fourth at 9th. Rangers gain expertise with the bow and one other weapon spec. The Battle Mage, Crusader and Mind Warrior kits gain expertise with one melee weapon spec of their choice (3/2 attacks a round, 2 from 7th level); a thri-kreen Mind Warrior may take the chatkcha.
  • ChangedWarriors attack at AD&D's plain rate with weapons outside their specializations (was 3/2 with any weapon). Specialists shoot missiles faster.
  • NewThe weapons spells make count for specialization: the Flame Blade as a long sword, Shillelagh as a club, Spiritual Hammer as a mace.

Saving throws

  • ChangedSpells are saved against with the spell save (was petrification/polymorph for almost every spell).
  • ChangedAgainst fire, cold and electricity, DEX helps you dodge, from −5 to +6 (was a doubled d20). Needing 14 at DEX 12, the chance to save against a Fireball is 35% (was 70%).
Developers' NotesThe doubled d20 isn't in the manual or in AD&D. It made most enemies shrug off a Fireball. Nimble targets still dodge; slow ones now burn.

Two weapons

  • ChangedTwo weapons cost −2 to hit with the main hand and −4 with the off hand, less with high DEX; rangers and Twin-blades fight with two at no penalty in light armour (was a small bonus at DEX 5 or less, otherwise nothing).
  • ChangedThe off hand attacks once a round, and extra attacks belong to the main hand (was the full rate in each hand).

Armour and protection

  • ChangedHelms give AC 1 (was 0).
  • ChangedRings and cloaks of protection follow AD&D: two rings don't stack, a ring gives no AC with magical armour, and a cloak does nothing with magical or metal armour or a shield.
  • ChangedAcid and corroding touches let each item save by its material, as in AD&D (was any non-magical armour destroyed outright).

Races

  • ChangedHalf-giants can hold a two-handed weapon in one hand.
  • ChangedA thri-kreen in the party fights with edged claws, so creatures that crushing weapons can't hurt are no longer safe from it (was crushing, as every unarmed attack).

Classes and Kits

All classes

  • ChangedLevels stop at a cap of 749,999 XP instead of 9th level, shared out: one class goes as far as its next level needs no more (cleric 11th, druid 12th, fighter, gladiator and ranger 10th, preserver and psionicist 11th, thief 13th); two or three classes share it evenly (374,999 or 249,999 each); a human who changed class has what its old classes' levels didn't use (was 9th level for every class).
  • ChangedCharacter creation keeps what the die rolls: lower a score to raise another, but the six never add up to more than the die gave (shown under CHR as SUM:99/101), and the hit points stay as rolled, moving only with CON's bonus (was any score clicked up to its most, and the hit points set by a click).
  • NewSTORE and RECALL on the character creation screen, as in Baldur's Gate: keep a roll, try the die again, and bring the kept roll back.
  • ChangedAn air cleric can use daggers, as its sphere allows (was none: no dagger in the game listed the air cleric).
  • NewNew characters start with fuller gear: a weapon in hand they can use, a sling, leather armour where they may wear it, a Stalker, Assassin or Shinobi a cloak, and some kits weapons of their own (a Battle Dancer two long swords, a Poisoner two of its weapon spec, daggers or short swords). A multiclass preserver starts with no armour, so it can cast.
  • ChangedClass restrictions hold: a character may use an item only if every one of its classes allows it, and a multiclass preserver can't cast in armour (was any one class allowing it). Thieves wear light armour only; a multiclass thief (or a human once a warrior or cleric) may wear heavier, losing its thief skills and backstab in it.
  • ChangedMulticlass characters gain hit points as in AD&D: CON's bonus on every hit die (a warrior's if one class is a warrior), the total divided between the classes. Every hit die is rolled twice and the better kept.
  • ChangedExperience, priests' THAC0 and spell slots follow AD&D's tables: a gladiator needs 2,250 XP for 2nd level (was 2,000), a preserver has 4 2 1 slots at 5th (was 3 2 1), a cleric 3 3 1 (was 3 2 1).
  • ChangedA ranger's spells last and deal damage at its level minus 7, as AD&D has it (was its full level).
  • ChangedPreservers' INT sets their chance to learn a spell from a scroll (35% at INT 9 to 100% at 24) and the most spells of each level they can know. New preservers choose their starting spells.

They stay in the quiver until shot, as a Flame Blade stays in the hand.
  • Wild Fury: Wild Fighting as each fight starts, a roll against CON at −4: one more attack a round in melee and +3 damage, or −3 to hit and AC 3 worse if it fails.
  • NewThe game's own party has features no one else can have: Gerakis fights wild, K'ratchek is a Wasteland Seer (spells and psionics) and throws her chatkcha twice a round, Cilla, an Elven Wanderer, hides as well in the sun as out of it, and Cermak is an Arcanist.
Developers' NotesEach kit is built into the game's own code, so it works everywhere the game checks: THAC0, spell slots, hit dice, PSP costs, attacks a round and what you may hold. The player's guide has all 37, with a short description of who takes each one on Athas.

Magic and Psionics

  • NewAges (a rule change, on unless unticked): each character has an age from the Dark Sun rules' tables, shown on the inventory screen and the Loader's Characters tab. Middle, old and venerable age change the scores, and a character dies at the most its race lives; everyone is a year older each 365 days of game time (the dice log says so), and magic ages them faster. Haste now asks each creature it speeds for a system shock roll by CON, as in AD&D: one that fails dies, and a party member who survives is a year older. Restore Youth (6th-level priest, below) takes magical years back.
  • ChangedEnlarge makes only the weapon's dice bigger, 10% a caster level, rounded up, with STR and magic added after, as in AD&D (a rule change, on unless unticked) (was the whole melee damage made bigger, STR included).

Improved Thieving

  • ChangedThief skills follow AD&D's table by level, with Dark Sun's race and DEX adjustments, and none goes above 95 (was a 3rd-level elf with move silently 66 and hide in shadows 61).
  • NewThieves hide in shadows and move silently in a fight to backstab, halved in daylight. Rangers do it to attack from behind, at full strength under the open sky (was a backstab only on a target turned to face someone else).
  • ChangedA backstab multiplies only the weapon's dice; STR and magic bonuses are added once after, as in AD&D, and only the round's first attack is a backstab (was the whole damage multiplied, STR included, so a strong thief could hit for over 100, on the first two attacks of the round).
  • NewPicking pockets with a thief's Pick Pockets button on the USE screen (where a priest's Turn Undead is), then a click on anyone in sight: any thief can try, leader or not, and what's lifted goes in that thief's backpack. A cleric/thief has both buttons, the purse beside Turn Undead. Fail and you roll move silently to get away. A purse holds 2 to 5 ceramic pieces for each of the thief's levels, and a few named weapons can be lifted for 200 XP.
  • NewA worn cloak adds 10 to hiding, boots 10 to moving silently, and a belt 5 to picking pockets and opening locks, for thieves and rangers alike; the inventory screen's thief skills count them.

  • NewA warning when memory runs low: the dice log tells you to save and restart before the game runs out (a fresh start has all of it back).
  • NewItem reports: if an item vanishes from the party with nothing ordinary to explain it (not dropped, given, sold, used up or broken), or the game destroys one (acid, Detonate's blast), the Loader saves a report in crash-logs, as it does for a crash, and says so in the dice log.
  • NewThe game's window at double, triple or quadruple size, or full screen, chosen in the Loader.
  • ChangedNo more copy protection: the dragon asking for a word from the manual never comes.

The Loader

  • NewThe Loader: it starts the game with the mod and your options, and stays open while you play, laid out in the game's own colours.
  • NewThe dice log: every attack, damage roll, saving throw, thief skill, initiative score and character creation roll as it happens, with the number it needed, the chance of success, and where every bonus came from.
  • NewCharacters: each party member's sheet as View Character lays it out, plus THAC0 with each weapon, saves as the d20 needed right now, attacks a round, current AC and what it's made of, spell slots and thief skills.
  • NewSpells: every wizard spell, priest spell and psionic power's damage dice, saving throw, effect and duration, read from the running game.
  • NewDialogue: everything said, with the speaker's portrait and name, and the reply you chose.
  • NewFind in the dice log and the dialogue (Ctrl+F): every match highlighted, Enter for the next.
  • NewMore of a fight in the dice log: the psionic defence against Ego Whip, Id Insinuation, Psychic Crush and Psionic Blast; the protection that halved a spell's damage (Resist Fire, Fire Shield...); what a monster's special attack does (Paralysing attack: lasts 7 rounds unless saved against); and Detonate's blast on an item: which one, the d20, and whether it's gone (it picks the worn item that breaks most easily, a bone helm before bone armour).
  • NewMore of the game's own choices in the dice log: why it refused a spell you clicked (no line of sight, too far...); what a script asked for (a rest, random monsters, an arena bout); the ability checks scripts make with a roll of their own (a CHA to talk someone round, STR to break chains); and a party member's ability score changing.
  • NewEach shot at the Bowyer's archery target in the dice log: the roll, what it needed, how far the archer is from the target and the Bowyer, and whether a centre shot earns his arrows.
  • NewHow the game works compares every cleric spell and psionic power with the manual: what the manual says, what the game's code does, and what the log shows.
  • NewThe Loader can run the game as Dark Sun Recompiled, the game translated into a program of its own, instead of in DOSBox, where it is installed.
  • NewOptions: nearly every rule change and addition in these notes has its own switch (not the keys and the game's fixed bugs), nearly all on by default, and Restore defaults sets them all back.
  • NewAccessibility: text that can be enlarged up to 2.5 times (A+, A-, Ctrl + and Ctrl -), every control reachable from the keyboard, colours with at least 4.5:1 contrast, and the logs saved as text for screen readers.
Developers' NotesShattered Lands rolls dice for everything and shows you almost none of it. Fireball victims kept saving and nobody knew why (the game doubles the d20 against fire). The Loader started as a way to see those numbers. Once we could see them, we started fixing them.

Bug Fixes

  • FixedMirror Image stopped no weapon blow, and swallowed the caster's own spells (Armor cast on oneself was lost three times in four, an image with it). Now each hit, and each foe's spell aimed at one creature, has a 75% chance to strike an image instead (50% for Reflected Image); spells from your own side always get through.
  • FixedArmor used up two charges for each hit, and one for any spell cast on its wearer, even a friend's Bless. Now one charge for each damaging hit, a weapon's or a spell's.
  • FixedShare Strength left its strength on the one who got it after the power ended. Now it ends on them too.
  • FixedThe preserver's Dispel Magic also stopped everyone it caught from casting spells, your own party included. Now it only dispels.
  • FixedCause Serious and Critical Wounds did 2d9 and 3d9 damage. Now 2d8+1 and 3d8+3, as AD&D has them and as the Cure spells heal.
  • FixedAbsorb Disease gave the psionicist 1d6 STR as well (and the STR alone with no disease to take). Now it only takes the disease.
  • FixedBless and Aid left out the wrong allies: the check for an enemy beside each one measured from the wrong place.
  • FixedNegative Plane Protection never stopped anything: it saved as against Vampiric Touch, which allows no save. Now a save against death magic stops an undead's draining touch, and the undead takes 2d6.
  • FixedAcid Arrow's acid never burned after the hit. Now it does 2d4 at the start of each round it lasts (four rounds for an 11th-level caster).
  • FixedCloak of Bravery's +4 on saves against fear never applied. Now it does against Scare, Fear, Cause Fear and Insect Plague, and the new Spook.
  • FixedA click on a spell's icon on the Effects screen no longer ends the spell by mistake. A psionic power's icon still ends it, as that's how you stop a power.
  • FixedA berserk or confused party member could be left fighting for the monsters when it wore off. Now they're back on your side.
  • FixedThe fire elementals' resistances were the wrong way round. Now the fire elemental needs +2 weapons and the lesser one +1, and neither is hurt by fire.
  • FixedMonsters' walls (Wall of Fire, Ice, Force and the rest) came out a single point. Now they run across the line of fire, four cells long.
  • FixedThief skills no longer drop for holding a weapon. The game's equipment penalty looked at the hands, quiver and legs, and never at the armour worn.
  • FixedDELETE in the character roster deleted the wrong character when the list was scrolled.
  • FixedA character not yet played showed 0 in every thief skill.
  • FixedOnce the party has killed Legcrusher, Kurzak no longer calls for him when they're late for a fight, nor greets them as if he'd brought them, nor speaks of him as still about when asked (Dinos neither): he wonders where his monster trainer has got to.
  • FixedThe Trustee in the slave pens could stop on his rounds and stay busy for good, never talking again. Now he walks on, even in a save where he's already stuck, once talked to; and a save made while he was walking no longer leaves him standing still when it's loaded (the same for anyone else who walks rounds of their own).
  • FixedIn a long session the game could stop with "Out of memory in OEOpenGraph": each time the map comes back (after the USE screen, a talk, a menu) it needs a large block of free memory, and little was left over. Part of the game's code and its music now live in upper memory, which leaves about 97 KB to spare with the map up (the original game: 57 KB).
  • FixedWhen the game rolls a thief skill for the whole party (a trap going off, listening in by a wagon), the first party member always rolled, however poor at it. Now the member with the best chance does.
  • FixedRandom monsters came in numbers worked out from the wrong monster, and in some areas could crash the game ("Divide error"). Now the count fits the monster that comes, and the crash can't happen.
  • FixedMagic items didn't shine in a shop (Kalzith's, for one): their glow stood still. Now it moves as it does everywhere else.
  • FixedThe game's Sling +1 had no icon.
  • FixedView Character showed one or two more damage than a non-magical wooden, bone, stone or obsidian weapon does: it left out the weaker material (2 for wood, 1 for the others). It now matches the inventory and the fight.
  • FixedA hasted or slowed character's damage lines (the inventory, View Character) showed their attacks a round as if they were neither. Now Haste doubles them and Slow halves them there (0.5x1D8), as in the fight itself.
  • FixedAn archer's pointer showed "can't shoot" after a shot that killed, with shots left (a click still shot). Now it shows "can't" only once every shot of the round is made.
  • FixedSlay Living's description said a creature that saves takes 3 to 17; the game does 2 to 16 (half of 4d8). It says so now.
  • FixedThree of the game's script bugs: in Darkhold the portcullis guard stopped fighting back when attacked and three creatures lost their look text; the Lava Rifts ranger's trigger ran the hermit's talk (endless, with a second Iron Necklace); and Linara in Gedron, after the extract, ended her talk with no menu, so Jasmine's spellbook never came up.
  • FixedCilla, an elf in the ready-made party, was drawn with the half-elf woman's figure; she looks like an elf now (a switch keeps the game's figure).

Rules the game got wrong are put right too, each under its own heading above with a switch, on by default: the saving throws against spells and the doubled d20 against fire, cold and electricity, two weapons, warriors' attacks and acid on armour (Combat); class restrictions, multiclass hit points, the class tables and rangers' spells (Classes and Kits); Enlarge's dice, CON's regeneration and poison saves, and Haste's aging (Magic and Psionics); and thief skills (Improved Thieving).

Your Saved Games

You don't need to start a new game for these fixes. They're in the patched game and the Loader, not in your saves, so they work from the moment you load: images, Armor's charges, Share Strength, Dispel Magic and the rest follow the new rules from the next spell cast, even on effects already running. The fixes stop the old bugs from happening, but don't undo what they already did to a save:

  • A character who kept the strength from Share Strength after the power ended still has it (it shows on their Effects screen). Load a save from before the power was used to be rid of it.
  • A character silenced by the preserver's Dispel Magic ("No spell use" on their Effects screen) casts again once it wears off.
  • A new game whose arena fight never began (no announcer, and walking said NO PATH FROM HERE: a 2.4.0 bug) can't be mended: start a new one.
  • Thieves' Tools from an earlier version of the mod (Mirlon's or Kel's) do nothing now: a thief picks pockets with the Pick Pockets button.
  • A Healer from an earlier version keeps its sphere and is given the cosmos spells when the game is loaded (the dice log says so).
  • With ages on, each character in a save, the game's own party too, is given a starting age the first time the Loader sees them (the dice log shows the roll).
  • A save made with 2.13.0 is not for earlier versions. It keeps the weapon specs packed with the age beside them, which an older Loader reads as other specs. Older saves load in 2.13.0 as they are.
  • A save made with this version is not for 2.13.1 or earlier. It keeps each character's starting roll beside the weapon specs (so Restore Youth can take back any number of magical years), which an older Loader reads as other specs. A character from an older save keeps its age, and its roll is put on its sheet when the game is loaded. From 2.13.1 a character's true age (how young Restore Youth can make them) is worked out a new way, the same as the creation screen shows; an age already given stays as it is.
  • A thri-kreen druid/fighter Wasteland Seer from an earlier version has no kit now: the Wasteland Seer is for a thri-kreen druid/fighter/psionicist.
  • In a save from 1.2.0 to 2.5.0 that has already been to the slave pens, the weapon rack holding Thornwall is gone from the pens (a new game has it). Fixing items that couldn't be swapped in a pack meant renumbering the rack, and the game drops an object it no longer finds.

A new game is still the way to see all of the mod. Kits, STORE and RECALL and fuller starting gear come with characters made with the mod, and much of the new content (Kalzith, Semyon, the vulture, the new items in the slave pens) is in the game's first areas. A save made with the original game loads and plays with every fix and rule change, but its characters have no kits, and what it has already been through stays as it was.
