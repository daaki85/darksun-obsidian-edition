procedure's rule 1 in [PLAYTESTING.md](../PLAYTESTING.md#startup-procedure)). The stories they hold,
by topic, are in [HISTORY.md](../HISTORY.md), whose [date index](../HISTORY.md#when-things-were-learned)
says what each day found. `img/` holds the pictures the logs name; `files/` the scripts, timing
schedules and diffs a log made or used.
