## What can pin a party member, and whether it still has a turn

From the game's code and the earlier logs, then seen here:

- **The pin itself** is the effect routine at DSUN.EXE 78480h (a far routine of the effects'
  overlay, `retf` at 78706h): for every effect put on (34 or 51, from any source) it runs
  786A9h-786CBh, `and word es:[bx+1D5h], 0FFFEh` (bit 0 of the turn flags, "can walk"). So whatever
  gives the effect, a spell, a spell on the ground, a monster's power, clears the walk at once, on
  whoever's turn it lands. The only other `and ...,0FFFEh` (591BCh) is the walk's own: under 10
  points left.
- **Stuck (51)**: Web, Entangle, Solid Fog, Quicksand, Grease. **It leaves the member its turn**:
  the turn opens with the END menu up, the points 0 and the turn's start points 0, the start square
  its own (f10watch: `start (0, 1512, 936)`); Escape closes the menu and the member can cast
  ([2026-11-08](2026-11-08-manual-spells.md)) or press F10. **And a Web pins mid-walk, on the
  member's own turn**: the save is rolled on the step into a web square (`Lex's saving throw vs Web
  (spell)` after his third step), and a failure stops the walk there with the points it had left
  and the END menu up. That is the real case the user asked for.
- **Paralyzed (34)**: Hold Person, Hold Monster, Rainbow Pattern, Blood Flow on a foe, a sand
  howler's gaze, Id Insinuation. **A member Paralyzed before its turn loses the turn**: the Templar's
  Hold Person (2026-11-20, Lex), and here Rainbow Pattern, whose save is rolled at each creature's
  turn start, before "X's turn": Seneca failed, "Seneca's turn" came and went in about 150 ms
  (f10watch: turn 3 at 27.09, turn 1 at 27.24), no input taken; Ester and Jenna the same a round
  later, their turns skipped until "Paralyzed ends". Rainbow Pattern rolls no save while a member
  walks in it (Ester walked five squares in it: none). **But a Paralyzed can land on the member's
  own turn**: its own Hold Person turned back by a foe's Spell Turning. The turn stays (Hold
  Person's pointer up for the next target, as Magical Stone's keeps the turn), with the flags 0 from
  the cast. In the original game F10 then put the caster back on its first square with its points.

## Learned

- A pin from the game's own play with the member's turn still running: **a Web** (and by the same
  rule, it seems, the other spells on the ground that give Stuck) entered on the member's own walk,
  with movement left or on the last step, and **a member's own Hold Person turned back by Spell
  Turning** (Paralyzed with the turn kept). A Stuck member also has every later turn while stuck,
  with 0 points.
- Paralyzed before the turn (Hold Person, Rainbow Pattern's turn-start save) skips the turn at once.
- Rainbow Pattern can be aimed at bare floor; the saves come at each
  creature's turn start, not on a step.
- A web square can be unmarked by `movestate.py` and still roll the save.

Water: none new (`fountain-user`, as in ROUTE.md).
