## Learned

- With the game's F10 no longer moving a pinned member, the Loader's mend keeps its sequence
  through a pin and gives the walk when the pin is taken off and F10 pressed again (case 4): right,
  as the member is then free and its last step was the last thing its flags saw.
- An effect put in the table by `addeffect.py` with movement left leaves bit 0 set (the game's own
  pin, 786CBh, would clear it): F10_PIN reads the effect table, not the bit, so it holds either way.
- The name box shows the pin ("Paralyzed", "Stuck") in place of "Okay" at once.
