# The item box's "Hit -N" for weaker materials (2026-11-09)

The user's choice: a plain wooden, bone, stone or obsidian weapon's item box shows its to-hit
penalty under the damage line ("To Hit", then "or just Hit -2": `Hit -N`, as the box's other short
lines, "Hide +10", "Pick +5, Lock +5"). DSCLOG's IB_MATERIAL in PROBE_ITEM_BOX, after the charges.
