# The item box's "Hit -N" for weaker materials (2026-11-09)

The user's choice: a plain wooden, bone, stone or obsidian weapon's item box shows its to-hit
penalty under the damage line ("To Hit", then "or just Hit -2": `Hit -N`, as the box's other short
lines, "Hide +10", "Pick +5, Lock +5"). DSCLOG's IB_MATERIAL in PROBE_ITEM_BOX, after the charges.

Concept first (scratchpad mock-up from a real box, the game's font from RESOURCE.GFF via
`art.GameArt(...).font`, ink (65,24,8), rows 7 apart: name rows y 87/94, damage 101, the new row 108,
x 88; draw the ink pixels only, not the font's shadow pixels, or it comes out bold).

Check: `loadsave.sh pens-after-scar`, `buildcheck.py` OK; I, a member's portrait (20,20 / 20,62 /
20,104), right-click the missile slot (66,48), right hand (66,92), left hand (173,92); Escape closes
the box and the inventory together (img/2026-11-09-hit-line-boxes.png):

- Gerakis's Bone Long Sword: "Bone / Long Sword / 1D8-1 / Hit -1".
- Gerakis's Bone Gythka: "2 Handed Bone / Gythka / 2D4-1 / Hit -1" (the row beside INFO, inside the box).
- Leather Sling: no line (leather). Cermak's Wooden Quarterstaff: none (a staff: type +08h 80h).
- K'ratchek's Obsidian Chatkcha: none. Its type's +08h is 83h: the game exempts it (no damage off
  either: 1D6+2), so no line is right.
- The fourth portrait (20,146) didn't take the click (the boxes repeated Gerakis's).

Item types read from memory (`ITEM_TYPES_PTR`, 20 bytes each): the weapons are those up to 139 with
+09h 5; three 1d1 types (93, 107, 113) have +08h 40h, no material, which a first build took for wood:
fixed before this check (the test covers it).
