# Mines 3 and Yuan-ti shelves in play

The stock and revised comparisons use DOSBox and a private copy of the Roost save. Reyhan’s travel script brought the party to each room. The Mines revisits selected the room’s sector and its camera bounds. In the Yuan-ti tunnels the regular region script set the bounds. The stock control loaded the original figure pictures and placements through the Loader. The comparison images use the native 320 × 200 frames. The whole maps and the Shattered Lands reference are map renders.

## The orb shelf and the chemical shelf

The six orbs in picture 1140 each contain the same 100 opaque pixels as loose laboratory orb 1052. At the Tyr furniture scale their height fell from ten pixels to six or seven. The revised shelf removes four empty or supporting rows below the top board, three below the middle board and one under the bottom board. Its top moves down eight pixels. Its floor row stays in place and all six orbs match the loose orb pixel for pixel.

In Mines 3 sector 10, Look at the shelf and choose the green hand on the Bookcase card. The revised shelf asked “Touch one of the orbs?” After Yes, the selected party member heard a Mind Flayer’s thoughts. The next message in this run said that Jor’anis’s faction wanted the forge and its tunnel had been sealed. The stock control also produced the touch prompt and the Mind Flayer message. From (36, 70), selecting the bottom board opened the card. The bookshelf uses GPL 91 at 584. [Three heights at the same magnification](../../reports/ravager-furniture/mine-orb-shelf-live.png)

The chemical shelf in sector 4 also identifies as a Bookcase. Its green hand opens the list of actions in GPL 86 at 2853. Took Element Z9 and reopened “Which will you take?” The list now started with Extract 2A. In separate stock and mod visits, choosing to smash the chemicals produced the message that the party member smashed all the vials. Both games still drew the stocked artwork immediately afterward. ## The northeast Yuan-ti storeroom

The room is behind the eastern map boundary on a fresh revisit. For the comparison, set story flags 118 and 119 in the private travel fixture before jumping to (115, 6). GPL 244 then kept the eastern bound at column 125 instead of 97. This reaches jars and sacks 9832 at (1890, 49), picture 3554, and barrels and jars 9839 at (1885, 144), picture 3555. Both already have redrawn art. Both matched their respective stock and mod bitmaps on every opaque pixel in DOSBox. No further height change was needed. These visits tested the fixtures independently of the story route. No drinking-water use was tested.
