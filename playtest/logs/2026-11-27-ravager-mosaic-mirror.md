# Completing the Mosaic museum mirror

Used the Loader from `735ce1fa` and the private Roost travel fixture in DOSBox. The first visit confirmed the coin and hand-mirror offerings. Reloaded the Roost and solved the entire puzzle in a second visit. No puzzle flags or items were edited, and the game was closed without saving.

1. Jumped to Mosaic at column 40, row 34. Picked up the cloth beneath the bird, the gold hand mirror and the bag of stone coins. The bag’s pickup button is the middle icon. Stored the three items in the backpack.
2. Dropped the offerings on clear floor below the stands, picked them up with Look and applied each to its bust. Dropping the cloth behind the pedestal in the first visit had left only a sliver visible. Tamind wears the green cloth, Finali displays her hand mirror, and a message confirms Kaldumeir’s coins disappear into the slot in his head.
3. Moved Fletch’s bookcase, placed the book on Biblid, left Terris alone, broke Khildril, removed Horace and Ellith, placed Horace on Ellith’s stand and stored Ellith in the backpack.
4. Picked up all eight glass shards individually and clicked the wall mirror with each. The game acknowledged each shard and reported 3,000 experience points per party member. The cracked pane remained through seven. The eighth produced a complete reflective pane in the same frame. Walked up through it into Veiled Alliance headquarters. The arrival screen matches the headquarters map around camera origin (688, 256), with a red hide rug and display shelves.
5. No water source was identified during this visit. The private travel fixture bypassed the story route into the museum.
