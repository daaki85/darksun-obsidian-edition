# Casting Restore Youth and Whirlwind in Ravager

In an isolated after-ambush save, a level 18 air cleric selected Restore Youth from the sixth-level menu. Terrannus had one added magical year. His system shock roll was 94 against 99 at Constitution 18, and his age returned from 19 to 18. The game was closed without saving.

Whirlwind rolled 4 + 3. Thy’rokh rolled a natural 1 on the save and lost 7 HP, going from 75 to 68. A pedestrian failed a separate save and lost 16 HP. The first build drew one funnel per affected square. The revised draw hook retains the centremost copy, leaving the area membership intact. The new area occupies slot 125 in a relocated copy of the native area table. Its spell number was 326 in the running game.

In the opening Draxan fight, Whirlwind rolled 5 on the cast. Thy’rokh saved with 17 against 13 and took 2 damage. The following passes rolled 7, 9 and 7. He failed each save, and the game removed exactly that much HP before the next initiative order. The playtest raised the caster’s level and the test party’s HP, and closed the game without saving. DSCLOG2 occupied 56,448 upper-memory bytes, leaving a largest free block of 99,008 bytes.

Replayed the assembled draw hook against the earlier cast’s native draw list. Of 45 entries for the area object, 44 are ground copies and one is an off-area placeholder. The draw hook kept one centre copy and skipped the other 44 entries. Two emulated round passes using the captured membership list called the damage routine once each for creature 2 and creature 16 at caster level 18.

On the final draw build, an after-ambush cast displayed one dust funnel above the affected ground. A second cast cycled through native frames 0, 1, 2 and 3, and the sampled screen images changed within the funnel. Thy’rokh failed the save and lost 8 HP. A separate startup with New spells disabled loaded the same save with the original area selector at load segment +41F1h and the original table length of 08BEh. With the patch enabled the length was 08DCh.
