# The journal, seen in the game (2026-11-16)

Under test: the Loader's journal (`dsloader/journal.py`): what is said goes to
`Journal/journal.jsonl`, and the newest entry's id is kept in the game's global numbers 198 and
199 so a save loaded shows its own journal. DOSBox 0.74-3 on Linux, the dice log without the
window (`start.sh`), save `pens-after-arena`.

## Before the code: are the numbers there?

- Global numbers 137-199 are zero in all 15 saves of `playtest/saves`; the highest in use is 136.
- In the pens, out of a fight, the numbers' pointer (DS:1356h) was set in 20 reads of 20.
- 1234h and 56h written to 198 and 199 read back; number 12 (the arena count) and 136 untouched.
  The save screen was opened and a name typed, but **the save itself was not made**: saving after
  a memory edit is against the kit's rule, and the step was refused. The numbers were put back to
  0 and the screen left by EXIT (253, 66).

## With the journal

1. `loadsave.sh pens-after-arena`, `follow.py 29`: Kurzak's two speeches at his stop are entries
   1 and 2 (clock 1296, region 29h, portrait 5), and the game's numbers hold 2.
2. A copy of the save's file with 1 in number 198 (made in the work folder, not in the game),
   loaded: the game's numbers read 1 after the load, so **a load puts the save's numbers back**,
   and the Loader left it at 1 (the journal now ends at entry 1).
3. `follow.py 29` again: the speeches are entries 3 and 4, entry 3's parent 1: a branch from the
   loaded save. The numbers hold 4, and the journal for id 4 is entries 1, 3, 4.

No error in the dice log.
