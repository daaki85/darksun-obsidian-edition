# Middle-button map dragging in Wake of the Ravager

Loaded the mod in DOSBox with upper memory enabled. DSCLOG2 occupied 50,832 bytes. The largest free upper-memory block was 104,624 bytes.

Loaded the ravager-after-ambush save and measured the view with ravtab.py. Holding the middle button and moving the pointer from (252, 47) to (172, 87) moved the top left of the view from (1080, 1280) to (1160, 1240). The helper counted one pan. Both Loader pan counters stayed at zero.
