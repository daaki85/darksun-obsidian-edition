# Mosaic museum in Wake of the Ravager

Ran the furniture branch at `4e10d128` through the Loader in DOSBox on a private copy of the game. Used the Roost save and the Reyhan travel fixture. After the jump, all interactions below used game controls. The party entered with its Roost story flags. The session ended without saving.

1. The first jump to Mosaic (region 03Ch, column 39, row 13) put the leader north of the dividing wall. A southern display returned “NO LINE OF SIGHT”. Reloaded the Roost via F2 and Return and used column 39, row 24 instead. This placed the leader between the two interactive rows.
2. Used Look on Horace, the far-left bust in the lower row. Dismissed his name, selected the green hand on the Bust card and answered Yes. His stand became empty and a separate bust appeared near the leader. Repeated the interaction on Ellith at the far right.
3. Look identified Khildril, third from the left in the lower row. Clicking with the sword broke him into pieces on his stand. Picked up Horace with Look, clicked Ellith’s empty stand and moved the pointer away. Horace stayed on the destination pedestal.
4. Lifted the bookcase beside Fletch and set it on clear floor. The red book appeared beside Fletch’s chair in the space the bookcase had covered. Picked up the book and applied it to Biblid at the upper left. The game drew the open pages on the front of his pedestal.
5. Picked up Ellith’s detached bust and pressed I. Dropped her in the first backpack slot and moved the pointer away to verify that she stayed in the slot. No water source was identified during the museum visit.
