# Play test: Color Spray, Hold Undead and a classless monster's level for saves (2026-11-08)

Under test: three new patches under "Spells as the manual describes them" (`spells_manual`, on by
default): `classless_save_level` (79D8Ch, DSCLOG's HD_SAVE_SITE: a creature with no class has its
hit dice, sheet +24h, as its level in the save modifiers), `color_spray_level` (79F83h,
SPRAY_SITE: Color Spray, spell 4, -100 on the save of a target of the caster's level or lower) and
`hold_undead_level` (7A345h, HOLD_HD_SITE: Hold Undead, spell 31, has no effect on undead of more
levels than the caster's). DOSBox, the Loader's patched game, dice log `$PLAYTEST_WORK/dice.txt`.

## Summary

| Case | Expected | Seen (dice log) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Color Spray (caster 9) on the Templar, level 4 | no save (a natural 20 still saves) | `d20 = 20 (natural 20) -> saved`, then on the next cast `none allowed (cannot save) -> failed` (twice) | OK |

| Hold Undead on the same undead Guard, fighter 5 | a save, then held | `Guard's saving throw vs Hold Undead from Lex (spell): d20 = 12, needs 16 (25% to save) -> failed` / `Lex gives Paralyzed to Guard` | OK |

## Steps

- Build check: `dos/DSUNLOG.EXE` (the Loader's patched copy) has `cd 98 7a` at 79D8Ch, `cd 98 7b 90
  90 90` at 79F83h, `cd 98 7c 90 90` at 7A345h (and CHAOS_SITE's `cd 98 79` at 79F80h). DSCLOG in
  memory at C91B0h: the new code's 40 bytes (HD_SAVE's `les bx,[bp-0Eh] / cmp byte
  [es:bx+21h],0`, image offset 8328h) equal the built EXE's; INT 98h's vector C91B:4A0F.
- `loadsave.sh fountain-user`, the speech clicked away: Pehtucl's ambush, fight on, Lex's turn.
  `spellsetup.py 0=9 hp=200 4 31`: Lex (preserver, his second class) at 9, Color Spray and Hold
  Undead taught, slots filled. `hp.py 150` for the foes.
- Color Spray is the first icon of Lex's level-1 page (176,59; the page is the USE screen's last).
  Its aiming pointer is the ring of beads; a click on a figure casts. It can catch more than the
  figure clicked: a click low on the Guard (190,161) sprayed Jenna beside him too.
- The Arena Guards' arrows kept hitting Lex before his turn, so his icons were crossed out two turns
  running: the Arena Guards were killed in memory (`aging.kill`, by name; never saved) and the
  Templar and a Guard, both in melee with Jenna, were left.
- One cast logged nothing at all but took a slot (a click at 213,124 near the Templar): the click
  had missed the figure, it seems. Aim from a zoomed shot (`convert -crop ... -scale 300%`).
- Levels and classes were set in memory per creature sheet (race +18h, classes +21h, levels
  +24h); the three Guards (creatures 8, 9, 31) share one sheet (15): what is written for one is
  written for all of a kind.
- Hold Undead is the only icon on Lex's level-3 page (LEVEL at 196,171 steps the page; slow
  clicks, the quick double click stepped once). Its pointer is the spell's icon (no ring). A cast
  gave **two** targets' worth: after the first click the pointer stayed for another, and the second
  click on the same figure logged a second "lasts" line; then the turn ended. The "lasts" line comes
  with each landing, immune or not: an immune target has that line and nothing more.

## Not done

- No real classless monster (an elemental, a slaad) was reachable from the saves: the Guards' sheet
  was made classless in memory instead. No real undead either (the pens' Zombie, creature 21, is at
  1496,472, far from the ambush): the Guards' race was set to 9 in memory.
- The switch off (`spells_manual` false) was not played: the unit tests check the three patches are
  the switch's (gamepatch.SPELLS_MANUAL) and their bytes in DSUN.EXE.
