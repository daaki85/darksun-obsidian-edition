# 2026-11-05: the mod's sounds for hiding and picking pockets

**Under test:** the two sounds the user picked are in the Loader's RESOURCE.GFF and play in the
game:
- BVOC 140, `hide.wav`: a breath sinking away.
- BVOC 141, `pick.wav`: a purse's jingle.

**Setup:**
- Loaded `pens-after-scar` with DOSBox's sound written to a file (`PT_AUDIO`; start.sh's disk
  driver, 16-bit stereo at 44100 Hz).
- Checked that `obsidian-edition/dos/RESOURCE.GFF` has BVOC 140 (6,033 bytes) and 141 (3,233).

**Seen:**
1. **First build, the sounds asked for by their numbers.** The helper took both requests
   (`playsound.py 140`, `141`), but the recording stayed silent. The game's own 53 and 96, asked
   for the same way, did record (peaks about 2,800).
   - The cause is the game's table of sounds, at load segment + 3A98h:08A2h. Entries 0-130 are
     digital (kind 5, with a descriptor naming the BVOC). 131-180 are another kind (4), so 140
     and 141 were never looked up as clips.
2. **Second build: the helper lends itself entry 131 for the call.** The hide sound matched its
   clip with r 0.997. The purse sound then played the hide sound again: the game keeps the last
   clip loaded under a number.
3. **Third build: entry 131 for one sound, 132 for the other.** The order 140, 141, 53, 141, 140
   gave:
   - hide r 0.997 each time;
   - purse r 0.995 each time;
   - the game's 53 unchanged.
   Afterwards the table's entries 131-133 were as the game had them (kind 4).

**Not yet seen in play:** the sounds from their real triggers.
- A pocket picked: no one was in line of sight from the save, and the way into the pens' hall
  was blocked by a figure standing in front of the doorway (route.py won't path through one).
- A thief hiding in a fight.

Both triggers are covered by tests, and will be checked in the full play test with the hidden
figure's look.

**Kit lessons (in PLAYTESTING.md):** `PT_AUDIO`, `playsound.py`, `soundmatch.py`; the game's table
of sounds; route.py and figures.
