# Research recipes: memory, sounds, pictures, timing, scripts and other targets

Part of the playtest guide ([PLAYTESTING.md](PLAYTESTING.md): read its startup procedure before any
in-game test). These recipes are for writing the game's memory for a test and for finding out how
the game works rather than for checking a change. They are split by subject; this page lists them.
Tools: [TOOLS.md](TOOLS.md).

| Page | What it covers. |
|---|---|
| [RESEARCH-MEMORY.md](RESEARCH-MEMORY.md) | The game's records (the party, items, fights and foes, spells and powers, creation and levels) and how to read and write them in memory for a test. |
| [RESEARCH-PICTURES.md](RESEARCH-PICTURES.md) | The game's sounds, the see-through figures, the picture store, frame rate and cycles, the picture and the music device, and the figure prototypes in the game. |
| [RESEARCH-CODE.md](RESEARCH-CODE.md) | The game's scripts and code, the unchanged game, DOSBox-X, and the recompiled program as the target. |
| [RESEARCH-RAVAGER.md](RESEARCH-RAVAGER.md) | Setting up, starting and driving the sequel, its rules as the port found them, and its dice log. |
| [RESEARCH-RAVAGER-SCREENS.md](RESEARCH-RAVAGER-SCREENS.md) | The sequel's picture cache, figures, shadows and shine, what's on screen where, its fights and spells, its screens, item boxes, item types and weapons, its map and its combat log's panel. |
| [RESEARCH-RAVAGER-RECORDS.md](RESEARCH-RAVAGER-RECORDS.md) | The sequel's transfer program and the Loader's transfer, its 48h sheet, the TOC the Loader writes, its AC and save modifiers, the kits, STORE and RECALL, saves, looting and keys, weapon specs, the starting gear, picking pockets, spell arrows and item weights. |
| [RESEARCH-RAVAGER-AUDIT.md](RESEARCH-RAVAGER-AUDIT.md) | The port audit's dated notes (its rest, a control run, its leftovers, the final items, the Effects screen's other lines), the sequel's spells in a fight and its save modifiers and THAC0 line in the dice log. |
