Obsidian Edition 2.10.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Nineteen of the game's magic items now shimmer in their icons, the Weapon Merchant and Jark are back in their markets, and the dice log shows the checks the game's scripts roll for themselves and each shot at the Bowyer's archery target. It is pull requests #64 to #67.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.10.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.9.0

**New**
- **Nineteen of the game's magic items shimmer in their icons**, as its other magic items do: the Parting Staff, the Chameleon Gloves, the Hornblade, Cahulaks +1, Dag's Dagger, the Terror Blade, Blackmace, the Shimmer, Drake and Silk Armor, Tanelyv's and Grey's arm and leg pieces, the Living Cloak, the Golden Torque, Light of Dawn, El's Ring and the Orb of Knowledge. Cahulaks +1, Light of Dawn and El's Ring had the same icon as plain items, so they now have icons of their own.
- **The scripts' own ability checks in the dice log**: when a talk or a place rolls against an ability itself, rather than with the game's ability check, the log shows the roll, the score and the result (`Daaki CHA check: 0-19 = 15, always made (CHA 20) -> success`). Chahl's CHA, breaking chains with STR and finding secret doors with INT are among the 13.
- **Each shot at the Bowyer's archery target in the dice log**: the roll, what it needed (DEX less half the distance), how many squares the archer is from the target and from the Bowyer, and the result: a miss, the outer or inner ring, or the centre, and whether the centre earns his 6 Arrows +2 (from more than 13 squares, with the Bowyer within 25, once).

**Fixed**
- **The Weapon Merchant in Teaquetzl's market and Jark in Tyr's were missing** (since 1.2.0): with the weapons the mod gives them, they had more items than the game could read, and it left them out. They're back, in a save already there too.
- **Dunesong**, the bow +1 the Bowyer sells, now counts at his archery target. Its script knew only the game's three bows, so a shot with Dunesong never counted.

## Your saved games

Everything works from the moment a save is loaded: the icons, the dice log's new lines, Dunesong at the target, and the Weapon Merchant and Jark, even in a save already in their market.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
