Obsidian Edition 2.13.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Characters have ages, Haste ages whoever it speeds, a new 6th-level priest spell gives the years back, and Enlarge and CON work as in AD&D.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.13.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.12.5

**New**
- **Ages** (a rule change, *Ages and magical aging*, on unless unticked). Each character has an age from the Dark Sun rules' tables, rolled the first time the Loader sees them (the dice log shows it). Middle, old and venerable age change the scores, and a character dies at the most their race lives. Only magic ages anyone. The age shows on the inventory screen, under the saving throws, and on the Loader's Characters tab with the next age to come.
- **Haste ages its target**, as the AD&D spell does: each creature it speeds rolls system shock by CON (85% to survive at CON 13, 99% from 18) and dies if it fails; a party member who survives is a year older.
- **Restore Youth**, a new 6th-level priest spell of the cosmos (druids and Healer clerics): takes back a year of magical aging a caster level, never below the character's true age, after a system shock roll. It has an icon of its own, a fountain on light blue water. That makes ten 6th-level priest spells.

**Changed**
- **Enlarge multiplies the weapon's dice only** (a rule change, on unless unticked): 10% a caster level, rounded up, with STR and magic added after, as in AD&D. The game made the whole melee damage bigger, STR included. The Effects screen says ENLARGED (+90% DICE).
- **CON as in AD&D** (a rule change, on unless unticked): CON 20 and up gives a hit point back an hour at 20 to one every 10 minutes at 25, where the game gave one every 5 minutes at any of them; and a dwarf's or a halfling's poison save gets only its racial bonus, not CON's adjustment as well.
- **The inventory screen's panel**: THAC0 and the saves sit a little higher, with the age below them.

**Fixed**
- A Haste already on a character in a loaded save no longer rolls system shock again.
- With the recompiled game, an ordinary exit is no longer reported as a crash.
- A `settings.json` saved by Notepad (with a byte-order mark) is read, not put aside as unreadable.

## Your saved games

Older saves load as they are. With ages on, each character in one, the game's own party too, is given a starting age the first time the Loader sees them.

**A save made with 2.13.0 is not for earlier versions:** it keeps the weapon specs packed with the age beside them, which an older Loader reads as other specs.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own. Restore Youth's icon is the mod's either way.
- Restore Youth, like the other new spells, comes with the **New wizard spells** option (in the Options tab's New content), and its years with **Ages and magical aging**.
