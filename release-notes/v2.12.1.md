Obsidian Edition 2.12.1: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). A fix release for 2.12.0: the Loader keeps its settings and keeps running when something goes wrong, plus a few smaller fixes.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.12.1.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.12.0

**Fixed**
- **Your options are never lost.** The Loader saves its settings whole or not at all. If the file was ever damaged, it is kept as `settings.json.bad` instead of being written over.
- **The dice log and party table keep going.** An unexpected error no longer stops them for the rest of the session. It is written once in the dice log, and they carry on.
- **DOSBox is never left paused** if the Loader's check of the party's items fails partway.
- **No slow build-up over a long session.** Each reconnect or restart of the game no longer leaves a process handle or a mouse hook behind.
- **The in-game combat log** shows its short line for an attack that hits on anything but a 1.
- **Dispel Magic** now weighs the 6th-level priest spells by their caster's level in the dice log.
- **A Spellarcher in armour** can't weave spell arrows, just as it can't cast.
- **Walking with the whole party in view is no longer taken for a fight.** Since 2.12.0 the party walks spread out after loads and fights, and the Loader read that as a fight. It then left people whose scripted walk had stopped standing still, kept the Battle Dancer's dances and Wild Fighting going after a fight, and rolled for defilers' scrolls outside fights. It now uses the game's own test for a fight.
- **Defilers' scrolls in every fight.** A later defiler in the same place under the same name, as in the arena's repeat fights, now gets its own chance of a scroll.

**Documentation**
- The player's guide and features page give a psionicist's weapons and shields correctly. A psionicist alone uses daggers, bone and obsidian short swords, clubs, chatkchas and bows, and no shield. With another class, it can also use the maces, metal short sword and slings that class allows, and a leather shield if that class is fighter, gladiator, ranger or cleric. 2.12.0 left out maces and slings for every psionicist, and gave a psionicist alone a leather shield.
- Thief shields are described exactly: leather only, and for a thief with another class, only if that class is fighter, gladiator, ranger or cleric.
- The README says what a crash or item report holds, and suggests reading it before posting it.

## Your saved games

Everything here works in older saves, from the moment a save is loaded.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
