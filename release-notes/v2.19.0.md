Obsidian Edition 2.19.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). The chance to hit shown before the swing; the squares a character can still walk to marked in a fight; quick saves and autosaves; a journal of everything said, with the quests; Alt to show what can be used; a choice of picture and music device; fewer switches; a thief's attacks after a backstab mended; and the fixes of two code audits.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.19.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: copy `settings.json` (your options) and the `Named saves` folder (your saves' named copies, and any `Replaced -` copies a restore kept) from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, or its `cloud_saves` folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.18.0

**In a fight**
- **The chance to hit, before the swing.** Choose an enemy with Tab and the dice log and the combat log's header say what the d20 must show and the chance (`needs 14+ (35%)`), for each weapon where they differ and from behind where the walk may end there. Rest the pointer on an enemy (the walk arrow or the sword, not the eye) and the number floats over it.
- **Squares in reach.** On a party member's turn a small cross marks every square one click can still walk to, counted as the game counts (10 a straight step, 14 a diagonal one). **R** hides the crosses and shows them again, and the choice is kept.
- **F10 takes a whole move back.** F10 is the game's own key to take a move back; after the last step it used to put the character back unable to walk. Now the character can walk again. Never after an attack.
- **The combat log's panel** is put away while a window, a screen or a menu is up (it used to cover the save and load windows), and comes back with the map.

**Saves**
- **F5** saves at once, with no window and no name to type; **F9** opens the load window on those saves, the newest chosen. The game also **saves by itself** a few seconds after the party comes into another area and after a fight.
- They are on a fifth page of the save and load window, **QUICK**: five quick saves and five autosaves, the oldest of each written over. Nothing is saved in a fight. A save you want to keep belongs on pages 1 to 4.

**On the map**
- **Hold Alt**, out of a fight: a gold ring under every door, chest, bag, body, item and person in view that a click does something with, and the names of the four nearest the pointer.

**The Loader**
- **The Journal tab**: every talk with the reply you chose, every reward and message, under the game's day and area. It is kept on disk and follows your saves: load an older save and the journal is as it was then.
- **Quests**, on the same tab: the open ones with what the party was told, the finished ones with how they ended. The game keeps no quest list; the Loader reads the story's own flags.
- **Picture and music** (Options, from the next start): Plain, Scanlines, TV or Sharp for the game's picture in a Double window or larger and in full screen; and the music sent to any of Windows' MIDI devices (a soundfont synthesizer or an MT-32 driver you have installed: none comes with the mod).
- **Fewer switches.** Most of the mod is always on now: the combat log, the rule changes but three (hit dice, half-giants' weapons, helms), weapon specialization, the AD&D thief skills, hiding and backstabs, the shops, the graphics and the controls. An older `settings.json` with one of them unticked has it on all the same. What keeps a switch: the new content, kits, starting gear, and this release's additions (under **In the game** and **Saves**). What this means for a game you were playing with some of them off is under [Your saved games](#your-saved-games).
- **Crash reports** keep the newest 20 freeze reports; older ones are deleted.
- The window's six tabs show their names whole.

**Items and the arena**
- **Magic items glow in their own colours**: Greenbright green, Mindshard violet, the water's and the air's weapons blue and white; the rest keep the fire.
- **The Flame Blade is the Searing Blade**, an obsidian short sword +2 (it was a long sword +1); one in a saved game is remade as you play.
- **The champions' bout**: the elf's bow is a Fine Bow and its Chill Arrows chill what they hit; the halfling has a staff sling; the defiler wears Bracers of Defense AC 6.
- Spell arrows' INFO says SPELLARCHERS: where it said USEABLE BY:.
- Tanelyv's chest piece shines as its arm and leg pieces do.

**Fixed**
- **A thief's later attacks after a backstab.** When a hidden thief backstabbed with the first weapon, the rest of that round's swings were 2 worse to hit than they should have been, yet still counted as from behind (the target's DEX and shield ignored). Each swing is now judged on its own: a plain swing, or from behind if the thief really is behind the target.
- The dice log could give a character being made an age of its own after each roll on the creation screen; it now gives the screen's **AGE**, once, when you press DONE.
- On Linux, the Loader's window kept saying "Connected to the game" after a game it hadn't started was closed; it now says the game has ended.
- A magical item sold while the Loader's window was closed was lost; it is kept back for the merchant now.
- Negative Plane Protection's save wrote past the new spells' records.
- The Elven Leader's Gythka +1 was drawn as Kreenfang.
- A second new character made in one sitting could miss its starting gear.
- On Windows, a file held for a moment (a virus scan) could start the game without the dice log.
- The elven slavers' leader's Goodbye arrested the party.
- The helper that runs beside the game leaves interrupts as the game had them, turns the A20 line on again if it finds it off, and keeps a wider margin from DOSBox's own tables in upper memory.

Smaller fixes, and the documentation's corrections, are in the [changelog](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.19.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## What was checked, and what was not

Played in DOSBox 0.74-3 on Linux, in the slave pens and the arena, with the game's own party: the chance to hit against the swings that followed, the squares against a walk a square, R, F10 (with movement left, after the last step, after a kill), the panel under the load window and a character's END menu, quick saves and autosaves loaded from a fresh start and played on, Alt's rings in the pens and on the champions' bodies in the arena, the journal, and the quests' list read from a running game. After the last fixes were merged in, all of that was played again on the final build, with a hidden thief's backstab and the plain swings after it, a barred item, Kalzith's shop, a rest, the creation screen's age, and the Loader's window noticing a game closed under it.

**Not seen, so worth a look when you play:**
- **Windows itself.** Every check was on Linux. The **picture** choices were seen there only (GOG's own display driver with Scanlines and TV, and full screen, were not), and the **music device was not heard at all**.
- **The Loader's tab names** were whole on Linux before the change too; the cut names were on Windows.
- **Regions past the pens and the arena**: the squares' and Alt's marks on other floors, the quests given there, the names of their areas in the journal.
- **Quests** finished by a reward's message alone, and two that hang on a number of the story's (Notaku's, Katti's), were not played through.
- The game's camp with F9, a full disk, and GOG's `cloud_saves` folder with the quick saves.
- A hasted character's farthest squares lie outside the marks (32 squares a side), and behind a wall the farthest cross can take two clicks.

If something is off, the Loader's **Freeze report** and its crash reports are the quickest way to tell us.

## Your saved games

Saves from 2.18.0 and earlier load and play as they are. The journal starts from the moment you load one.

**If you played with some switches off.** The switches that are gone are on from the moment you load, for every character:
- **Weapon specialization**: a warrior who has no weapon specs yet attacks once a round with most weapons until it picks them, which it does at its next level, on the same window as at creation.
- **The AD&D thief skills**: a thief's skills are AD&D's table by level with Dark Sun's race and DEX adjustments, none above 95; hiding in shadows and moving silently work in a fight, and a backstab counts only on the round's first attack.
- **The other rules** (saving throws, class restrictions, multiclass hit points, the class tables, ages, resting, the shop prices and the rest) apply from then on; what already happened (a level gained, an item bought or sold) stays as it was.
- The **items changed** in this release (Greenbright's green glow, the Searing Blade, the champions' gear) show up in a saved game too: a Flame Blade the party has becomes the Searing Blade as you play, and the arena's champions bring their new gear to any game that hasn't fought them yet. A Flame Blade in a save becomes the Searing Blade. The QUICK page's saves (`SAVQ01.SAV` to `SAVQ10.SAV`) are saves like any other, but the game started without the Loader doesn't list them. A save made with 2.19.0 was not tried in 2.18.0: once you have saved with 2.19.0, stay with it.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Optional: Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
