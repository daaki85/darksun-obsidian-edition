Obsidian Edition 2.13.1: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Characters age with the game's clock and grey on the map as they do, the combat log shows how long you've played, Kurzak notices when Legcrusher is gone, and RECALL on the creation screen no longer stops the game.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.13.1.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.13.0

**New**
- **Natural aging.** With *Ages and magical aging* on, everyone is a year older each 365 days of game time, and the dice log says so (`A year has passed: Cermak is 23.`). The age categories and the most a race lives count as they do for magic. The years counted are kept in the save, so none is counted twice or missed; one that passes in the arena is counted as the party leaves it. It is flavour: a party that rests 5000 times is only about 5 to 9 years older.
- **Grey hair on the map.** A party member's hair turns as they age: iron grey streaks when middle-aged, grey when old, white when venerable, in each figure's own shades (with *What the party wears* on). Dwarves and muls are hairless on Athas, and thri-kreen keep their looks.
- **The age on the creation screen**, above RECALL: the age the character will start at, from its name and race. The inventory screen's line now reads `AGE: 23` too.
- **How long you've played**, on the combat log panel's top line out of a fight: `Day 47, 14:20`, from day 1 at 00:00 (the game has no time of day). In the arena it shows the count of arena fights instead, once the game has shown it.
- **The dice log, line by line**, a page of its own (`DICE-LOG.md`): every kind of line the dice log writes, and what it means.

**Fixed**
- **RECALL on the creation screen stopped the game** ("DynrecCore: illegal option") when pressed before the die had been rolled on that visit to the screen. It happened since 2.8.0.
- **Kurzak notices Legcrusher is gone.** Once the party has killed Legcrusher (at the fountain, with no alarm), Kurzak no longer calls for him when they're late for a fight, nor greets them as if he'd brought them, nor calls him good with monsters when asked who the half-giant is; he wonders where his monster trainer has got to. Dinos no longer warns that Kurzak will send him out.
- The Loader no longer stalls on an empty floppy drive or a sleeping network drive while it looks for the game or Wake of the Ravager, and says so on its status line when its settings can't be saved, rather than losing the choice.
- The game window box shows *Single (320x240)* for a window at its own size; it said *Triple*.

## Your saved games

Older saves load as they are. In a save from before 2.13.1, natural aging starts counting from where its clock stands, aging no one for the time before. A character's true age (how young Restore Youth can make them) is now worked out the same way the creation screen shows it; an age already given stays as it is.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
