Templar's Ledger 1.1.0, the second release: a companion and mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). It is pull requests #14 to #18.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Templars-Ledger-1.1.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating from 1.0.0: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from the old `Templars-Ledger-1.0.0` folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Templar's Ledger.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Ledger's **Options** tab, pick the rule changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Ledger follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Ledger runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder. Saves on pages 2 to 4 are files of their own in the game folder, beside the game's (`SAVB`, `SAVC` and `SAVD`), and characters 20 to 29 are in the game's own `CHARSAVE.GFF`.

## What's new since 1.0.0

- **New content:**
  - **Kalzith,** a defiler slave in the slave pens who, treated with respect, sells arcane spell scrolls at the game's own prices.
  - **Semyon kept his word:** after he leaves the arena he is in the slave pens to talk to, and if he is beside the party when they break out with Scar, he breaks out with them.
  - **Kreenfang and Shadowseeker,** two named magic weapons (Shadowseeker lets its wielder see the invisible), 200 XP for lifting Shadowseeker from Kurzak, and Alagorn tells the story of each.
  - Dinos and the Trustee know about the new people; the cooked vulture is part of Dinos's talk, with the game's quest sound; finding the Ring +1 is worth 50 XP.
  - Each addition can be switched off on the Options tab.
- **Rule changes**, each switchable:
  - items saving against acid by material as in AD&D;
  - rings and cloaks of protection as in AD&D;
  - cloaks and boots help thieves and rangers hide and move silently, and a worn belt helps pick pockets and open locks (their item boxes say so).
- **On the screen:**
  - what the party wears, on their figures on the map, walking and fighting;
  - see-through shadows under every figure, and dust behind walking feet.
- **Controls:**
  - scroll the map with the mouse wheel, or press the wheel and drag;
  - in a fight, Tab chooses an enemy and Enter attacks it.
- **More saves and characters:** 40 saves on four pages of the save and load window, and 29 saved characters instead of 19.
- **Game speed** on the Options tab: DOSBox gets more of the computer for smoother walking; at the fastest, the whole party walks as fast as the leader alone.
- **The dice log:** searching junk piles, haystacks and wardrobes; items' checks against acid; hits that do less than rolled; regeneration.
- **The Look box:** a monster's alignment, and its magic resistance beside its level.
- **The Ledger:** the Options tab's sections open and close; the rule changes come with the ones that change play most first.
- **Crash reports:** if DOSBox crashes or the game stops with an error, the message stays on screen and what happened is saved in a file to send.
- **Fixed:**
  - a crash in the opening fight, and DOSBox crashes, garbled figures and freezes;
  - two of the game's own bugs: DELETE in the roster removed the wrong character when the list was scrolled down, and a new character's thief skills showed 0;
  - the Effects screen no longer ends a party member's spell when its icon is clicked;
  - and more, listed in the changelog.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
