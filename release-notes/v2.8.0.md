Obsidian Edition 2.8.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). The party's magic gear glows on their figures, the pointer works in the game's scenes and behaves like your desktop's, and the game has more memory to spare. It is pull requests #60 and #61.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.8.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.7.2

**New**
- **Magic gear glows on the party's figures**, as Kreenfang and the Flame Blade do: a weapon's bright edge, a shield's boss and rim, and sparks on armour, bracers, cloaks, helms, boots and belts, each in the animated colour nearest its icon's (fire, green, violet or blue). Boots of Elvenkind now show on the feet.
- **A pointer in the game's scenes** (the intro, its logos and the rest), and a click skips a scene as a key does.

**Changed**
- **The pointer** starts in the middle of the screen, stays where you leave it as screens change, moves as far up and down as across, and in a window as fast as your desktop's pointer. Placing items in the inventory is no longer fiddly.
- **A shorter pause in the inventory**: the pointer stands still for less time after you pick up or put down an item, so it jumps less far when it moves again.
- **No thief skill above 95** with **Thief skills from AD&D's table**, as in AD&D (a high-level thief's move silently could read 121).
- **More memory for the game**: its music buffer and part of its code now sit in upper memory, so long sessions are less likely to run out ("Out of memory in OEOpenGraph").

**Removed**
- The combat log's strip below the map (the panel over the map, shown with **L**, stays), scrolling by holding the right button (the mouse wheel's drag stays), and rings under every enemy (the one chosen with Tab keeps its ring), to free memory for the mod's helper inside the game.

**Fixed**
- In a big fight (the arena's), most figures lost their **shadows**. They keep them now.
- An archer's pointer showed "can't shoot" after a shot that killed, with shots left. Now it shows only once every shot of the round is made.
- The mod's own magic items glowed blue on figures whatever their colour: a bone dagger +1, red in the inventory, glowed blue on the map. They now glow in their icon's colour.
- A cloak seen from the front looked held in one hand on the elves and the half-elf man.
- Cermak's Arcanist spell slot was missing at the start of a new game until his first rest.
- The dice log no longer puts a regenerated hit point down to a spell or attack ("Regains 1 HP from Fire attack"), and no longer lists a dozen "a creature changes sides" lines as a fight starts.

## Your saved games

Every change works from the moment a save is loaded. Settings for the removed options are ignored.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
