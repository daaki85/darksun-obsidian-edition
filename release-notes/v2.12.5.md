Obsidian Edition 2.12.5: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Whirlwind works as its description says, and the Effects screen tells you more about Enlarge and the armour spells.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.12.5.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.12.4

**Fixed**
- **Whirlwind's swept creatures lose their next turn.** A creature swept off its feet still took the turn that swept it and lost the next round's instead, and the turn it lost spared it a round of damage. Now Whirlwind strikes everyone in it at the start of each round, before anyone acts, and those who fail their save lose that round's turn. As it is cast it strikes at once: a creature swept before its turn loses it there and then, one that has acted loses its next.

**New**
- **Enlarge's damage on the Effects screen.** With the pointer on Enlarge's icon: ENLARGED (+90% DAMAGE), 10% a level of its caster. (The game allows only one Enlarge on a creature; a second cast uses up the spell and adds nothing.)
- **The armour spells' AC on the Effects screen.** With the pointer on Barkskin's, Flesh Armor's or Magical Vestments' icon: the armour AC it gives by its caster's level, BARKSKIN (AC 3). DEX is still added to it, and armour already better is kept, so it also shows when the spell does nothing for that character.

## Your saved games

Everything here works in older saves, from the moment a save is loaded.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
- Whirlwind, like the other new spells, comes with the **New wizard spells** option (in the Options tab's New content).
