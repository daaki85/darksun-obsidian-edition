Obsidian Edition 2.6.0 (draft, in progress): a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). The Healer kit becomes the water cleric's healer, with the cures only druids had; the combat log shows your count of arena fights in the pens and the arena; a way for items to vanish as an area loads is closed; and seven more of the original game's bugs are fixed. It is pull request #52.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.6.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.5.0

**Added**
- **Your count of arena fights.** In the slave pens and the arena, between fights, the combat log panel's top line shows "Arena fights: N": the game's own count (one a trip into the arena, however many waves). In a fight it shows the round, and once the party escapes Draj it's gone.

**Changed**
- **The Healer has the sphere of the cosmos, and is a water cleric's only** (with kits on). In the game, Cure Serious and Critical Wounds, Raise Dead, Neutralize Poison, Free Action and the other common 4th and 5th-level priest spells are druids' alone, so a cleric never had them, and the Healer's extra healing on the two cures never came into play. A Healer now knows them from the start and casts them at its cleric level, its cures each healing 1 more a die. On the creation panel, mark WATER before opening KITS; after DUAL, only a new water cleric is offered the kit.

**Fixed**
- **Items could vanish if the Loader changed the party's gear just as an area loaded.** The game renumbers every item when you change areas, and a change of the Loader's (a ring found, a scroll bought from Kalzith, a pocket picked, a sold item kept for buyback) made at that moment could cut an item out of its list. The Loader now changes items only while the game is settled, holding the game still for the instant it takes.
- **A new character's gear could be fitted before its weapon specs were picked**, if the weapon window was left open a few seconds, leaving some (a Titan Mind) without a weapon.
- **A Poisoner's poison wasn't ready again in the arena's next wave**, and the wave's rounds went on counting from the last.
- **Item reports after loading a later save of the same party**, for items that save doesn't have; and a vanished item could go unreported when money came in at the same moment. Resting no longer pauses the watch: an item that goes while the party rests is reported.
- **Pick Pockets could lose an item** in a rare case where the thief's backpack couldn't take it.
- **The freeze report** listed only a few places after a long session.
- **More of the original game's bugs:** Negative Plane Protection never stopped anything (it now gives a save against an undead's draining touch); Cloak of Bravery's +4 against fear never applied (it does now, against Spook too); Absorb Disease gave the psionicist STR too; Bless and Aid left out the wrong allies; a berserk or confused party member could be left on the monsters' side; the fire elementals' resistances were the wrong way round; monsters cast their walls as a single point.

## Your saved games

A Healer from an earlier version keeps its sphere and is given the cosmos spells when the game is loaded: the dice log says so.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
