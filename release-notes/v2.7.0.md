Obsidian Edition 2.7.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Nine 6th-level priest spells, with pictures of their own; levels capped by XP instead of at 9th, so priests reach 11th and 12th; backstabs as AD&D has them; spell names floating over their casters; the Spellarcher's spell arrows; three new magic items; and eight more of the original game's bugs fixed, three script bugs in Darkhold, the Lava Rifts and Gedron among them. It is pull requests #55 and #56.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.7.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.6.0

**Added**
- **Nine 6th-level priest spells** (with the new spells and AD&D's tables switched on), for clerics and druids of 11th level and up: a 6th-level slot at 11th, two at 12th.
  - **Heal** and **Harm** (druids and Healer clerics): every wound healed, or a creature left 1d4 hit points with no save.
  - **Blade Barrier** (air) and **Wall of Thorns** (earth): walls of their own, 8d8 (a save for none) or 8 plus the creature's AC to anyone in them or passing through.
  - **Sunstroke** (fire): one creature collapses, helpless, for 2 rounds a level unless it saves, and burns for 1d4 each round.
  - **Fire Seeds** (fire): 8d8 in an area, a save for half.
  - **Heartseeker** (earth, water): save against death or die.
  - **Water of Life** (water): a creature's wounds healed by passing them to the caster.
  - **Whirlwind** (air): a turning funnel of dust for a round a caster level, 2d8 each round to those in it (a save for half), and one that fails loses its next turn; nothing can be shot or cast through it.

  Each has its own icon and description. If **Wake of the Ravager** (GOG's) is installed beside Shattered Lands, they use its icons and its pictures for the blades, the thorns and the fireball; without it they have the Loader's own.
- **Names over figures:** a spell's name floats over its caster as it is cast, and "Miss" over anyone an attack misses (an Options switch, on).
- **Spellarcher spell arrows:** outside a fight, a button on the USE screen for each touch spell the Spellarcher knows. One click turns a spell slot into 5 Burning, Chill, Shocking or Vampiric Arrows a preserver level, each casting its spell on what it hits. They fade at the next rest.
- **Three new magic items:** the **Girdle of Back Defense** (no bonus for attacking its wearer from behind, and no backstab), the **Golden Girdle of Urnst** (AC 3 better against long and short swords) and the **Amulet of Magic Resistance** (15%, and it never shatters).
- **The Crusader and the Mind Warrior** have expertise in one melee weapon spec of their choice, as the Battle Mage has.
- **The weapons spells make count for specialization:** the Flame Blade as a long sword, Shillelagh as a club, Spiritual Hammer as a mace.
- **Find** in the dice log and the dialogue (Ctrl+F).
- **More in the dice log:** why the game refused a spell you clicked (no line of sight, too far...), the protection that halved a spell's damage, the psionic defence against attack modes, a power check's roll, Detonate's blast on an item, and a party member's ability score changing.
- **How the game works** has tables of every cleric spell and every psionic power: what the manual says, what the game's code does, and what the log shows.

**Changed**
- **Levels are capped by XP (749,999) instead of at 9th**, shared out between a character's classes: one class goes as far as its next level needs no more (cleric 11th, druid 12th, fighter, gladiator and ranger 10th, preserver and psionicist 11th, thief 13th); two or three classes share it evenly.
- **A backstab multiplies only the weapon's dice**, with STR and magic added once after, as in AD&D (a switch, on). A strong thief no longer hits for over 100.
- **The Wanderer halves fire and cold spells' damage**, as Resist Fire and Resist Cold do; **the Myrmidon's initiative is 2 worse**.
- **Axes and maces on the party's figures** look like the game's own, with a wooden haft and a solid head.

**Fixed**
- **Acid Arrow's acid never burned after the hit.** Now it does 2d4 at the start of each round it lasts.
- **Three of the game's script bugs:** in Darkhold the portcullis guard stopped fighting back and three creatures lost their look text; the Lava Rifts ranger's trigger ran the hermit's talk (endless, with a second Iron Necklace); and Linara in Gedron ended her talk with no menu after the extract, so Jasmine's spellbook never came up.
- **A party's thief skill roll** (a trap going off, listening in by a wagon) always went to the first party member. Now the one with the best chance rolls.
- **Random monsters** came in numbers worked out from the wrong monster, and in some areas could crash the game ("Divide error").
- **Figures vanishing and then a freeze** in long sessions: a likely cause is gone (the shadows could have the game load a figure's picture in the middle of drawing).
- **Magic items didn't shine in a shop**, Kalzith's among them, and some of the Loader's magic items didn't shine at all.
- **An item the game destroys** (acid, Detonate's blast) now gets an item report.
- **A Fireball no longer floods the dice log.**

## Your saved games

Every fix and rule change works from the moment a save is loaded. The script fixes take effect when those scenes come up: a save already past one keeps what happened there.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Optional: Wake of the Ravager (GOG's), installed beside Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells.
