Obsidian Edition 2.13.4: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Fuller starting gear's armour now counts for AC, Psionic Blast's imagined wounds heal after the fight, and more of the game's own bugs are fixed, in its code and its scripts.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.13.4.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.13.3

**Changed**
- **The dice log names a thri-kreen's claw or its bite** (`with a claw (1d4)`, `with its bite (1d4+1)`), and **Erdlu Claw's talons are the claws alone**: a thri-kreen's four claws become 1d6+1, +1 to hit, as a +1 weapon; its bite stays as it is.
- With **Show unlabelled rolls**, the dice log gives each AC the game works out for an attack, and what it's made of (base, armour, DEX, the rest).

**Fixed: the Loader's own**
- **Fuller starting gear's armour gave no AC.** The leather armour (and a Stalker's, Assassin's or Shinobi's cloak) showed as worn but the game didn't count it: Gerakis was hit at AC 8 in place of 6. It counts now, and a save from an earlier version is put right as it loads (the dice log says "... is now counted as worn").
- A thri-kreen's bite got the claws' Erdlu Claw bonus, and a Slowed thief's skill chances in the Loader were wrong.
- Erdlu Claw's and the spell arrows' descriptions ran past the bottom of their box. They fit now.

**Fixed: the game's own bugs**
- **Psionic Blast** took a party member down to a fifth of their hit points for good, though the power's wounds are imagined. They come back as the fight ends (the dice log says so).
- **Items that raise or lower a score** (gauntlets, rings, a helm) worked from the backpack. Now only worn.
- **The gladiator's armour optimisation** (1 AC better every 5 levels) came with no armour on. Now only in armour, as Dark Sun has it.
- **A character's most PSP** took the wrong WIS bonus, and INT or WIS over 22 gave the wrong amount.
- **A fight's music** took its level from the wrong creature.
- **The game's scripts:** the slave pens' **sewer grate** (hacking the lock off was never offered; an attack always broke it), a **ranger's tracking** at the Wagon Train's bodies, the **Rebel Mindhome**'s 5000 XP given at every talk, **region 9's messenger**, the **Draj serf**'s second obelisk gem, and lines that took every active member for a man of no race (a halfling's greeting, a woman's, a thri-kreen's).

Found with the help of [yuan-ti](https://github.com/CalvinRodo/yuan-ti)'s and [OpenDS](https://github.com/VirInvictus/opends)'s notes, each checked in the game's own code.

## Your saved games

Saves from 2.13.3 load as they are, and so do 2.13.4's in 2.13.3. Armour the Loader gave in an earlier version is moved to where the game counts it, once, as the save loads.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Optional: Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
