Obsidian Edition 2.11.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). A rebalance of eight kits, so each one's gift and cost weigh more evenly. It is pull request #68.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.11.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.10.0

**Changed: kits**
- **Wilderness Avenger** (half-elf druid/fighter): +1 to hit and damage against anything, not only undead, and its Cause Light, Serious and Critical Wounds do 1 more a die (+1, +2, +3). Its cost is now **no healing spells**: it never knows Cure Light, Serious or Critical Wounds, Blood Flow, Heal or Water of Life.
- **Assassin**: a backstab one step deadlier than a thief's, ×3 at 1st-4th level up to ×6 from 13th.
- **Poisoner** (halfling fighter/thief): every melee weapon hit poisons (a save against paralysis/poison/death or 10 damage), not only the first of a fight; shots and throws don't. No −1 to hit any more; its weapon spec is the dagger or the short sword.
- **Rescuer** (half-elf cleric/ranger): its cures heal 1 more a die anywhere, not only under the open sky, and it has 2 more movement in a fight; it may no longer hold a shield.
- **Scholar** now has a spell slot more at each spell level; the **Arcanist** keeps its two spells a turn, for a d3 hit die.
- **Mind Warrior**: the psionicist's d6 hit die, not a d8; its tenth fewer PSP stays.
- **Healer**: after changing class it stays a water priest (a ranger it becomes is a water ranger), and a ranger of air, earth or fire can't take it.
- **Spellarcher**: spell arrows are woven only into an empty quiver, and stay there until shot or faded, as a Flame Blade stays in the hand.

## Your saved games

The kits' new rules work from the moment a save is loaded. A Wilderness Avenger in an older save loses its healing spells when the game is loaded, and the dice log says so.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
