Obsidian Edition 2.15.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Three new AD&D rules (held targets, creature size, and cahulaks as Dark Sun has them), spells, psionics and monsters as the manual describes them, new cahulaks to find, a better-armed Scar, and graphics that no longer vanish in a busy area.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.15.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.14.1

**New rules** (each a switch on the Options tab, on by default)
- **Held targets as in AD&D:** a blow on a held or sleeping creature hits automatically, for normal damage (the game rolled to hit and doubled the damage).
- **Creature size:** a large target (a half-giant, a large monster, anyone Enlarged) takes AD&D's damage for large targets (a long sword 1d12, a great axe 2d8, a gythka 1d10), and Hold Person and Charm Person don't work on it. A weapon's item box shows both damages.
- **Cahulaks as Dark Sun has them:** a head in each hand, so two attacks a round, and a hit can tangle the target.

**Rules closer to the manual**
- **Spells, psionics and monsters as the manual describes them,** with Turn Undead by AD&D's table; missile and hostile touch spells **roll to hit**.
- **Resting** heals by the priests' spells and natural healing; **dual-classing** follows the manual.
- **Every thri-kreen**, foes too, throws a chatkcha twice a round.
- **Air clerics may use cahulaks** (thrown weapons in Dark Sun, as daggers are in AD&D), and a Brute may dual-class to air cleric.

**New gear and people**
- **Boots of Speed**, and more cahulaks: a **Cahulaks +2**, **metal** and **obsidian** cahulaks in the shops (earth and fire clerics may use the obsidian ones, and an earth or fire cleric-warrior who picks the cahulaks starts with them), and two magic pairs to find: **Emberlash**, obsidian +1, and **Ironwhirl**, metal +2. Alagorn has a story for each.
- **Scar** fights with a pair of obsidian cahulaks and wears a full leather suit with a helm; his henchmen swing bone great axes and wear leather armour. The game's own **Polearm +1**, never placed before, is placed, and foes who fought with their fists are armed.

**The dice log, the panel and the Loader**
- The dice log has a readable layout, a summary as each fight ends, and Turn Undead's rolls.
- The combat log panel has a smaller font (8 lines docked, 14 expanded).
- The Loader has a **Help** button that opens the player's guide; Fastest is the default speed.
- Smoother play: steady pace without the choppy walking; shadows, dust and see-through figures cost less to draw.

**Fixed**
- **Graphics no longer disappear in a busy area:** the game's 300 picture slots are reused, and figures no longer vanish after many gear changes.
- **The game's own bugs:** Scare's save, "can't save" that saved, Pehtucl's ambush, missiles from behind, the Quicksilver Glove's material, Alagorn's stories, and more ([the game's bugs](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.15.0/obsidian-edition/GAME-BUGS.md)).
- **The Loader's and the helper's own,** from a full code audit, among them the Spellarcher's quiver lock and a settings file that could be overwritten with the defaults.
- Smaller fixes are in the [changelog](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.15.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Your saved games

Saves from 2.14.1 load as they are, and the new rules apply at once. Gear given to the game's people reaches only those the game makes from now on: in a game already past the arena's first bout, Scar and his henchmen keep their old gear, and an area already visited keeps what it had. A save made with 2.15.0 is for 2.15.0 and later: its new items (the cahulaks, the Boots of Speed) are ones 2.14.1 doesn't know.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Optional: Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
