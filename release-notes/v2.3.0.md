Obsidian Edition 2.3.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). A combat log in the game, three more wizard spells, two magic shields, defilers' scrolls, buying back magical items, saves kept under their names, fuller starting gear, and Templar's Ledger renamed the **Dark Sun Obsidian Edition Loader**. It is pull requests #44 to #46.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.3.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.2.1

**New**
- **A combat log in the game.** In a fight, a panel over the map's lower right shows every roll as it comes, in the game's own lettering: at the least, in short or in detail, with the round and who acts next on its top line. It scrolls back over the fight (its scrollbar, or the mouse wheel on Windows), collapses to one line or opens up with filters (All, Party, Damage, Saves), and **L** folds it away. On by default, settings from earlier versions included (once; after that your choice stands).
- **Three more wizard spells,** with the new spells on: **Reflected Image** (one image, struck instead of the caster half the time), **Spook** (one creature flees for 3 rounds unless it saves) and **Friends** (CHA + 2d4, for better prices). The Shinobi can learn them, and Fogbolt.
- **Two magic shields:** Gutterward, a wooden shield +1, and Ironbulwark, a metal shield +3, the game's first.
- **Defilers' scrolls:** a defiler in a fight now and then carries a scroll of a random preserver spell, found on its body.
- **Buying back magical items:** a magical item sold stays in the merchant's stock, to buy back.
- **Saves under their names:** the Loader keeps a copy of every save in its **Named saves** folder, named as you named it in the game, and can put one back in a slot.
- **Fuller starting gear:** new characters also get a weapon in hand they can use, a sling, leather armour where they may wear it, a belt, boots and a dagger (rangers and Stalkers a cloak). A multiclass preserver's armour starts in the backpack, so it can cast.

**Changed**
- **Templar's Ledger is now the Dark Sun Obsidian Edition Loader**, the program that starts the game with the mod. Copy `settings.json` from your old folder to keep your options, as for any update.
- **The leader's CHA at shops:** 5% off from CHA 18, 5% more for each point after, to 40% at 25; shops pay 60% when you sell.
- **An air cleric can use daggers,** as its sphere allows (none of the game's daggers listed the air cleric).
- **More kits that can't go together** (the Grove Warden and Ravager; the Stalker and thief; the kits that give a warrior's THAC0).
- **The bone and obsidian axes weigh 30:** either goes in the off hand, and both can backstab.
- **EMS off in the Loader's DOSBox settings,** for more room for the mod (the game uses XMS).

**Removed**
- **Each turn's rolls in the game's dialogue window:** the combat log shows them without stopping the game.

**Fixed**
- **Starting gear a character's classes can't use** is left behind (a fighter/druid's armour and shield).
- **New preservers weren't offered their starting spells** at DONE.
- **The creation panel:** stuck on the KITS page after adding a class; a kit lost on a new roll; the wrong panel after a new race or sex; rows that took clicks only at their left.
- **The Trustee stuck at "I have to get back to work"** after a thief dropped his key.
- **Item boxes:** a missile weapon's damage line, a helm's AC BONUS, doors showing charges, and a polearm's line running out of the box.
- **The Cloak of Protection forbidden to druids,** and the dice log stopping in a fight it couldn't match.

**Documentation**
- The kits as a table per class, the player's guide renamed `PLAYER-GUIDE.md`, and every page reviewed for the rename, clarity and repetition.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
