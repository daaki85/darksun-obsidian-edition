Obsidian Edition 2.19.1: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). One fix after 2.19.0: F10 no longer gives a held character its walk back.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.19.1.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: copy `settings.json`, `Named saves`, and `Journal` from your old Loader folder if present. Your saves and characters stay in the game folder or its `cloud_saves` folder. Open a known save and check its journal in the new Loader before deleting the old folder.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.19.0

**Fixed**
- **F10 and a held character.** F10, the game's key that takes a move back in a fight, gives a character its walk back after the last step (new in 2.19.0). A character Paralyzed or Stuck after that last step got the walk back too, and could take a step before the game ended its turn. Now a held or stuck character is put back on its square as the game does, but can't walk.

F10 after a spell, one of 2.19.0's "not seen" items, was played too: casting ends the walk, and F10 gives no walk back, as it should.

What 2.19.0 brought is in [its notes](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.19.1/release-notes/v2.19.0.md); smaller changes are in the [changelog](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.19.1/CHANGELOG.md).

## Your saved games

Saves and settings from 2.19.0 carry over as they are. Coming from 2.18.0 or earlier, read [2.19.0's notes](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.19.1/release-notes/v2.19.0.md#your-saved-games) first: several rules became always on there.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Optional: Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
