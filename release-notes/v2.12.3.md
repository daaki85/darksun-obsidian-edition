Obsidian Edition 2.12.3: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). A fix release: Haste and Slow show in the attacks a round, View Character's damage matches the fight's, the arena's Ring +1 shimmers as soon as it is found, and fixes from a code audit of the Loader.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.12.3.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.12.2

**Fixed**
- **Haste and Slow show in the attacks a round.** In a fight Haste doubles a character's attacks and Slow halves them, but the inventory, View Character and the Loader's Characters tab showed them as if neither. Now they show `3x1D8+13` for a hasted fighter, and `0.5x1D8+6` for a slowed one.
- **View Character's damage matches the fight's.** A plain weapon of wood, bone, stone or obsidian does less damage (2 less for wood, 1 for the others), which the fight and the inventory counted and View Character didn't. The dice log now names it on each hit (`-1 bone`).
- **K'ratchek's second chatkcha throw a round** shows on the Characters tab with weapon specialization on.
- **The arena's Ring +1 shimmers as soon as it is found.** An inventory opened just after finding it showed a plain ring until it was clicked. The same holds for every item the Loader makes during play.
- **The Loader keeps going through errors.** Play Dark Sun (in-game rolls).bat no longer stops its dice log, combat log and item checks after an unexpected error, and still writes a crash report when DOSBox closes.
- **Your options are never lost to a locked file.** A settings file held for a moment by an antivirus or a backup is read again rather than taken as empty and saved over.
- **The dice log** never mixes a stale die into a roll after rolls came too fast to record, and keeps a pass's rolls when something after them fails.
- **The dialogue pane** stays at 3,000 lines, as the dice log does, so a long session doesn't use more and more memory.
- **The arena's Ring +1** never uses up an item record when no one has room for it.

**Documentation**
- The player's guide explains weaker materials' penalties (to hit and to damage); the features page says Haste and Slow change the attacks a round; KITS.md says a thri-kreen Mind Warrior gets the chatkcha if nothing is picked on the weapon window.
- The Loader screenshot shows K'ratchek's attacks as they are now.

## Your saved games

Everything here works in older saves, from the moment a save is loaded.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
