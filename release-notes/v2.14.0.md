Obsidian Edition 2.14.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Hidden thieves and invisible figures are drawn see-through, a champions' bout follows Scar's death in the arena, Pehtucl's chest is locked and trapped, the game's people wear their gear from the start, and Kalzith has a face of his own.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.14.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.13.4

**New**
- **See-through figures:** a thief or ranger hidden in a fight, and anyone invisible, is drawn half see-through, blended with what is under it. (Options: on.)
- **The champions' bout:** once a party member has killed Scar in the arena, the next visit begins with six champions from a distant arena, after a speech of the Announcer's: a Tohr-kreen with the new **Kreenfang**, a half-giant, a mul, a halfling, an elf and a defiler, each armed and armoured.
- **Pehtucl's chest, locked and trapped:** the slave pens' head templar's chest holds the Cloak of Protection +1. A thief checks it for traps and picks the lock; anyone else may bash it, which springs the poisoned needle if it is still there. A new game only.
- **Sounds for hiding and picking pockets:** a soft breath as a thief or ranger hides in a fight, and a purse's jingle for a picked pocket. A Spellarcher making spell arrows hears the spell's own cast sound.
- **Kalzith's own portrait,** painted in the game's portrait style, in place of the one he shared.

**Changed**
- **Thornwall comes onto the slave pens' weapon rack once Semyon has told of it,** in a new question ("Seen anything useful?"). It had been on the rack from the start.
- **The game's people wear the cloaks, belts, boots, amulets and rings they carry,** which the game never put on; each counts by the same rules as the party's.
- Cilla's own feature is the **Elven Shadow-walker** (her hiding never halved in daylight). On the Loader's Characters tab the game's own party's features show as `Features:`, and K'ratchek's as "One more chatkcha throw".
- The Loader's window: **All fields** widens its columns to fit, the byte view scrolls sideways, and the window keeps a size at which every tab and button shows.

**Fixed: the game's own bugs**
- **The game's people put on their weapons and armour only at their first action in a fight.** Until then each was AC 10 and fought with nothing, so a first round's blow, a backstab or an opening shot met no armour. They wear it from the moment they are on the map now.
- **Psionic Blast** could kill a party member while their imagined wounds were still to come back. It knocks them out now, and they wake at the fight's end. **Scare** gave an elf or half-elf priest below 6th level no save.
- **The game's scripts:** Kurzak spoke of Legcrusher as gone while he lived.

**Fixed: the Loader's own**
- **Items could be lost:** one the Loader moved when the game had no free lists left, or a sold magic item while the game was busy with its item lists. The Loader now finds the new list first, makes its changes only while the game's lists hold still, and leaves the game a reserve of free lists.
- **One failing step stopped the rest of the dice log's work,** every pass, and one refused write ended it for the session. Each step now runs on its own.
- **A weapon's 200 XP for a picked pocket could be had again,** and a pocket picked or a speaker's name learned could be forgotten.
- **The helper** (DSCLOG): a floating name could be drawn above the screen; the floating names and the shadows could leave the graphics card's settings wrong for each other (a likely cause of rare drawing errors); and with no memory for its buffers it could exit with its interrupts still pointing at it. All fixed.
- Many smaller fixes, in the [changelog](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.14.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Your saved games

Saves from 2.13.4 load as they are. A few things follow an older save: in one that has already been to the arena, Kreenfang is likely still on the dead body by the stone arch as well, and Thornwall is already on the rack; in one that has already been to the slave pens, Pehtucl's chest is the plain one and he still wears the cloak. A save made with an earlier version while Kalzith was switched off may start Kalzith's conversation when you talk to Semyon. Saves made with 2.14.0 are not meant for earlier versions: the new chest and the Loader's conversations use scripts an older Loader doesn't have.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Optional: Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
