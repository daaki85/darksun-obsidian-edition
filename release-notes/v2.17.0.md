Obsidian Edition 2.17.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). The player's manual as linked web pages in the download, a fairer rule for a thief caught at a pocket, and fixes to Kurzak's door and a ranger's weapon window.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.17.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: copy `settings.json` (your options) and the `Named saves` folder (your saves' named copies, and any `Replaced -` copies a restore kept) from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, or its `cloud_saves` folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.16.1

**The manual**
- The zip's **`manual`** folder holds the player's pages as web pages that link to each other: the start page, the player's guide, features and rules, kits, items, creatures, the game's bugs fixed, the dice log line by line, how the original game works, and the changes from the original game. Each has a bar of all the pages and an index of its sections; the pages with spoilers are marked, and keep their warnings. The Loader's **Help** button opens the start page.
- The patch notes' web page, `patch-notes.html`, is in the zip too (2.16.1's release didn't have it).

**Rules**
- **A thief caught at a pocket** (with **Thieves as in AD&D**): only a thief who is caught, failing both the pick and the move silently, must gain a level before trying that person again. A thief who slips away unnoticed may try again at once. The game no longer tells you a thief "needs another level"; the person just keeps a closer hand on their purse.

**The story**
- **Kalzith's past** fits Athas: no sorcerer's apprentice where wizardry is a crime, but a merchant house's slave scribe in Draj, taught in secret by a defiler among its caravan hands, and caught by the templars drawing life from a noble's garden.

**Fixed**
- **Kurzak's door** could stand open but still bar the way: the party couldn't step into the doorway, and Legcrusher, sent to fetch them, stood still for good. The doorway now opens as the door shows it, in a save already caught that way too.
- **A ranger** (or a Battle Mage, Crusader or Mind Warrior) was asked at creation to "PICK A WEAPON SPECIALTY" though it takes expertise: the window now asks for a weapon of expertise.

Smaller fixes are in the [changelog](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.17.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Your saved games

Saves and settings from 2.16.1 carry over as they are. A thief barred from a pocket by 2.16's rule (after slipping away) may try again.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Optional: Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
