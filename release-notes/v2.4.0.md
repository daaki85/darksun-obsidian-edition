Obsidian Edition 2.4.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Thirteen multiclass kits, features of their own for the game's own party, thieves in light armour, the combat log shown with **L** and fixed (2.3.0's could make fights quit or freeze), a steady view in fights, and crash and freeze reports that say why. It is pull requests #48 and #49.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.4.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.3.0

**New**
- **Multiclass kits** (with kits on): thirteen kits for two classes taken at once, each for one race only, chosen on the creation panel's KIT page as a class's kit is: the **Battle Dancer** (elf cleric/fighter), **Shadowblade** and **Military Scout** (half-elf fighter/thief), **Wilderness Avenger** (half-elf druid/fighter), **Rescuer** (half-elf cleric/ranger), **Ghetto Fighter** and **Wayfinder** (dwarf fighter/thief), **Spellarcher** (half-elf fighter/preserver), **Titan Mind** (half-giant fighter/psionicist), **Poisoner** (halfling fighter/thief), **Spirit Caller** (halfling cleric/psionicist), **Wasteland Seer** (thri-kreen druid/fighter) and **Wild Fury** (mul druid/fighter). Each gives something and costs something; the player's guide has them all.
- **The game's own party gets features of its own** (with kits on): Gerakis fights wild as a Wild Fury does, K'ratchek casts as a Wasteland Seer and throws her chatkcha twice a round, Cilla is an Elven Wanderer whose hiding isn't halved in daylight, and Cermak is an Arcanist. No character you make can have the first three.
- **The combat log below the map** (off by default): the game's screen 22 rows taller, with a strip under the map that always shows the round and the newest line.
- **Rolls out of fights in the combat log:** a thief's skills, a scroll learned, a new level's hit points.
- **Crash and freeze reports that say why:** when the game quits on its own error, the report keeps the message the game printed (it used to be cleared from the screen). When it freezes, the Loader's **Freeze report** button saves where it was running.
- **Monsters' unnamed powers** in the dice log show their damage dice and kinds ("special attack 195 (1d10 crushing)").

**Changed**
- **The combat log's panel is shown and hidden with L**, in a fight or out of one, and stays as you left it. It no longer opens in each fight by itself.
- **A steady view in fights** (on by default): the view moves only to bring back a figure that has walked off the screen.
- **Thieves wear light armour only** (with class restrictions). A multiclass thief, or a human thief who was a warrior or cleric, may wear heavier armour, but has no thief skills or backstab in it. The Assassin, Shinobi, Shadowblade, Poisoner and Spellarcher wear light armour only.
- **Fuller starting gear:** no belt, boots or backpack dagger for everyone, a cloak only for a Stalker, Assassin or Shinobi, and some kits their own weapons (a Battle Dancer two long swords, a Poisoner two daggers). The game's own party gets it too. A multiclass preserver starts with no armour, so it can cast.
- **The inventory screen's HIDE and MOVE count a worn cloak and boots**, as the rolls already did.
- **Thieves' Tools are Mirlon's** (in the slave pens) and Kel's to sell; no thief starts with a set.
- **The leather helm keeps its own name**, and a ranger's bow is listed on the Effects screen.

**Fixed**
- **Fights quitting with "Return code 01h", or freezing, with the combat log on** (2.3.0).
- **The combat log's panel** slowing the game down, leaving copies or patches of map behind, hiding the pointer, and showing on the USE screen.
- **The backpack's dagger fought with** (2.3.0): Gerakis attacked with it in place of his long sword. Games where it happened are put right the next time they're played.
- **A multiclass preserver could cast in armour.**
- **A new character's bow listed as "Arrows 1D6"**, yellow text on the creation screen, and a weapon spec carried over from the character made before.
- **A new thri-kreen given gear it can't use**, and a Battle Dancer whose cleric sphere bars long swords unable to specialize in one.
- **The dice log's damage for STR 18** was 2 short.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
