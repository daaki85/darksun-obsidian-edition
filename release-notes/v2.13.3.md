Obsidian Edition 2.13.3: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). The creation screen shows a new character's THAC0, the Wasteland Seer and the Shinobi grow, a Spellarcher can top up its arrows, and EDIT keeps a party member as it was.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.13.3.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.13.2

**New**
- **THAC0 on the creation screen.** Beside the hit points, `THAC0: M18 R19`: M for melee, with STR's to-hit counted, and R for missiles, with DEX's. It changes as you change the scores, and counts a kit chosen on the kit panel (a Scholar's, a Swashbuckler's, a Battle Mage's...).

**Changed**
- **The Wasteland Seer is a thri-kreen druid/fighter/psionicist**, all three classes (a druid/fighter has no kit now). Its psionic powers follow the sky as its spells do: 2 PSP less under the open sky, 1 more under a roof, never below 1. K'ratchek is a full Wasteland Seer through her own feature.
- **The Shinobi casts to 13th level:** its slots go on to 3 3 2 1 at thief level 13, and Improved Invisibility, Solid Fog and Confusion join its list as 4th-level spells, offered from 13th.
- **A Spellarcher adds to the same spell's arrows** in its quiver, rather than being told the quiver must be empty. Other arrows still stop it.
- **An Elementalist has at least 1 slot** at each spell level a cleric of its level can cast (1st-level spells from 1st), its slots otherwise a level behind as before. Its 6th-level slot comes only with AD&D's tables, as a cleric's does.

**Fixed**
- **EDIT on a party member** (to change a name, say) took its kit away, rolled a new age and asked for its weapon specs again. It now keeps the kit, the age (until you roll the die) and, while the classes and kit stay the same, the weapon specs.
- K'ratchek's line on the Effects screen now says her open-sky feature covers psionics too.

**Docs**
- The player's guide, the rule list and the Loader's Options tab are reorganised: the rule changes come in three groups (Characters, Weapons and armour, Magic), Experience and levels follows the classes and kits, and the kits' spell slot table goes on to the XP cap's highest levels with a druid column.

## Your saved games

Saves from 2.13.2 load as they are. A thri-kreen druid/fighter Wasteland Seer from an earlier version has no kit in 2.13.3, as the kit now needs psionicist too. A three-class Wasteland Seer made in 2.13.3 has no kit in an older version.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
