Obsidian Edition 2.0.0, the fourth release, and the first under the mod's new name (it was Templar's Ledger up to 1.2.0): a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox), with its companion window, Templar's Ledger. It is pull requests #31 and #32.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Obsidian-Edition-2.0.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating from 1.2.0: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from the old `Templars-Ledger-1.2.0` folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`** (it was `Start Templar's Ledger.bat`). The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Ledger's **Options** tab, pick the rule changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Ledger follows it.

**Start a new game** to get the most from this release: kits are chosen when a character is made, and Kalzith's gear and the new robes are written into the game's own data, so they appear in places the party hasn't visited yet.

The game's own files are never changed. The Ledger runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder. Saves on pages 2 to 4 are files of their own in the game folder, beside the game's (`SAVB`, `SAVC` and `SAVD`), and characters 20 to 29 are in the game's own `CHARSAVE.GFF`.

## What's new since 1.2.0

- **Kits:** three for each class, chosen on the creation screen's new KIT page, each giving something and costing something (the Myrmidon's second weapon spec, the Arcanist's two spells a turn, the Shinobi's spells, the Elementalist's second sphere and 20 more). A human who changes class can take a kit for the new one too, up to three; a kit sleeps with its class. The Effects screen and the Ledger name them, and the dice log shows what each does.
- **New rule changes**, each switchable and on by default:
  - **AD&D's class tables:** the XP for each level, priests' THAC0, and clerics', druids' and preservers' spell slots;
  - **preservers' INT:** the chance to learn a spell from a scroll, and the most spells of each level;
  - **the leader's CHA at shops:** 5% to 25% off, as in Baldur's Gate;
  - **rangers' casting level:** a ranger's spells last and do damage as cast at its level less 7.
- **Two weapons:** the off hand attacks once a round; extra attacks are the main hand's.
- **Two magic robes** for preservers, psionicists and druids, worn on the chest and not armour: the Ashen Robe and the Veiled Robe. Kalzith now wears his robe, cloak and staff, and sells them with his scrolls.
- **New preservers choose their starting spells**, and so does a human who becomes a preserver.
- **Item boxes** show a wand's or other charged item's charges left.
- **The player's guide** (`darksun-companion/GUIDE.md`), new and spoiler-free: the rules and tables, a section for each class, and a short description of each kit.
- **The helper loads into upper memory again**, leaving the game 62 KB more conventional memory.
- **Fixed:** a kit or a second sphere could change a sound; a crash in the arena as a creature died (hardened); and more, listed in the changelog.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
