Obsidian Edition 2.9.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Spells that last a number of uses, such as Armor and Mirror Image, now show what's left in the log, over the figures and on the Effects screen; M opens the overhead map; and a Shinobi can learn from scrolls. It is pull requests #62 and #63.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.9.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.8.0

**New**
- **Charges you can see.** Armor, Mirror Image, Reflected Image and the other spells that last a number of uses:
  - the dice log says how many are left each time one is used (`Improved AC on Slig: one charge used, 7 left`);
  - the use floats over the creature in light blue ("Armor: 7 left", "Reflected Image: 2 left", "Armor ends"), for three seconds, and a "Miss" or a spell's name never pushes it out;
  - on the Effects screen, the pointer on the spell's icon shows them: IMPROVED AC (4 CHARGES).
- **Up to four names over figures at once** (were two).
- **M opens the overhead map**, and M again closes it.
- **A Shinobi learns spells from scrolls**, if it can read them: its read languages roll comes first, and its Effects screen shows the chance under its kit (`READ LANG. 35%`).
- **A scroll's answer on the inventory screen**: YOU LEARN THE SPELL, YOU CAN NOT READ THE SCROLL, THAT IS NOT A SHINOBI SPELL... on the line under the paper doll. The game wrote these there, but they never reached the screen.
- **Scar's henchmen carry bone axes**, which they drop when they fall.
- **The Parting Staff shines** on the map as the game's other magic weapons do.

**Changed**
- **A Battle Dancer can't use a shield**, as it fights with long swords and bows only.
- **A backstab's line in the dice log shows the sum** of the multiplied dice: `+2 backstab (the dice 1 x3 = 3)`.
- **More room for the mod's helper inside the game**: its big buffers moved out of the game's upper memory, leaving about 58 KB free there (from half a KB).

**Fixed**
- A Shinobi couldn't learn from a scroll at all: clicking the spell in the scroll's box did nothing.
- A Spellarcher's spell arrows cast at 6th level whatever the archer's level (a 2nd-level archer's Shocking Grasp did 1d8+6). They now cast at the archer's preserver level; a magic weapon's own spell is still 6th, as the game has it.
- A backstab could multiply the round's second attack too (an off-hand one after a miss). Only the first is a backstab now.
- The dice log could miss a hit after one the creature was immune to (a slime struck with a club). Each hit is now matched to the hit points lost, and the one that did nothing says why.
- Bracers of Defense +4's name ran past the item box.
- A preserver's scroll line in the dice log could show a Shinobi's read languages roll.
- Using the cooked vulture on a party member said it was tough and bland.

## Your saved games

Every change works from the moment a save is loaded, on spells already running too. Scar's henchmen's axes are new items: like the mod's other new items, they come from the next start of the game, and a saved game keeps what it already has.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
