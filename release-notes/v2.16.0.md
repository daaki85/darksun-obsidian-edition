Obsidian Edition 2.16.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). Loot a body and sell your spare gear with one key each, thieves who may try a failed lock or pocket again after a level, new weapon traits (polearm reach, staff sling stinkpots), the footman's pick, and a fight with Legcrusher at the fountain.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.16.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: copy `settings.json` (your options) and the `Named saves` folder (your saves' named copies, and any `Replaced -` copies a restore kept) from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, or its `cloud_saves` folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.15.0

**Quicker looting and selling**
- **Loot a body in one go:** with a body's (or a chest's) window open, **T** takes everything into the party's packs, and **Shift+T** everything but plain weapons.
- **Sell your spare gear in one go:** on a shop's screen, **S** sells the plain weapons, armour, shields and clothing in the backpack of the member on show. It keeps what is worn or held, magic items, ammunition and anything metal (rare and dear on Athas).

**Rules**
- **A thief who fails a lock or a pocket** may try that one again only after gaining a level, as in *Player's Option: Skills & Powers* (with **Thieves as in AD&D**).
- **Weapon traits** (the switch that brought the cahulaks' two heads): a **polearm** wielded alone strikes at two squares, and the **staff sling** throws stinkpots (1d3, and a hit may sicken the target, -2 to hit for a few rounds).
- **The pick** is the footman's pick (1d6+1, 2d4 against large targets), and **the axe** does 1d10 against large targets.
- **Grand mastery** steps a weapon's die to the next real size (a d12 to a d20) against every target.
- Six switches merged into three: **Saving throws against spells** (with the old "no doubled save"), **Shops as in AD&D** (with buying back magic items), **Thieves as in AD&D** (with the backstab's dice). If you had both halves of a pair on (the default) or both off, nothing changes; if you had one on and one off, the merged switch starts **off**, so tick it again on the Options tab if you want it.

**The story**
- **Legcrusher at the fountain:** sent to fetch a party that is out by the fountain, he sees they've left their cells, bellows for the guards (the fountain drowns him out) and attacks alone.

**Fixed**
- **The sewer grate's hack** wants a melee weapon in either hand (not a bow or a shield), and names the weapon in hand: the game named the next item it carried.
- **Semyon's shipment** reaches a weapon rack the party had emptied.
- **Every thri-kreen** leaves a thri-kreen's body, not a human's.
- **Pehtucl's chest** no longer speaks with the face of whoever talked last.
- **The pointer** no longer leaves images of itself on the map in a fight.
- **The Spellarcher's arrows** play their spell's sound as they are made.
- **Stealth:** a thief beside any square of a big creature counts as beside an enemy, so can't hide there.
- The hide and pickpocket sounds are louder, and the combat log panel's font is a pixel bigger.
- Smaller fixes are in the [changelog](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.16.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Your saved games

Saves from 2.15.0 load as they are, and a 2.15.0 `settings.json` keeps its choices, except a merged switch whose two halves differed: it starts off (see above).

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Optional: Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
