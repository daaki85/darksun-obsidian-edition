Obsidian Edition 2.2.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox), with its companion window, Templar's Ledger. Two new wizard spells, half-elves and elves with a look of their own, STORE and RECALL on the creation screen, and an Options tab in clearer groups. It is pull requests #39 to #42.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.2.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Ledger's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Ledger follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Ledger runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.1.1

**New**
- **Two new wizard spells, Erdlu Claw and Fogbolt,** beside Cat's Grace, which is now a spell of its own (it no longer takes Flaming Sphere's place). Erdlu Claw (1st level) turns unarmed attacks into talons: 1d6+1, +1 to hit, as a +1 magical weapon. Fogbolt (1st level) is a dark grey bolt that gives a creature −3 to its attacks and +2 to attacks on it for 2d4 rounds. Each has its own icon, description and sounds.
- **Half-elves have their own figures** on the map and in fights, instead of the humans'. The man is built like the elf, a little broader, with short fair hair, a leather jerkin and green trousers; the woman is a little slimmer than the human woman, with her golden hair in a bun, a purple cloak and red boots. What they wear is drawn on them.
- **Elves have pointed ears,** walking and fighting.
- **STORE and RECALL on the creation screen,** as in Baldur's Gate: keep a roll, try the die again, and bring the kept roll back.

**Changed**
- **The Options tab in four groups:** Rule changes, More character customization (weapon specialization, kits, levels up to 10), Expanded features (the leader's CHA at shops, and Improved thieving) and New content (the new spells, people, a quest and items). Your settings carry over.
- **Multiclass hit points follow Demihuman Deities:** CON's bonus on every hit die, the total divided between the classes. A fighter/preserver/thief with CON 18 gets far more hit points from CON than before.
- **The level-up weapon window shows each weapon type's picture,** and names it when pointed at.
- **View Character labels each save** and shows MOVE.
- **Rangers and Twin-blades fight with two weapons at no penalty only in light armour.**
- **Cermak is an Arcanist** in the game's own party (with kits).

**Removed**
- **Boots no longer give a move in a fight** (a leftover from testing); boots are plain Boots again.
- **P in a conversation** for picking pockets: use Thieves' Tools.

**Fixed**
- **Cilla, an elf, was drawn with the half-elf woman's figure;** she looks like an elf now.
- **EXIT on CHOOSE A SPELL did nothing** when a new preserver picked its starting spells.
- **The game's own party had to pick spells;** Cermak and Cilla keep the game's own again.
- **The dice log showed XP for kills that give none,** and some save lines read as if the save had succeeded.

**Documentation**
- The README follows the Options tab's groups, with a Graphics section. The patch notes have a table of contents and list every new item. Semyon's story is told as the game tells it.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
