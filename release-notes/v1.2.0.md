Templar's Ledger 1.2.0, the third release: a companion and mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). It is pull requests #19 to #29.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Templars-Ledger-1.2.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating from 1.1.0: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from the old `Templars-Ledger-1.1.0` folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Templar's Ledger.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Ledger's **Options** tab, pick the rule changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Ledger follows it.

**Start a new game** to get the most from this release: the new items are written into the game's own data, so they appear in places the party hasn't visited yet, and weapon specialization is chosen when a character is made.

The game's own files are never changed. The Ledger runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder. Saves on pages 2 to 4 are files of their own in the game folder, beside the game's (`SAVB`, `SAVC` and `SAVD`), and characters 20 to 29 are in the game's own `CHARSAVE.GFF`.

## What's new since 1.1.0

- **New rule changes**, each switchable and on by default:
  - **weapon specialization:** fighters and gladiators specialize in weapon types (+1 to hit, +2 damage), fighters on to mastery and grand mastery; rangers have expertise, and every ranger has it with the bow. Chosen on the creation screen's new WEAPON SPEC pages, and at a level gained in the game's own pop-up. Specialists shoot missiles faster, and every warrior's other missile weapons speed up from 7th level, as melee does;
  - **class restrictions:** each class's limits on armour, shields and weapons hold, the strictest winning (a cleric's sphere, a psionicist's light armour), and a multiclass preserver casts no spells in armour;
  - **multiclass hit points** as in AD&D, and **hit dice rolled twice**, the better kept.
- **New items across Athas**, each with an icon of its own and a story from Alagorn:
  - plain bone, obsidian and metal weapons the game lacks (short swords, axes, great axes, daggers, maces, picks, polearms), sold by the Weapon Merchant and Jark and carried by the people of the world;
  - magic weapons, among them Galefang, Glasshewer, Headsman, Linebreaker, Mindshard, Stillwater, Thornwall, Drakejaw, Gutterknot, Deepbiter, Windlash, Greenbright and the Flame Blade;
  - the Warden's Plate, the Cloak and Boots of Elvenkind, Bracers of Defense, Arrowbane, the Sunking Crown and the Tome of Understanding.
- **Thieves:** a thief can lift Shadowseeker, Gutterknot and Mindshard (200 XP each); Kel sells Thieves' Tools; defilers leave a body to search.
- **The game's own party** (START GAME) is made ready for the rule changes.
- **View Character** names the class that levels up next (`EXP:87230 (90000 Pr)`).
- **No manual check:** no dragon asking for a word from the manual, the game's copy protection.
- **The Ledger:** the Options tab is regrouped (Rule changes, Thieves, New content, On the screen, Controls, Game speed), with one box for all the new items; the dice log's own switches are above the log.
- **The guide** is reorganised, with tables of every magic item and every mundane weapon, and where to find them.
- **Fixed:** the game's Sling +1 had no icon; Alagorn's empty clothes menu; and more, listed in the changelog.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
