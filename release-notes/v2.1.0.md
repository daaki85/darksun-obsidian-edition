Obsidian Edition 2.1.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox), with its companion window, Templar's Ledger. It fixes a freeze on the creation screen in 2.0.0, and character creation now keeps what the die rolls. It is pull request #35.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Obsidian-Edition-2.1.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from the old `Obsidian-Edition-2.0.0` folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Ledger's **Options** tab, pick the rule changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Ledger follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Ledger runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.0.0

- **Fixed: clicking a score on the creation screen froze the game** (in 2.0.0, with the Ledger running).
- **Scores and hit points as rolled** (a new rule, on by default, with its own box on the Options tab):
  - a right click lowers a score, and the points it frees raise another with a left click, but the six never add up to more than the die gave;
  - under CHR the screen shows what they add up to and the most they may: `SUM:99/101` has 2 points to spare;
  - a new class that raises a score to its minimum raises the most with it;
  - clicking the hit points does nothing.

  The dice log adds each roll's total. Untick the rule for the game's own creation screen, where a click raises any score to its most.
- **Turning the mouse wheel no longer scrolls the map**; pressing the wheel and dragging still does.
- **Patch notes** (`PATCH-NOTES.md`): every change from the original game in one page, to share.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
