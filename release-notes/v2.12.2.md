Obsidian Edition 2.12.2: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). A fix release: the combat log in the game no longer flickers in a fight or slows it down, a Poisoner starts with two blades of its weapon spec, a thri-kreen Mind Warrior may take the chatkcha, and the spells' weapons glow on the party's figures.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.12.2.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.12.1

**Fixed**
- **The combat log in the game no longer flickers in a fight.** Each time the game showed an attack or a spell, the panel vanished for a moment, for up to half a second in a monster's turn. It now stays up, and is only hidden under the game's own windows.
- **A fight runs as fast with the combat log up as without it.** A monster's turn took about a sixth longer with the panel showing. The difference is now within how much turns vary anyway.
- **A Poisoner who picks the short sword starts with a short sword in each hand,** not two daggers. With the dagger, or no pick, it is still two daggers. A Poisoner made with 2.12.1 and not yet played has both daggers made short swords when the game is loaded.
- **Shillelagh and Spiritual Hammer glow on the party's figures,** as the Flame Blade does: Shillelagh in the fire colours, Spiritual Hammer's head in blue.

**Changed**
- **A thri-kreen Mind Warrior may take the chatkcha as its weapon spec,** the thri-kreen's own weapon, as well as the melee weapons its class allows. Its expertise gives 3/2 throws a round, 2 from 7th level in a fighter, gladiator or ranger class. Crusaders and other Mind Warriors still choose a melee weapon.

**Documentation**
- Three animated pictures recorded in the game: the combat log filling in as an arena fight is played, Whirlwind's funnel turning over the sligs, and Gerakis in magic gear.
- The kits page, player's guide, features page and patch notes cover the Poisoner's blades, the thri-kreen Mind Warrior's chatkcha and the spell weapons' glow.

## Your saved games

Everything here works in older saves, from the moment a save is loaded.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
