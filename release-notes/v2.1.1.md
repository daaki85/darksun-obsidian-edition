Obsidian Edition 2.1.1: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox), with its companion window, Templar's Ledger. Two fixes to character creation. It is pull request #37.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.1.1.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old `Obsidian-Edition-...` folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Ledger's **Options** tab, pick the rule changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Ledger follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Ledger runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.1.0

- **Fixed: the dice log missed creation rolls.** The die rolls a whole character many times while it tumbles, faster than the log could read, so abilities often said "its rolls came too fast to record". Every ability of the character the die stops on is now logged in full.
- **Fixed: raising CON on the creation screen didn't raise the hit points.** With **Scores and hit points as rolled** (on by default), a change of CON now adds or takes off its bonus for every level: a warrior's full bonus, at most +2 for others.
- Releases are now titled **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands - Obsidian Edition**.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
