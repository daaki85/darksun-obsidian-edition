Obsidian Edition 2.5.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). A Pick Pockets button for thieves, four more magic weapons, thri-kreen claws that cut, six of the game's own spell bugs fixed, an arena that always starts, and the player's guide and patch notes as web pages. It is pull request #51.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.5.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.4.0

**New**
- **A Pick Pockets button for thieves** (with Picking pockets on): on the USE screen, where a priest's Turn Undead is, a character with thief levels has a purse. Press it and click anyone in sight: that thief tries their pocket, party leader or not, and what's lifted goes in that thief's backpack. Not in a fight. A cleric/thief has both buttons. It replaces Thieves' Tools, which no one carries now.
- **Four more magic weapons** (with New items on), for the kits that had too few: **Hayneedle**, a bone dagger +1 a water cleric can wield, early on; **Nightsliver**, an obsidian dagger +2 a fire or earth cleric can wield; **Dunesong**, a bow +1 sold in a shop; and **Skycutter**, a chatkcha +2. The dagger fighters (Poisoner, Ghetto Fighter), the Spellarcher and the thri-kreen now have more to find. Each has its own icon and a story from Alagorn, the game's item sage.

**Changed**
- **Thri-kreen claws cut**: a thri-kreen in the party fights with edged claws, so creatures that crushing weapons can't hurt are no longer safe from it. Erdlu Claw's talons are edged too.
- **A Shinobi reads a scroll before learning from it** (with kits on): a roll against its read languages, then INT's chance. The dice log shows both.
- **A picked purse holds more as the thief grows**: 2 to 5 ceramic pieces for each thief level.

**Fixed**
- **A Battle Dancer's dance and Wild Fighting** weren't rolled in a fight that came soon after another (the arena's next wave) or after loading a save, and kept the last fight's result. Now both end with the fight, whatever came of them, and every fight rolls them anew.
- **Mirror Image** stopped no weapon blow and swallowed the caster's own spells (Armor cast under it was lost three times in four). Each hit, and each foe's spell at one creature, now has a 75% chance to strike an image (50% for Reflected Image); your own side's spells always get through.
- **Armor** used two charges a hit, and one for any spell on its wearer, even a friend's Bless. Now one for each damaging hit.
- **Share Strength** left its strength on the one who got it after the power ended.
- **The preserver's Dispel Magic** stopped everyone it caught from casting, your own party too. Now it only dispels.
- **Hold Undead** held the living too.
- **Cause Serious and Critical Wounds** did 2d9 and 3d9. Now 2d8+1 and 3d8+3, as AD&D has them.
- **A new game's arena could be left without its announcer** (2.4.0): the opening fight never began and walking said NO PATH FROM HERE. A game this happened to can't be mended: start a new one.
- **A Brute started with no weapon**, and a Shadowblade or Ghetto Fighter could start with a shield in the backpack.
- **Figures swung empty-handed** with the mod's own weapons (the new short swords, axes, metal daggers and the rest).
- **The Trustee could stop on his rounds** and stay busy for good. He walks on now, even in a save where he's already stuck, once talked to.
- **Dinos's talk after the cooked vulture** came back with only "Goodbye."
- **Defilers' scrolls** dropped by creatures that aren't defilers (one of Scar's henchmen).
- **Turn Undead's right click** showed a new spell's description, and Pick Pockets' showed nothing.
- **White ability labels** on the inventory screen among the yellow.
- **A crash after Continue** on a monster's whole description.
- **No crash report when DOSBox closed** while the game still ran: the Loader now says the game didn't end on its own.

**Documentation**
- **The player's guide and the patch notes as web pages**, `obsidian-edition/player-guide.html` and `patch-notes.html`, the patch notes with a button that copies them as Markdown for forums.
- **Items**, a page of its own: every magic item and every weapon, with its icon and where it is (spoilers). **How the game works** (the original game's own rules) has a page of its own too.
- **The Game's bugs, fixed**: every bug of the game's own the mod mends, in one section.
- The project is free software under the **GNU General Public License v3.0**, and the front page has its credits.

## Your saved games

The fixes work from the moment you load: no new game needed. They don't undo what the old bugs already did to a save: a character who kept Share Strength's strength still has it, and a 2.4.0 game whose arena never began can't be mended. Thieves' Tools from 2.4.0 do nothing now. New items go where the party hasn't been yet; a new game has them all.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
