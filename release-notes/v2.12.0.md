Obsidian Edition 2.12.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). The whole party now walks at full speed, the whole party is in view after a load or a fight, U and I close their screens again, and some smaller fixes. It is pull requests #70 to #72.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.12.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: to keep your options, copy `settings.json` from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.11.0

**New**
- **Steady pace:** the whole party walks at full speed on the map, with shadows and dust on, at the default game speed. In the slave pens that is about 250 to 280 pixels a second, up from 150 to 175. When the screen can't keep up, a picture is skipped rather than everyone slowing down. Fights still draw every picture. It is a switch under **Game speed** on the Options tab, on by default.
- **The whole party in view:** every party member is on the map when a game starts or loads, and after each fight, as the game's **5** does. **6** still gathers them into the leader. It is a switch under **Controls**, on by default.
- **U and I close their screens again** when pressed a second time, as **M** does the overhead map. Each still switches to the other screen.

**Fixed**
- **The Effects screen** no longer shows "15 CHARGES" on a magic item's power, such as Shadowseeker's Detect Invisibility.
- **Two Whirlwinds on the map** both turn, not only the last one cast.
- **Restore defaults** on the Loader's Options tab keeps the combat log on.

**Documentation**
- A full review of every page. The player's guide is shorter: it repeats less of the features page and KITS.
- [Items](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.12.0/obsidian-edition/ITEMS.md) gains a table of every plain armour piece and shield, with its AC and who sells or carries it.

## Your saved games

Everything here works in older saves, from the moment a save is loaded.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
