Obsidian Edition 2.18.0: a mod for **Dark Sun: Shattered Lands** (the GOG release, in DOSBox). The kits rebalanced, each closer to its source book; the Spellarcher remade after *Dragon* #214, with a Fine Bow and arrows from the start; an archer's turn no longer lost to an empty quiver; CHOOSE A SPELL's icons centred.

## Getting started

1. Download **`Dark-Sun-Shattered-Lands-Obsidian-Edition-2.18.0.zip`** below and unzip it anywhere. (Updating: copy `settings.json` (your options) and the `Named saves` folder (your saves' named copies, and any `Replaced -` copies a restore kept) from your old folder into the new one. Your saves and characters are in the game folder, or its `cloud_saves` folder, so nothing else needs moving.)
2. Double-click **`Start Obsidian Edition.bat`**. The first time, it offers to install 64-bit Python if you don't have it.
3. On the Loader's **Options** tab, pick the changes and additions you want. They're remembered.
4. Press **Start the game**. The game starts with your options, and the Loader follows it.

The game's own files are never changed. The Loader runs a patched copy of the game from its own folder.

## What's new since 2.17.1

**The kits, rebalanced** (with **Kits** ticked; every kit's gains and costs are in the manual's Kits page)
- **Two weapons** is always on (AD&D's penalties, a ranger's, Twin-blade's and Battle Dancer's exception): its switch is gone.
- **One kit a character**: a human who changes class keeps its kit and takes no other; one with none may take one for its new class.
- **A warrior's THAC0 with the chosen weapon only** for the Swashbuckler, Battle Mage, Crusader and Mind Warrior, as their handbooks have it. The Swashbuckler picks its weapon of choice (long sword, dagger or short sword) after DONE, and its flat −10 to thief skills is now **bad luck**: 0 to 25 off each roll, rolled each time.
- **Arcanist**: its preserver spells cast a level higher (duration and damage), in place of two spells a turn.
- **Scholar**: a spell more learnt at each level, no slot more; still 1 worse to hit.
- **Myrmidon**: its second weapon spec is the polearm.
- **Poisoner**: each melee weapon's first hit in a fight poisons, not every hit.
- **Crusader and Mind Warrior**: their extra attacks with their chosen weapon only; the Mind Warrior has a fifth fewer PSP.
- **Justifier**: its chosen weapon specialized, the bow at a ranger's expertise.
- **Seeker and Grove Warden**: their sphere's weapons, the bow only where the sphere allows one.
- **Sentinel**: AC 2 better with a shield, −1 to hit in melee without one; no initiative bonus.
- **Wayfinder**: initiative only (+1 for the party while it stands, +3 for itself), no movement bonus and no penalty when it falls.
- **Twin-blade and Brute**: the cahulaks are the Twin-blade's, at no penalty in light armour; a Brute may not hold them, nor become an air cleric.
- **Healer and Lifebinder** never know the harming spells; the Lifebinder's Cell Adjustment heals a d4 more.
- **Wild Fury**: Wild Fighting as *The Complete Barbarian's Handbook* has it, −3 to hit and AC 3 worse whatever the roll, the extra attack and +3 damage with the roll made.
- **Assassin and Shinobi**: their light armour limit sleeps with the kit after a change of class.

**The Spellarcher**, after *Dragon* #214's "The Complete Half-Elf"
- One arrow a spell slot, woven from Chill Touch, Shocking Grasp or Vampiric Touch (Burning Hands, a cone, makes none); a stack of up to 24 of each that **lasts until shot**; only a Spellarcher carries them.
- What an arrow hits saves only as against the spell itself (Chill Touch).
- It starts with a **Fine Bow** (+1 to hit, not magic) and **24 arrows of each touch spell it picked** at creation, else 24 plain arrows; it wears no armour, helm or shield.

**Archers**
- After each party shot, **"Arrows: 17 left"** or **"Out of arrows"** floats over the archer (with Names over figures on).
- An archer whose quiver ran dry mid-volley keeps the rest of its turn.

**Fixed**
- **An archer that killed with its last arrow** lost the rest of its turn: it goes on now, as after any kill.
- **CHOOSE A SPELL** set its icons right of centre when a level offered more than 15 spells (the game's own 4th level has 16; the 1st, with the new spells, 18). Six to a row, centred, now.
- A character made with the mod and **added again from the roster** in a later session had lost the Loader's starting gear.
- A Brute could still change class to air cleric through the sphere menu.

Smaller fixes, and the documentation's two reviews, are in the [changelog](https://github.com/daaki85/darksun-obsidian-edition/blob/v2.18.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Your saved games

Saves from 2.17.1 load and play as they are; the new kit rules apply to their characters from the moment you load. A character who took a kit for each of several classes keeps them all; a Myrmidon keeps the two weapon specs it chose; a Healer or Lifebinder loses the harming spells. **A save made with 2.18.0 is for 2.18.0 and later**: a Spellarcher's Fine Bow names an item 2.17 doesn't have.

## Requirements

- Windows, with the GOG release of Dark Sun: Shattered Lands.
- 64-bit Python 3.8 or later. The `.bat` files offer to install it.
- Optional: Wake of the Ravager, which GOG sells with Shattered Lands, for its icons and pictures for the new priest spells. Without it, the Loader uses its own.
