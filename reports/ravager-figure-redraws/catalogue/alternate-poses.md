# Alternate poses and mobile counterparts

Miric’s seated still is approved. The shipped named scenery and character records in OBJEX.GFF yielded one Miric, object 50, picture 3690. Dialogue script 257 identifies him as the prisoner whose family was taken. No standing counterpart was identified. Sirah (13, picture 3461) and Abelard (520, picture 3597) likewise each have one named record. Script 303 describes finding Abelard’s cage empty after he leaves.

The audit cross-checked object names in both record formats, object-to-picture assignments, catalogue decisions and known encounter aliases. A repeated occupation such as Miner or Merchant does not establish that two actors are the same person. A script can also use an anonymous object or assign a different picture at runtime. The entries below distinguish identified counterparts from cases where the available evidence does not identify one.

## Figures without an identified mobile counterpart

The named-record check yielded a single picture for the Sultan (including his dialogue name Akhmed Windcaller), Heroga, Fletch, Utella, Pavis, Aleka, Ikibod, Sirah, Abelard, Miric and Generoe. The same check found only one picture for the Mushroom Lady, the seated Beggar and the group called Prisoners. Sicky, Wounded Citizen, Wounded Soldier and Sick Guy also lack an identified personal standing record. For these figures the Shattered Lands comparison supplies pose, scale and material references. It does not establish the patient’s or occupant’s identity.
